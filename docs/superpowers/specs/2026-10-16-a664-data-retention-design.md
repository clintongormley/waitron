# A664: keep evidence, retire expired data

**PROPOSED · OWNER/ADVISER APPROVAL PENDING · NO IMPLEMENTATION**

Dated 2026-10-16. All choices described as DRIVER PROPOSALS remain unapproved. This is a repository design for a Spanish common-territory restaurant, subject to confirming that jurisdiction. It does not approve a purge, a fiscal invariant change or the wider GDPR programme.

You need to remove personal and operational data when its purpose ends without losing evidence you still owe an inspector, worker or claimant. Choose a bounded schedule for each record class, preserve the necessary evidence in a separate inspectable archive, and destroy eligible copies when their duties and holds end. **Do not apply one number of years to the whole database.** The recommended approach starts with an inventory and eligibility report, then permits only approved mutable-data retirement. Protected historical data needs an additional archival storage design before removal is possible within the existing invariants. Leaving that work open must create a visible, time-reviewed exception, not a silent policy of keeping everything forever.

A billing record is abbreviated **RF** below; the original invoice and its RF are different artifacts. A **foreign key** is a declared database reference to another row. An **append-only** declaration protects a row from later update/deletion; a **chain head** identifies the latest record in a linked history.

The [source register](../../compliance/research/2026-10-16-a664-retention.md) defines every legal/external claim ID used here. The [table appendix](2026-10-16-a664-retention-table-inventory.md) gives every application table once, its classification, independent append-only bit, source and concrete declared dependencies. All other requirements below are **policy proposals**, unless labelled **source-reading-only**. No product test, deletion, migration, archive or restore ran for this design. **Before any A664 product code, including a read-only report or review queue, you and the relevant fiscal/commercial, labour and privacy advisers must agree this design (G0–G3).** Research and repository documentation may continue while those approvals wait.

## Choose bounded evidence archives over whole-database accumulation

| Option | What you gain | Why choose or reject it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the entire venue indefinitely | Avoid immediate deletion work | Reject. It never resolves exhausted personal-data purposes or growing copies (P1, A1). |
| Delete everything after four, six or ten years from creation | A simple sweep | Reject. Different legal triggers, live references and protected chains make row creation age an inadequate decision rule (F01–F11, L1–L9; appendix). |
| Rotate a venue folder or start a fresh chain periodically | Apparently separates old from new | Reject. Whole-folder or chain rotation is not an authorised retention mechanism. It would require separate fiscal and operational decisions. |
| Separate evidence archives, short operational windows, explicit holds and verified copy expiry | You can explain each retained field and each blocked deletion | **Recommend as DRIVER PROPOSAL.** Costs: record-level policy metadata, archive reader/key custody, dependency review and later approved storage separation for protected data. |

A recovery backup restores service after failure. A legal archive provides selected historical evidence until that evidence expires. They have different contents, lifetimes and reopening rules. A whole current database, including credentials, placed in a bucket is not the proposed archive. The archive can use the configured storage destination only after a separate namespace, access policy, location and expiry have been validated. Invoice archive access has requirements independent of encryption (F12–F16); a non-VERI export is also independent of backups (F23).

## Compute a floor and a purpose limit for each record

**Law and researched interpretation:** commercial evidence has a six-year clock from the last book entry, not invoice creation (F01/F02). Tax limitation, interruptions and special surviving evidence require separate evaluation; four-year limitation is not unconditional destruction permission, and ten-year checks are not universal retention (F03–F11). Invoice copies remain distinct from RF sent to AEAT (F12–F28). Daily time records and part-time monthly summaries have four-year rules; payroll/contribution artifacts need their own mapping, including the five-year salary-receipt provision (L1/L2/L6/L8/L9). GDPR supplies purpose limits, rather than blanket maximum years (P1, A1).

**Law/guidance, mode-qualified:** Orden Article3 excludes Articles8/9 while a system acts as VERI*FACTU (F18–F21). AEAT exempts that mode’s RF conservation/export obligation, not invoice evidence (F26–F28, F12). For non-VERI systems, Article8 instead requires correct export and oldest-first consecutive removal under its conditions, with continued conservation (F22–F25). **Researched interpretation:** neither route approves Waitron’s local chain retirement; actual mode and artifact classification remain G1 inputs.

**DRIVER PROPOSAL:** each record or indivisible evidence group has a policy ID/version, jurisdiction, purpose, basis/claim IDs, clock event and original timestamp, calculated legal end dates, operational end date, archive end date, dependencies, copy locations and any holds. Store calculations with their rule and inputs; an unexplained date is invalid. An unknown book boundary, unassessed purpose or unresolved special period produces `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not a guessed date. The fiscal adviser identifies which books and last entry actually control each evidence group; a year-end date must not be invented for an ongoing book.

An evidence group is the smallest set you must retain together to read and verify the record. Its earliest retirement is the latest of its applicable legal floors, justified purpose ends and active holds. An operational window alone cannot extend lawful processing: if there is no continuing basis, shorten it or apply the specialist-approved blocking procedure. The purpose limit triggers review and retirement; a longer legal floor keeps only the necessary evidence under limited access. Routine service use ends even when legal custody continues. Fully anonymised statistics require a documented re-identification assessment; removing a name while leaving linkable IDs is not a completed anonymisation.

**DRIVER PROPOSAL for time calculation:** use venue-local calendar dates for work days, service days, books and calendar-year periods; store the timezone and rule. Retain through the end of the relevant anniversary day, and first select on the next local day. Day windows below mean elapsed 24-hour days from a recorded terminal instant. One month means the next calendar-month anniversary, clamped to its last day where necessary. Missing terminal timestamps block eligibility until reconstructed and reviewed; do not substitute `created_at`. Exact legal trigger mapping remains G1/G2 below.

**DRIVER PROPOSAL for routine execution:** evaluate daily, in small resumable groups, with a maximum 100 mutable rows per transaction and a five-second work budget per pass. Recheck holds, state and dependency changes within the write transaction. Record an intent before mutation, then completion with counts, policy version, archive digest where applicable and copy-expiry deadline. Crash recovery reconciles intents against results before continuing. Do not put deleted contacts or health text in the audit record. An unknown table, column, module version, clock regression or changed schema blocks its group and alerts the administrator. No network/archive wait belongs inside a sale transaction. Failed retention work reports a retention problem; it must not remove sale-critical state to meet a cleanup deadline.

### A hold is a decision with an accountable reviewer

**DRIVER PROPOSAL:** a hold names the evidence IDs and copy classes, purpose, legal matter, authorising role, start, reviewer, next review date and release conditions. Tax inspection, litigation, payment dispute, labour inspection and a documented health incident are examples. Put only necessary evidence on hold. New holds reach the candidate evaluation, archive index, backup-expiry process and restore gate before any mutation. A hold races with deletion by sharing the same decision gate; archive destruction needs the same check.

Use separate states: `ACTIVE`, `LEGAL_CUSTODY`, `BLOCKED_ART32`, `HELD`, `RETIREMENT_ELIGIBLE`, `COPY_EXPIRY_PENDING`, `COMPLETE` and `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A hold has no automatic release merely because its review date passed. Review every hold at most 90 days after the previous review; overdue review alerts and freezes the affected destruction. Unresolved lawful long retention names the decision needed and reviewer. The owner coordinates; fiscal, labour and privacy specialists decide within their remit. No system checkbox can create a legal basis.

**Law:** Spanish Article32 blocking prevents ordinary viewing as well as other processing, with defined authority exceptions, and ends in destruction after the relevant limitation (B1/B2). **Proposal:** a blocked collection has a separately controlled key and no routine archive browsing; access requires the recorded qualifying authority request and an audit. It is not the same as an ordinary owner-readable archive, a GDPR restriction request (P7, A5), or a litigation hold. G3 determines which state applies and how long. Do not describe all encrypted archives as statutory blocking.

### Personal text embedded in evidence needs a separate route

**Source-reading-only:** the appendix identifies JSON, binary and candidate personal columns. It is a search aid, not a proof that every occurrence was found: for example `bookings.contact_phone` is present even though the research candidate-name filter missed it. Read all columns and the writers/readers, including `print_jobs.payload`, invoice recipient fields, adjustment notes/policy snapshots, pay-period snapshots, attendance facts and daily-close snapshots. A hash may cover a nested payload; a table without a declared key can still preserve a person ID or a copy of their words.

**DRIVER PROPOSAL:** prevent new service-only health/contact text from entering long-lived evidence unless its necessity and basis are approved. Separate short-lived notes from mandatory invoice content before a new writer is built. For existing mutable copies, inventory the precise field paths, mask them from routine views, remove them after the short purpose ends, and check all managed copies. Field removal must preserve required evidence and schema constraints; a not-null name field needs an approved non-identifying representation or whole-row deletion, not a fake claim of anonymisation.

For existing immutable or hashed payloads, **do not edit, rehash, null out, destroy the only decryption key, or conceal the unresolved source copy behind a redacted view**. Create a restricted exception entry naming the row, field paths, purpose/basis under review, specialist, copies and a review due within 30 days. Serve an approved redacted derivative for ordinary use only where the source can remain lawfully in restricted custody; label it as a derivative and preserve its link to the original digest. G3 decides retention/blocking/destruction obligations; G1/G2 decide mandatory evidence. If no lawful basis remains and the current storage cannot retire the personal payload without changing protected history, escalate the incompatibility promptly to the owner and relevant specialist. It blocks that removal path and requires a separate design/item and permission for any invariant change. Design approval here never supplies that permission. Key destruction is permissible only for a separately encrypted, independently expirable object after proving no surviving evidence shares its key and after G3 approval; it does not solve personal fields already stored inside a shared immutable snapshot.

## Preserve exact history before considering local removal

**Source-reading-only constraints:** classification and append-only are independent. `packages/store/src/append-only.ts:68` generates update/delete refusal triggers; `packages/migrations/src/apply.ts` installs declarations carried by migration options. The appendix lists protected state tables as well as protected ledgers. `cadenas` and `workforce_chains` are mutable head state, not disposable logs. `sales` points to working orders; time entries point to people, corrections and closures. Removing only old leaf rows cannot be assumed safe. These are static observations, not a run of any refusal or hash verifier.

**DRIVER PROPOSAL archive contract:** create an encrypted, independently expirable evidence package with exact original stored bytes for RF, hashed events, invoice originals and other source payloads; a manifest maps each original table/key/column, encoding, original timestamp and payload digest. Include the source schema/migration set versions, application commit/release, jurisdiction/mode, policy version, clocks and holds, item count, relationships, media closure and a package digest. Preserve original JSON/XML/binary byte spelling wherever used as evidence or hash input; pretty-printing is a separate derivative. The manifest and payload must be authenticated together.

For each fiscal, working-time, order-amendment, pay-period and daily-close chain present, carry its identity, ordering rule, original sequence positions, first predecessor identity/hash, first/last included record, original head and adjacent boundary references. Retain the payload needed to recompute each included link and the verification algorithm/version, with known-answer examples and offline instructions. A lone head hash does not replace the removed evidence. No chain gap may be described as complete; retain the whole necessary prefix/closure in accessible packages until a separately approved boundary format establishes what can expire. Invoice series and number high-water marks must survive unchanged while there is any possibility of allocation or evidence interpretation. Original identities, predecessors and numbering are never rewritten to make an archive easier.

Include an offline reader or durable open-format views, invoice presentation and structured originals, human instructions, media and dependencies, and an index that can select by invoice/period/person under appropriate permission. Keep the archive reader isolated from the trading application: inspection must not file, allocate, import or restore records. Demonstrate selection, viewing, printing and required electronic authority access for the actual invoice archive (F13–F16); offline readability supplements that access. Store two encrypted copies in independently checked destinations as a proposal, with both under the same expiry/hold register, and verify them after creation and at least annually.

Use dedicated archive keys, distinct from live provider/login credentials and from recovery-backup keys. Record key version and custodian; give the owner a sealed recovery copy and an independently controlled recovery route for death, incapacity or business closure. Test recovery of an eligible synthetic archive without the production server. Preserve public verification identities where required; do not export provider secrets, PIN/password hashes, sessions, recovery codes or active pairing tokens into evidence packages. If an original evidential artifact actually contains a secret, quarantine and classify it explicitly rather than silently rewriting bytes or distributing the secret. G3 approves custody/access, G1/G2 approve evidence sufficiency. Archive custody continues across closure under an assigned successor (F02).

**Storage boundary requiring later approval:** a selected evidence package can be copied and verified without deleting the source. It cannot currently make a protected source row removable. Ordinary purge is barred by append-only declarations and the incoming references recorded in the appendix. A later proposed archival format/storage separation must define a read-only evidence store, stable reference resolution from live rows to archived identities, authority access, chain-boundary verification, backup/restore behaviour and final lawful destruction of expired groups. This spec sets requirements for that design, not permission to bypass triggers, detach keys or rebuild a database while omitting protected records. No edits to `registerSif`, `restoreFiscal`, hash writers or allocation logic are authorised. No artificial new filing chain, trigger bypass, number reset, whole-folder rotation or chain rotation is an A664 mechanism. If a required change conflicts with these invariants, bring a separate item and explicit permission; the campaign must not infer it from accepting this approach.

### Resolve dependencies for disabled modules and live readers

**DRIVER PROPOSAL:** re-enumerate every migration set at implementation, including disabled modules and physically present historical tables. Compare live schema, manifest, descriptor classifications, migrations, triggers, declared keys, non-FK IDs, exported references, snapshots and consumers. A disabled module does not waive a hold or append-only protection. The current watcher’s module scope described in `docs/developers/conventions-data.md:1254` must not become a deletion allowlist.

For each candidate, compute incoming and outgoing dependency closure and the cascade impact before selecting an action. Never rely on cascade to choose what evidence is disposable. Include active bills/tickets, receipt/replay paths, disputes, live queries/change feed, module exports/imports, media and caches, and open labour corrections. Stop when an unknown dependency appears. Work inside the existing venue ownership/transaction arrangements; do not unlink lock files, turn off foreign keys or replace protected trigger definitions. Revalidate candidate state after any concurrent write. A retained parent can remain while eligible optional personal fields expire, only if its contract allows that reduction. Run future checks against each enabled/disabled composition, not just the currently enabled module list.

**Backup approval gate:** 35 days is a proposed recovery tradeoff, not a legal exemption or AEPD safe harbour (P5/P8, A1; research uncertainty). G3 must accept or shorten inaccessible-copy delay before enablement; G4 must verify actual backend expiry. A hold on evidence should normally extract that evidence to its segregated archive rather than freeze a credential-bearing whole backup. If only the whole backup can preserve it, register a scoped temporary exception, restrict access and decide replacement within 30 days.

**Restore/import proposal:** keep the current retention decisions and revocations in an authenticated register outside individual recovery snapshots, replicated with monotonic version/checkpoint information. Require the latest known register plus explicit verification against its independent custody copy; missing, stale or unverifiable decisions leave the restore quarantined. Before trading, filing, login, printing, mail, streaming or ordinary UI access, reconcile holds and expiries, revoke obsolete credentials, retire eligible mutable payload and invalidate caches. A protected expired payload that cannot be retired leaves that restored group blocked/restricted and requires its separate resolution; do not silently resurrect it. An old import cannot clear a newer erasure or re-enable an authenticator. Keep backup restoration’s existing fiscal identity requirements separate; retention must not trigger them merely to remove data. No claim is made that the current restore path provides this gate.

**Expired copies beyond managed storage:** maintain an export/recipient register, include an expiry and deletion instruction with each transfer, notify known recipients at expiry, request deletion/return acknowledgement and follow up within 30 days. Record unknown/unreachable copies as unresolved exceptions, with privacy specialist assessment of duties and response wording. The controller coordinates processor-contract deletion and lawful recipient handling (P8). Do not report those copies erased merely because Waitron deleted its source. You cannot promise erasure of paper receipts, downloaded files, third-party replicas or flash remnants through SQLite `DELETE`. Report managed deletion, latest possible backup expiry and unresolved external copies separately. On decommission, inventory devices, removable media, storage snapshots, sealed keys and recipients; keep the evidence custodian and recovery instructions available until their last duty ends.

## Approve the approach, then clear the specialist gates

There is **one owner question**, reproduced in the campaign’s proposed question receipt:

**Do you approve the bounded approach in this design: short expiry for routine personal data, separate inspectable evidence archives and visible holds, with protected-data removal blocked until its separate storage design and the relevant specialist gates are approved?**

Recommended answer: approve the proposed design approach, then obtain the relevant advisers’ agreement before any product code. No legal clearance or destructive action follows from that answer. Alternative: revise or defer the proposed approach and continue research/documentation only; no product reporter is authorised while approvals wait. The operational defaults above are DRIVER PROPOSALS supplied to make the choice concrete; the owner has not agreed them. Adviser answers can require changing them before their gated slice is built.

**Worked example 1, a dinner invoice and its copies.** Suppose a full invoice is issued on 15 October 2026, and the fiscal adviser identifies 31 December 2026 as the last entry in the relevant closed book. The F01 component then retains evidence through 31 December 2032; 1 January 2033 is only the earliest candidate under that component, not permission to destroy. Tax, adjustment and dispute checks can move it later (F03–F17). A tax enquiry notified on 1 June 2032 creates a scoped hold and recalculation; it does not expire on the old candidate date. A disposable print job completed on 15 October 2026 targets 15 November 2026, but a retained reprint reference blocks its row deletion. Its invoice is preserved separately; RF acceptance alone cannot replace it. In the proposed 35-day backup design, the last pre-erasure capture at 23:59 UTC on 15 November remains tracked until 20 December at 23:59 UTC. It is not available for ordinary access during that interval. These dates assume no later capture reintroduces the payload and G3/G4 approve the copy design.

**Worked example 2, a booking, a worker and an exception.** A booking finishes at 22:00 on 16 October 2026. Its identifiable allergy note targets removal by 22:00 on 17 October; ordinary service contact targets 22:00 on 15 November. A reported allergic reaction creates a specific incident hold before deletion and sends the minimum evidence to G3 review, rather than putting every diner on a permanent health register. A worker whose last authorised role ends on 31 October loses that access that day; a daily time record dated 16 October 2026 has a four-year floor, with first candidate 17 October 2030 under the proposed end-of-day convention (L1). The person link needed to read that evidence survives; their obsolete login does not. A labour claim on 1 October 2030 suspends retirement for its assessed scope. Any name embedded in an immutable closure follows the protected-payload route, and a closure classified as payroll remains blocked for G2’s distinct period decision.

### Acceptance checks for later implementation, not product proof

These checks are **unrun requirements**. Revalidate inventory at the implementation commit because other lanes can add migrations. In synthetic stores, compare every manifest/classification/table and live schema, including disabled modules and unknown-table refusal; exercise every action in both eligible and held/live-dependent states. Use distinct fixtures for expired sessions versus minimum retained actor identity, health text versus non-personal dish allergens, four-year time records versus five-year salary artifacts, and last book entry versus invoice issue. Demonstrate interruption/released-hold recalculation and local calendar boundaries. A negative control that deletes a hold/reference check must fail for that missing protection.

Prove byte-preserving archive round trips and hash verification for each chain family across proposed boundaries; prove invoice selection, readable original content, printing and electronic authority access. Test loss of a key/custodian and recovery without trading or filing. Reject incomplete dependency/media closure, changed schema, tampered manifests, stale policy and unsupported mode. Demonstrate current-head triggers and numbers remain unchanged. No acceptance check authorises disabling a trigger to get a passing purge.

Exercise interrupted intent/archive/delete steps, concurrent holds/writes, replayable commands, drawer uncertainty, active kitchen problems, invoice reprint restrictions, unexpired labour identity and cross-module references. Retained disabled printers, card readers and devices must still Enable/rejoin under their original IDs beyond the generic configuration window; retain any proof their actual reactivation path needs. Ending one employment or location role must not remove independent authorised access. Measure bounded write time and confirm sales remain served. On representative backends, retain inventory of all versions/replicas/staging, then demonstrate actual expiry; distinguish logical removal from physical-media assurance. Restore a pre-erasure backup and import an old export: erased data and revoked logins must not become ordinarily available, while lawful held evidence and existing fiscal recovery rules remain intact. Missing external retention decisions must quarantine release. Verify external-copy reports do not claim acknowledged deletion where no acknowledgement exists.

**Safe incremental slices, only after G0–G3 design agreement and their additional gates:** begin with a read-only policy/inventory report and review queue, then selected archive creation/reader and key-recovery rehearsal. After G4, enable only explicitly approved mutable personal/operational cleanup plus copy tracking and restore gates. Only after G5 may a separately reviewed protected-storage proposal lead to any further authorised build. No product slice begins with G0 alone. Each slice needs its own concise implementation plan and focused tests when authorised. This document deliberately does not schedule a full implementation before design approval. A664 remains open until the approved build and evidence checks are complete.
