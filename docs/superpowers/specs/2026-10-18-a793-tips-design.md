# A793 — Tips: who a tip belongs to, how it is shared out, and what payroll gets

Status: **design, waiting for the owner's answer to one question (Q38 in lane C's questions file,
repeated in §10 below). Nothing is built until then.** Backlog entry: `docs/backlog.md` "A793"
(under _Payments and card readers_). Pictures: `docs/backlog/mockups/a793-tips/` (drawn in HTML
with the apps' colour tokens, not screenshots). The Spanish tax and labour research behind it, with
every source quoted: [2026-10-18-a793-tips.md](../../compliance/research/2026-10-18-a793-tips.md).

File references are to `main` at `9b6fd2d81`. **(read)** marks a conclusion from reading code, not
from running it. Nothing in this design was run: it changes no behaviour, and every statement about
today's code is (read).

**Words.** A **tip** is money a guest gives on top of what the bill costs. The **pot** (in Spanish,
the _bote_) is all the tips of one business day put together to be shared out. A **share** is one
person's part of the pot. **Taking part** means a person gets a share. The **pay period** is the
workforce module's week, fortnight or month that payroll is done for. The **gestoría** is the
outside firm that runs the venue's payroll. The **business day** is the day the cash-up uses, which
can run past midnight.

## 2. What exists today

Waitron already **takes** tips and keeps them off the invoice. Nothing after the payment knows who
a tip belongs to.

- **Two ways of paying, and only one offers every tip.** (read)
  - **A bill payment** (the bill payments design of 2026-09-26, `apps/till/src/widgets/bill-pay-dialog.ts`)
    offers "Tip on the card (optional)" for a card, cash "Leave as a tip" (Nothing: give it all as
    change / All of the change / An amount), and "Pay {amount} with a {tip} tip" when the dishes
    picked cost more than the bill still owes (`:1249-1258`, `:1608-1636`; `apps/server/src/bill-allocation.ts:110-114`).
  - **Paying a whole order at once** (`apps/till/src/widgets/tender-pay.ts`, `POST /api/pay`) offers
    a tip only on a connected card reader (`:1074-1086`). Cash and a card typed into a machine
    Waitron does not talk to always record a tip of 0.00 (`apps/server/src/till-sale.ts:965`, `:2098`).
  - The counter's coming single payment window (A739, lane F) replaces the whole-order screen and
    takes the tables' bill payment path (backlog `docs/backlog/till.md` → A739). A739 does not
    say which tip choices its window offers (its one mention of a tip is the Retry state from
    A767); this design asks that its window offer the bill payment dialog's tip choices.
- **No card reader is asked to show its own tip screen.** Neither Stripe
  (`packages/payments-stripe/src/stripe-client.ts:18-33`) nor SumUp
  (`packages/payments-sumup/src/sumup-client.ts:153-166`) is sent any tipping setting. The till
  asks, and charges bill plus tip.
- **Tips are on or off for the whole box through one setting outside the dashboard**,
  `WAITRON_TILL_TIPS` (`apps/server/src/till-config.ts:85-86`). A box also reads a `trading.env`
  file from its state folder (`apps/server/src/box-env.ts:7`), but neither `deploy/` nor anything
  setup writes sets the variable, so (read) a real box takes no tips today.
- **Where a tip is stored** (all whole cents): `bill_payments.tip`, `tenders.tip_amount`, and on the
  two kinds of refund, `bill_payment_refunds.tip_amount` and `tender_refunds.tip_amount`. The sale's
  `total` holds no tip (`packages/db/src/schema/sales.ts:50-53`), and the fiscal record is built from
  that total: the fiscal backend is handed the total and VAT breakdown and no tender
  (`packages/core/src/record-sale.ts:81-83`, `:368-382`), so (read) a tip cannot reach the
  Veri\*Factu record or its hash. A test pins that the sale's stored total holds no tip
  (`packages/core/src/record-sale.test.ts:297`, with a fake fiscal backend).
- **Who a tip could be credited to.** A bill payment records who took it (`bill_payments.requested_by`);
  a whole-order payment records only the sale's operator (`sales.operator_id`). The person a dish
  "counts as" (`working_order_lines.credited_to`, `packages/db/src/schema/orders.ts:213-215`) stays
  on the order's lines, which the orders list still reads after the order is settled
  (`apps/server/src/orders-list.ts:268-273`); it is not copied to `sale_lines`. Tenders carry no
  person. (read)
- **Refunds.** A tip is given back only all at once, together with everything still unrefunded of
  its payment, and never more than was given; the rest of a payment can first be refunded in part
  without its tip, except a payment for chosen dishes, which is refunded only whole
  (`apps/server/src/bill-refunds.ts:635-648`;
  `apps/server/src/tender-refund-reservation.ts:27-89`).
- **Split bills.** Each payment keeps its own tip; one payment's tip is never used to pay the bill
  (`apps/server/src/bill-allocation.ts:155`).
- **Receipts.** The receipt and the A4 invoice print a "Propina" line under each payment that has a
  tip, below the total and outside the tax lines (`apps/server/src/receipt-document.ts:395-423`).
  The card slip prints "Importe" and "Cobrado", with "Propina" between them when there is a tip
  (`apps/server/src/payment-slip.ts:55-59`).
- **The daily close.** The cash-up has a tip column per device and method and a tip total, and
  counts the tips **inside** each device's takings and the "Tender total"
  (`packages/reporting/src/types.ts:136-155`; `packages/reporting/src/cash-up.ts:36-58`). Sales &
  takings and the Overview show it read-only. A frozen close that compares the counted cash with
  the expected cash exists only as a function (`packages/reporting/src/record-daily-close.ts:202`:
  float + cash takings − a typed `payouts` figure) that a demo script calls; no screen counts a
  drawer yet (backlog A239, "recorded cash in and out of a till's drawer"). Nothing takes tips out
  of a drawer.
- **The working-time record and pay periods** live in `packages/workforce`: clock-ins, a pay period
  (weekly, fortnightly or monthly, `pay-periods.ts`) and a function that closes one
  (`closePayPeriod`, `pay-period-close.ts:76`), freezing a snapshot that holds hours and no money.
  **Nothing in the product calls it yet**: outside tests nothing calls it; its callers are test
  files, including the fixture `apps/server/test/attendance-export-fixture.ts:77`. A closed period keeps its frozen record, and
  later corrections are shown beside it, not moved into the next period
  (`packages/workforce/src/record-projection.ts:105-135`). **There is no payroll export yet**: it
  waits for the gestoría's import layout (backlog, _Working time and staff_). The workforce module
  can be switched off (`tier: "toggleable"`, `packages/composition/src/modules.ts:169`).
- **No service charge exists**, and the architecture design forbids a compulsory one
  (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-18-pos-architecture-design.md` §10).

## 3. What Spanish law says (short version; sources in the research file)

- **VAT: a voluntary tip is outside the VAT base, cash or card, and must not be in the invoice
  total.** DGT rulings 2174-03 (restaurants), V2182-23 and V5110-26 (tips collected electronically
  and passed on). The invoicing regulation lets a simplified invoice carry other information, so
  printing a tip beside it is allowed; nothing requires it. Today's design already does this.
- **A compulsory service charge is part of the VAT base** (EU Court of Justice, C-404/99), and
  consumer law requires the full price up front. This design adds no service charge.
- **Income tax: tips are the employee's earnings.** When the employer pays tips out to staff, it
  must withhold income tax on them with the rest of their pay (ruling 1866-03, on card tips shared
  out by a hospitality business; income-tax regulation art. 76.1.3.º). Whether that also applies to cash a
  guest hands straight to a waiter is not settled by any source found.
- **Sharing.** A Madrid regional court (TSJ Madrid, 31 May 2023, read as quoted in a labour-law
  blog; the court's own copy was not found) held that card tips the business
  collects belong to the staff, the business only holds them, and without a fair agreement by the
  staff's works council they are shared **equally among everyone who works in the restaurant
  service** (in that case a hotel's restaurant department that included its kitchen). The Supreme Court (2021) held that once staff receive tips, the employer cannot
  simply take that away (read in a published copy of the judgment; see the research file). None
  of the hostelería collective agreements sampled (the national one and some provincial ones) uses
  the word "propina".
- **Social Security: open.** No source settles whether shared-out tips count towards
  contributions.

So the design has to be able to share tips among kitchen as well as floor staff (the Madrid case's
department included its kitchen), give payroll the
figures to withhold on, and never treat a tip as the venue's own money. The asesor questions it
raises are in §9.

**How this differs from the architecture design** (`2026-07-18-pos-architecture-design.md`). Its
"Decisions closed during design" table says "support both, configurable per tenant", which the Tips
tab does. Its §10 said "pool per pay
period"; this design pools per business day, so hours and tips are matched on the day they happen.
And it said tips passed straight to the employee who earned them "may qualify as *mediación de
pago* and fall outside withholding"; the income-tax regulation's art. 76.1.3.º takes tips the
employer pays out of that exception (research file), so here every tip the venue pays out goes
through payroll, whichever way tips are shared.

## 4. The design, as staff and managers see it

### 4.1 Taking a tip (picture 01)

- **"Take tips" becomes a venue setting** on a new **Tips** tab in Venue settings, replacing
  `WAITRON_TILL_TIPS`. Recommended default for a new venue: **on**.
- **Every way of paying offers the same choices**: a card adds "Tip (optional)"; cash offers "Leave
  as a tip" (nothing / all of the change / an amount). The whole-order screen gains the cash and
  typed-card tip it lacks today, through A739's single payment window rather than a change of its
  own.
- **The till asks; the card reader's own tip screen stays off**, so the till always knows the tip
  before the card is charged and the receipt, the cash-up and the shares agree with what was
  charged. (Today a reader that added its own tip would be recorded wrongly, §8.)
- **No suggested percentages** in the first build. A suggestion the guest can refuse is very likely
  still voluntary, but that is asked of the asesor first (Q13(d) in the research).

### 4.2 Who a tip belongs to (picture 02)

The venue chooses on the Tips tab:

- **Shared pot (recommended default).** Every tip of the business day goes into one pot, shared
  among the people who took part that day. Who takes part: everyone who clocked in that day, floor
  and kitchen, except a person whose "Takes part in tips" switch (on their Users page) is off — the
  owner, say. How it is split: **by hours clocked** (recommended) or **equally per person**. Equal
  shares are what the Madrid court applied when nothing had been agreed; by hours is a split a venue
  can agree with its staff, so the setting says, under the choice, "Agree how tips are shared with your
  staff."
- **Each person keeps their own.** A tip is credited to the person who took that payment.

A shared pot needs the working-time record. With the workforce module switched off, only "each
person keeps their own" is offered.

The house keeping tips is **not offered**: the Madrid ruling above treats card tips as the staff's
money, and the venue that wants it can switch tips off instead.

**Worked example — Bar Santet, Saturday 3 October, shared pot by hours.**

| Who | Role | Clocked | Share of card tips | Share of cash tips | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ana | Floor | 8 h | €13.60 | €5.76 | €19.36 |
| Marc | Floor | 5 h | €8.50 | €3.60 | €12.10 |
| Lucía | Kitchen | 8 h | €13.60 | €5.76 | €19.36 |
| Jordi | Kitchen | 4 h | €6.80 | €2.88 | €9.68 |
| Pere (owner) | — | 9 h, takes no part | — | — | — |
| **Pot** | | **25 h** | **€42.50** | **€18.00** | **€60.50** |

Card tips that day came from seven payments, cash tips from four guests who left their change.
€60.50 over 25 hours is €2.42 an hour. A cent that cannot be split evenly goes to the largest
remainders, so the shares always add up to the pot exactly.

The same day **with each person keeping their own**: Ana took five of the tipped payments (€31.00),
Marc six (€29.50); Lucía and Jordi get nothing, because they took no payment.

**Hours** come from the working-time record, counted to the business day: a shift 19:00–01:30
counts in full to Saturday. Someone still clocked in when the day's figures are read counts up to
that moment; the shares are worked out again as hours are corrected, until the pay period closes
(§4.4).

### 4.3 The daily close: cash tips leave the drawer that day (picture 03)

- **This waits for a counted close.** Today Sales & takings shows the day's figures read-only and
  no screen counts a drawer (§2). Handing out cash tips needs A239 (recorded cash in and out of a
  till's drawer) and a close that compares the counted cash with the expected cash.
- With those, the close (shown in picture 03 on Sales & takings, where today's read-only close is)
  gains one block, **Cash tips to hand out**, listing each person's share of
  the day's cash tips and one **Hand out** button. The manager picks the drawer the cash leaves;
  handing out records one A239 entry per person on that drawer ("tips", the amount, who received
  it), so the expected cash in that drawer already allows for it when it is counted. No amount is
  typed.
- **Card tips stay with the venue until payroll.** The money arrives from the card company days
  later; each person's card share goes on the pay-period statement (§4.4) for the gestoría to pay
  with that period's wages, with tax withheld.
- A venue can instead choose **all tips through payroll** (Tips tab): then nothing is handed out at
  the close, and cash tips stay in the takings like card tips. Which is right turns on asesor
  question L6(g) (§9): whether cash the venue hands out at the close also needs tax withheld.

Worked example, continued: at Saturday's close Ana, Marc, Lucía and Jordi are handed €5.76, €3.60,
€5.76 and €2.88 from Till 1's drawer. Its expected cash drops by €18.00.

**When hours change after the hand-out.** Shares are worked out again as hours are corrected (§4.2),
so a person can end up having been handed more cash than their final share. The difference is shown
on the pay-period statement as owed back, and comes off that person's tips in the next period —
never off their wages.

### 4.4 The Tips page and the pay-period statement (pictures 04 and 05)

A **Tips** page under _Team_ on the dashboard, with two views:

- **By day**: the pot (card, cash, tips given back), each person's hours and share, what was handed
  out in cash, and a warning when a day's tips had nobody taking part to share them ("€6.00 not
  shared — nobody taking part clocked in. It goes into Tuesday's pot."). Such a day's tips move to
  the next day's pot, the same way a shortfall does (§4.5), so nothing is ever shared by hand.
- **By pay period**: one row per person: their shares, cash already handed out, and **to pay
  through payroll** (shares less cash handed out). A **Download for the gestoría** button gives a
  CSV of that table. When the payroll export exists, its tips column comes from the same figures.

**Closing the pay period freezes the tips.** The workforce module has a function that closes a
pay period and freezes its hours, but nothing in the product calls it yet (§2); slice 6 needs that
closing step first. When a period is closed, its tips statement is frozen with it and never
changes. A refund lands on its own day, which is in an open period. A correction to a day in
a closed period changes nothing in that period's statement: the difference it makes to that day's
shares is added to the next open period's statement. (The working-time record itself shows such a
correction beside the frozen period instead; money has to move forward to be paid.) A manager can
look but not edit; there are no hand-typed shares.

Worked example, continued: for October, Ana's shares come to €412.80, of which €96.30 was handed out
in cash, so €316.50 goes through payroll.

### 4.5 Refunds and voids

- The refund rules stay: a tip goes back only all at once, with everything still unrefunded of its
  payment, and never more than was given.
- **A tip given back comes off the pot of the day it was given back**, the same rule the cash-up
  already follows for money ("counted on the day it moves", bill payments design §9a). Example: a
  €30.00 card payment with a €3.00 tip, taken on Saturday, is refunded in full on Monday; Monday's
  pot is €3.00 smaller. If Monday's pot goes below zero, the shortfall comes off the next day's
  pot. With each person keeping their own, it comes off the person who took the original payment.
- **Voids.** A void does not touch a payment today. When the void screen is designed (backlog,
  "Cash handed back for a voided cash sale is recorded nowhere"), a tip given back with it follows
  the refund rule above.

### 4.6 A tip on a split bill

Each payment's tip is its own, as today, and every one goes into the day's pot. With each person
keeping their own, each tip goes to whoever took that payment. Example: table 4 owes €80.00; Laia
pays €40.00 by card with a €4.00 tip, Joan €40.00 in cash and leaves €2.00 of his change. The pot
gains €6.00; with own tips, both go to Marc, who took both payments.

### 4.7 What the receipt shows (picture 06)

Unchanged: the invoice part (its lines, total and VAT), then below it, outside the invoice, each
payment with its tip. For a bill payment that is "Tarjeta" with what was charged, or "Efectivo" with what
was handed over and "Cambio" when there is change, then "Propina" when there is a tip (`apps/server/src/receipt-document.ts:395-413`).
A whole-order cash payment prints no tip today because it cannot take one; it gains the same line
when A739's payment window replaces that screen. Nothing else is added to the receipt.

## 5. What a tip never does

- Enter the sale's total, the invoice, the VAT summary, the Veri\*Factu record or its hash.
- Count as the venue's income: tips keep their own column and total, and the VAT and invoiced
  totals exclude them. (Today's "Tender total" on Sales & takings counts money taken, tips
  included, beside its own tip total; it stays a count of money.)
- Pay part of the bill, or be moved to another payment (as today).
- Have its share or statement figure typed in by hand: every such figure comes from payments,
  refunds and hours, and Hand out types no amount. (The tip itself is typed at the till.)

## 6. Data (decided in the build's plan; listed here so the size is visible)

A new **tips module** (`packages/tips`), because sharing tips needs both the payments and the
working-time record and belongs to neither (house rule: a new domain lands as a module). It needs
migrations:

- the venue's tip settings (state);
- who does not take part (state, one row per person excluded);
- cash handed out at a close (ledger, append-only: amount, person, day, who handed it out, when),
  written together with A239's drawer entry;
- the frozen statement of a closed pay period (ledger, append-only: period, person, shares, cash
  handed out, to pay through payroll).

Shares themselves are worked out when read, from the stored payments, refunds and hours, until the
pay period is frozen. A tip already stored on a payment does not change.

## 8. Defects and gaps found on the way (new backlog entries)

1. **A negative tip is not refused on `POST /api/pay`** (read, not run). With tips on, a request that
   did not come from the till's own form (which accepts only an amount of zero or more with at most two decimals,
   `apps/till/src/state/bill-payment.ts:42-47`) can carry `tip: "-5.00"`: the route does not check
   `tip` (`apps/server/src/till-api.ts:1682-1737`) and `decimal()` accepts it
   (`packages/shared/src/money.ts:14-25`), so the reader would be asked for the total less €5.00 and
   the tender would then fail its check `tip_amount >= 0` after the card was charged. A tip with
   more than two decimals is not checked either. The bill payment routes refuse both
   (`apps/server/src/bill-payments-api.ts:32`). With tips off, `tipOf` turns any tip into 0.00
   (`apps/server/src/till-sale.ts:1371-1375`).
2. **The tender records the expected amount, not the captured one** (read): a capture that differs
   from bill plus till tip is written as bill plus till tip (`apps/server/src/till-sale.ts:1568`,
   and `:1879` for an order already placed); only the recovery path reads the captured amount. The
   only cause found is a reader whose own tip screen is switched on outside Waitron; other ways a
   provider could capture a different amount were not checked.
3. **The dashboard's order detail leaves out a bill payment's tip** (`apps/dashboard/src/widgets/order-detail-dialog.ts:398`).
4. **The card slip's words are fixed Spanish** (`apps/server/src/payment-slip.ts:55-59`), and the
   Spanish refund slip says "Propina" where the English says "Tip returned"
   (`apps/server/src/tender-refund-receipt.ts:79-92`).
5. **The old "Tip-collection UI" backlog entry was out of date**: cash and typed-card tips arrived
   with bill payments (#956). It is replaced by A793; its other point — the till's Pay tab shows a
   general error for `sale.empty_basket` (`apps/till/src/till-app.ts:11896-11909`, read) — is
   already its own entry, A848.

## 10. The owner question (Q38)

**Q38. Do you approve tips as designed, with these six choices?** "All recommended" is a complete
answer.

- **(a) Who a tip belongs to.** Recommended: **the venue chooses on the Tips tab: a shared pot
  (the default) or each person keeps their own.** The house keeping tips is not offered.
- **(b) How the pot is split.** Recommended: **by hours clocked, among everyone who clocked in that
  day, kitchen included, unless a person's "Takes part in tips" is off**; equal shares per person as
  the other choice. By hours is a split to agree with the staff: the one ruling read (Madrid, 2023,
  through a blog; it follows a 2017 País Vasco ruling known only from that blog) split equally where nothing had been agreed, and L6(e) asks the asesor whether that binds a
  venue with no agreement.
- **(c) One pot per business day.** Recommended: **yes**. Alternative: one pot per service period
  (lunch, dinner), shared among the people clocked in during that period.
- **(d) Cash tips.** Recommended: **handed out at the day's close, card tips through payroll**, both
  on the pay-period statement, once a close that counts the drawer exists (A239) and if the asesor's
  L6(g) allows it; until then, and as the other choice, all tips through payroll.
- **(e) Taking tips.** Recommended: **a "Take tips" switch in Venue settings, on for a new venue;
  the till asks for the tip, never the card reader; no suggested percentages until the asesor
  answers.**
- **(f) The order of the work.** Recommended: **the six slices in §7, in that order** (slices 5 and
  6 wait for the work they name).
