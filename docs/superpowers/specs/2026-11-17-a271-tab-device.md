**Contract.** Allow the chooser in devMode or a Demo venue, with Live always refusing its headers. Anyone reaching the Demo service on the venue network can list devices and act as any listed active till without pairing. This access to sample data follows the owner's request; staff sign-in and permissions still apply.

Chooser tabs never borrow device or session cookies, including before selection, after list failure, or with blocked sessionStorage. Keep the device choice in tab memory, optionally retaining it in sessionStorage; keep sign-in only in memory. Reload locks again. Use existing startup/error UI when the list fails. Ordinary appliances outside chooser mode retain cookies.

Preserve `x-waitron-person`, session hashes, device/session binding, revocation and origin checks. No per-tab pairing, device secrets, tables, registry, adoption or rotation protocol, or new Retry screen.
