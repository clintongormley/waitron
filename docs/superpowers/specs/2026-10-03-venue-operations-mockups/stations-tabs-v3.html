The numbers update on their own. Waiting, Being made and Ready follow the cook's taps on the kitchen screen. A station with no screen has nobody tapping, so it shows "No screen" there; its dishes count as waiting, and lateness runs from when the ticket printed until the dish is served. Late counts dishes past the station's late flags (Settings tab); Oldest is the longest a dish there has waited. Click a number to see those dishes.

Create, rename and remove all happen here: "New station"; ⋮ → Rename · Make default · Switch off. A station is never deleted, only switched off (greyed at the bottom, ⋮ → Switch on), because past orders and tickets still name it.

Yes, every cell is edited in place. The dropdown's blank first entry clears the cell, which then shows what it inherits in grey. Every cell always shows its result, set (normal text) or inherited (grey), so you never have to work the rule out. The rule: an empty cell looks along its own row first (to "Every zone"), then up to the parent category's row, ending at "All categories". The "All categories / Every zone" cell is the default station; changing it is "Make default". Switched-off stations are not offered.
