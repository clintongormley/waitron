Where does the trading name print?

Today's receipt header, top to bottom: the legal name, then the line you can write yourself under it (optional, the same for the whole venue), then the NIF. The trading name is a new line (highlighted). The printer prints every line in the same size, so position is the only difference.

First, above the legal name

La Charcutería de Ana
Restaurantes Gormley S.L.
Charcutería · Quesos · Vinos
NIF: B12345678
— — — — — — — — — —
Order 42 · Invoice D-000123

The name customers know is what they see first; the legal name and NIF follow as the small print. Most shop receipts read this way.

Keeps today's first line, so the issuer stays on top, and the trading name reads as a subtitle above your own line.

Grey line: your own optional header line, as today. Either way the legal name and NIF still print, as the receipt rules require.
