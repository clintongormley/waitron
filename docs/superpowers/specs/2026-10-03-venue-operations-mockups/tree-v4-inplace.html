Departments and zones as one table, edited in place

No side editor at all, the same as the Prep stations tabs: click a cell to change it. Department rows add two columns, the trading name and its receipt switch; on a zone row those cells are empty because a zone has neither. A zone changes department by being dragged onto another one.
