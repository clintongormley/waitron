# A664: personal data in every current schema table

**STATIC SCHEMA AUDIT · 2026-10-16 · PROPOSED POLICY · NO RUNTIME INSPECTION**

You cannot assume the venue holds no identifying information merely because it has no customer profile table. The schema declares names and contact fields in bookings and accounts, invoice identities, worker/actor links, card metadata and provider references. This audit reads every declared column in every latest manifest snapshot. It does not inspect venue rows, establish prevalence, execute writers/readers, decode every payload or certify that a field is legally necessary. All interpretations below are conservative review categories, not findings that the values are populated.

## What the schema can hold

- `persons` has display/first/last names, telephone, email variants and Google subject, alongside authenticators: `packages/identity/src/schema/persons.ts:28`.
- `bookings` has required `contact_name`, optional `contact_phone` and free-text `notes`, plus a tab and creator: `packages/bookings/src/schema/bookings.ts:35`. There is no dedicated email column in this booking snapshot; text fields can still carry one.
- `sales`/`working_orders` carry invoice recipient tax/name/address fields; `purchase_invoices` identifies suppliers. An individual trader’s business identity can identify a natural person. Retain only the required evidence identity; do not generalise it to every contact copy.
- `payments` declares provider/payment/external references, card scheme, last four, entry mode and authorisation code: `packages/payments/src/schema/payments.ts:47`. References can link to provider records and people. This audit has not inspected arbitrary text, provider traffic, logs, binary print payloads, backups or actual stored values.
- A669 adds `party_allergy.answer/changed_by`, `party_allergy_guests.who/allergens/note` and `allergy_line_ticks` linking guests to draft or working lines: `packages/venue-service/src/schema/party-allergy.ts:20`, `:34`, `:64`. A description such as “woman at seat 2” can link health to a diner without a civil name. Dish allergen declarations in products/ingredients differ from diner-linked allergy evidence; working-line contexts can join them to an order.
- Employment, pay rate, shifts, absences, attendance, time corrections, closure snapshots and actor links can identify workers. Free text, JSON/XML/document fields, rendered binary/media and indirect order/table/payment/time links need content and consumer tracing even when no person foreign key is declared.

Necessary staff, invoice and evidence identities remain with their duties and holds. Unnecessary identifying fields receive separate purpose review and earlier approved removal. Obsolete authenticators, sessions, delivery claims and disposable queues remain purpose-bound machinery; the shared live schedule never gives them years of retention. Hiding, archiving or deleting in the UI does not establish evidential removal eligibility. See the [design](2026-10-16-a664-data-retention-design.md) for C-LIVE, retired-record checks and G0–G5; all product/report code remains gated.

## Read each field category as a review instruction

Each table lists **all columns exactly once**, grouped by the following categories. A dash means no column assigned to that bucket, not proof of absent personal data. The categories record schema review, not a runtime detector or a legal classification algorithm.

| Category | What you assess                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D        | Direct identifying/contact/business-identity field, including identifiers of sole traders; determine the actual subject and evidence need.                                                                                                                                  |
| H        | Explicit diner allergy answer/health content, or worker absence kind/note potentially revealing health; G3 must decide basis and necessary evidence.                                                                                                                        |
| S        | Authentication/ceremony/secret material or credential-linked key; keep purpose-bound and distinguish public verification proof from obsolete operational secrets.                                                                                                           |
| L        | Linkable keys, actors, references, network addresses, hashes and source/replay pointers. Configuration IDs are not automatically personal, but joins and provider access can identify people.                                                                               |
| U        | Text, document/JSON, binary/media or other content needing semantic inspection, including configured labels. These fields may carry identifying or health details; a label is not presumed a person’s name. Some schema strings have validation not executed by this audit. |
| R        | Remaining scalars, timestamps, enums and business facts. They can become personal through the same row’s joins/time context; this bucket is not “anonymous” or deletion clearance.                                                                                          |

Health can also appear in U, especially order/draft/kitchen/booking notes, reasons, event detail, adjustment policy/note, receipt payloads and closure snapshots. Payload subfields and embedded IDs need a later writer-to-copy audit; enumerating a column cannot enumerate its dynamic values. All row-level dependencies in the [retention appendix](2026-10-16-a664-retention-table-inventory.md) still apply.
