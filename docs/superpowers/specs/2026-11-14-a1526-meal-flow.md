## One place to read and edit the meal

Open a department and select **Tables**, between Service and Receipts. One list contains:

- **Grey fixed moments:** Party seated, Bill requested and Party leaves. You cannot move or
  delete them. Party leaves holds the department's Needs clearing switch.
- **Blue waiter steps:** editable names, starts and delays, with Add step and Delete.
- **Amber kitchen courses:** the venue's courses, in their existing course order. Each row offers
  Fire now / Hold for later. A held course also offers its own reminder delay, including At once
  and Off, after the preceding course is served.

The Courses page keeps course names and their order. **Service** keeps orders, payment, the
collection ticket and the manager requirement for sending after a period ends. Tables shares the
department's existing draft and Save. Each department owns its send/hold choices.

Grey identifies fixed settings, leaving the floor's Bill requested colour unchanged. Labels and
icons accompany colour in both themes and at phone width.

## Restaurant: follow one table

Restaurant sends Starters with the order, holds Mains with a ten-minute reminder after Starters,
and holds Desserts with an immediate reminder after Mains. Its four replacement starter steps are:

| Step | When it becomes due |
| --- | --- |
| Set up | At once when seated |
| Take order | Two minutes after Set up is done |
| Dessert? | Five minutes after Mains are served |
| Coffee or bill? | Five minutes after Desserts are served |

Seat T9 at 20:30: Set up appears. Done at 20:33 makes Take order due at 20:35. Send the order:
Starters go to the kitchen; Mains and any preordered Desserts wait. Starters served at 20:55 makes
the Mains reminder due at 21:05. The waiter fires Mains. Mains served at 21:30 makes Dessert? due
at 21:35 and any held Desserts due to fire immediately. The reminder never fires food itself;
Dessert? does not gate preordered desserts. Desserts served at 21:50 makes Coffee or bill? due at
21:55.

Keep the existing latest-due step, Done, order-clearing and party-leaving rules. Keep Fire,
Snooze, per-table hold/release choices and group rearrangement. Fiscal records, payments, notices
and other floor-state priorities retain their existing behaviour.

**An interaction for your judgement:** today's reminder follows the table's actual groups, which
can skip or mix courses. Should a missing Starters prevent a Mains countdown? I recommend keeping
the waiter's actual sequence: wait for earlier groups to be fully served, name the actual
predecessor on the table, and use the earliest enabled reminder among courses mixed into one held group.
With no preceding serving there is no countdown; Fire stays available. Please confirm this
interpretation, since the new wording names a course while table overrides can change the sequence.

## Deli and a department without courses

Deli selects Fire now for every course, so it has no course-fire countdowns. It can remove all
waiter steps if it does not visit tables. Food without a course continues to send immediately;
counter orders keep their existing release behaviour.

**Recommended no-course start, for your agreement:** create only Set up and Take order. Once you
have courses, add Dessert? and Coffee or bill? using Add step and select the actual courses they
follow. Do not create waiting records, infer courses from their names or manufacture courses.
Does this two-step start until you choose the course links suit you? I recommend it over reminders
whose starts cannot happen. Keep existing authored steps when defaults change.

3. **Fixed colours or venue choices?** Recommend fixed colours by kind and state, using the
