## One place to read and edit the meal

Open a department and select **Tables**, between Service and Receipts. One list contains:

- **Grey fixed moments:** Party seated, Bill requested and Party leaves. You cannot move or
  delete them. Party leaves holds the department's Needs clearing switch.
- **Blue waiter steps:** editable names, starts and delays, with Add step and Delete.
- **Amber kitchen courses:** the venue's courses, in their existing course order. Each row offers
  Fire now / Hold for later. A held course also offers its own reminder delay, including At once
  and Off, after the preceding course is served.

The Courses page keeps course names and their order. **Service** keeps orders, payment, the
collection ticket and the manager requirement for sending after a period ends. Tables shares the
department's existing draft and Save. Each department owns its send/hold choices.

Grey identifies fixed settings, leaving the floor's Bill requested colour unchanged. Labels and
icons accompany colour in both themes and at phone width.

## Restaurant: follow one table

Restaurant sends Starters with the order, holds Mains with a ten-minute reminder after Starters,
and holds Desserts. In this example its reminder is At once after Mains;
that is an example choice, not an owner-decided default. Its four replacement starter steps are:

| Step | When it becomes due |
| --- | --- |
| Set up | At once when seated |
| Take order | Two minutes after Set up is done |
| Dessert? | Five minutes after Mains are served |
| Coffee or bill? | Five minutes after Desserts are served |

Seat T9 at 20:30: Set up appears. Done at 20:33 makes Take order due at 20:35. Send the order:
Starters go to the kitchen; Mains and any preordered Desserts wait. Starters served at 20:55 makes
the Mains reminder due at 21:05. The waiter fires Mains. Mains served at 21:30 makes Dessert? due
at 21:35 and any held Desserts due to fire immediately. The reminder never fires food itself;
Dessert? does not gate preordered desserts. Desserts served at 21:50 makes Coffee or bill? due at
21:55.

Keep the existing latest-due step, Done, order-clearing and party-leaving rules. Keep Fire,
Snooze, per-table hold/release choices and group rearrangement. Fiscal records, payments, notices
and other floor-state priorities retain their existing behaviour.

The party takes its department from its first table's zone, as today's reminder does. Use that
same department for default send/hold choices. Moving a party changes defaults for newly added
dishes; it does not change dishes and groups already arranged or sent. Per-table choices still win.

Keep today's actual group sequence: earlier groups must be fully served before the next held
one counts down, and without an earlier serving there is no countdown. Read the setting from the
held group's first course when it mixes courses. Label the row "after the course before it is
served", rather than promising that every table ordered Starters before Mains.

Coffee or bill? does not appear for a table that ordered no dessert. Dessert? can still appear
when desserts were preordered. These follow the starts the owner chose, without another fallback prompt.

## Deli and a department without courses

Deli selects Fire now for every course, so it has no course-fire countdowns. It can remove all
waiter steps if it does not visit tables. Food without a course continues to send immediately;
counter orders keep their existing release behaviour.

For a fresh venue, create its country preset's courses before its starter steps. The preset
explicitly links Dessert? to the Mains id and Coffee or bill? to the Desserts id, rather than
matching translated names. A new department reuses those explicit course links. If a linked
course does not exist, start with Set up and Take order only; add the later steps when you choose
their actual course links. Do not manufacture courses or dormant steps. Keep existing authored steps.
New courses start held in each department, preserving today's default; their reminder starts at
ten minutes, today's department default, and each department can change or switch it Off.

## Reuse and the minimum data change

Reuse follows read-only source inspection, not runtime verification. Data changes are proposed.

- Reuse `department-settings`, `department-service-steps` and `service-step-draft` in
  `packages/venue-service/src/dashboard/`, with their existing draft protection and controls.
- Reuse `service_steps`, `service_step_completions`, `kitchen_courses` and order groups.
  `apps/server/src/service-step-reckoning.ts` supplies starts and clearing;
  `apps/server/src/order-groups.ts:1809` supplies today's group reminder reckoning.
- Move `kitchen_courses.held_until_fired` and `departments.release_reminder_minutes` to
  department/course settings. If named predecessors below are accepted, store the referenced
  step rather than deriving it from position. Carry these settings through configuration transfer.
  The per-department course settings belong to venue-service, alongside its service steps.
  A deleted course loses its settings rows; a deleted named step removes dependent starts, following
  the existing deleted-course rule. Before go-live, rebuild the affected starting migration from
  the schema and reset practice venues. Make fresh backups and exports after the change.

## Three open choices

1. **Automatic placement or dragging?** Recommend placement from each step's named start,
   replacing “after the step before” with “after Set up”, for example. Courses retain their
   order; steps sit by their starting event with their delay visible. Actual times can overlap.
   Repeating and Everything served starts remain available and explicitly labelled. Dragging
   must not silently change what starts a step.
2. **Amber outline for an undone step?** Recommend Waiting's amber outline five minutes after
   the step becomes due, for every step. Keep its blue identity and show how long it has waited.
   Existing stronger warnings take precedence. Waiting already uses amber for a warning with
   nothing to do yet; the bell and elapsed time distinguish a late step. Your recorded
   “probably makes sense” is tentative.
3. **Fixed colours or venue choices?** Recommend fixed colours by kind and state, using the
   shared theme tokens. No colour picker per venue or step: the same cue should mean the same
   thing wherever you work.
