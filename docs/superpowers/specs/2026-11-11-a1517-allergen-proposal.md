# A1517: allergen choices before go-live

These are recommendations for your choices, not permission to build. Costs are estimates in developer days, including focused tests, English/Spanish wording and guides. [Your October decisions](2026-10-14-a668-allergen-info-editor-and-till-design.md#decisions-owner-2026-10-14) warn about missing information at publication, remove “Not reviewed” from kitchen screens and retire the till’s allergen-sheet screen. The recommendations below preserve those decisions; point 3 asks whether to bring existing menu work forward.

1. **Filtering unchecked dishes and extras.** The running filter retains an unreviewed dish and a reviewed salad offering walnuts when nuts are excluded. A mounted unreviewed tile has no mark (R1; `apps/till/src/menu-filter.ts:66`). Recommend excluding unknown or incompatible *choices* during an active allergen filter: use each variant’s effective declaration, leave optional extras separate, remove incompatible preselected extras, and hide a dish if its required choices cannot be completed. Unknown extras follow the same rule. These additional combinations are source-identified, not runtime-verified here. A guest says “no nuts”: unknown soup disappears; salad remains without walnut topping. **Estimate: 2–4 days. Your choice:** apply this exclusion, or retain today’s rules? Recommend exclusion, without adding tile warnings contrary to October decision 6.

2. **Kitchen paper.** A sent milk-declaring burger supplies milk to the kitchen queue but prints only its name when no guest allergy is recorded; guest-specific printed instructions have separate passing tests (R2; `apps/server/src/kitchen-print.ts:351`). Recommend printing recorded declarations on every ticket, with selected extras separately and guest instructions prominent. Preserve October decision 8: no “Not reviewed” label. A cook sees “Milk” beneath a burger without a guest first being flagged. **Estimate: 1–2 days after point 5. Your choice:** declarations on every ticket, or guest-specific instructions only? Recommend every ticket.

3. **Information for guests.** The retired allergen destination is rejected and one sample guest invoice has no allergen list; these runs do not establish absence across every public surface (R3; `apps/till/src/navigation.ts:17`). October decision 5 chose printed/online menus, A670/A509, instead of a separate sheet. Recommend bringing forward a small A670 cut: printable customer menu with dish, variant and extra declarations, reusing published content and printing. A diner reads the soup’s declaration before ordering. **Estimate: 3–5 days**, subject to A670’s design. **Your choice:** prioritise this before go-live, or keep the existing sequence? Recommend prioritising A670; do not restore the retired screen.

4. **Choosing extras.** Mounted checkbox and quantity choices omit their nut/milk labels; a dish-information control renders “Milk” (R4; `apps/till/src/widgets/modifier-picker.ts:985`, `:1006`). Recommend declared-allergen labels beside each extra, using existing wording, without introducing missing-information warnings against your October principle. Someone choosing white sauce sees milk before adding it. **Estimate: 0.5–1 day**, excluding filtering. **Your choice:** labels before selection, or today’s presentation? Recommend visible labels.

5. **Earlier orders.** Editing milk to eggs changes a sent table row’s nutrition; its saved context/version retain milk. Parked counter retrieval separately retains its original declaration (R5; `apps/server/src/line-nutrition.ts:24`). Recommend freezing the chosen effective declaration at sending in existing order context, and using it consistently for historical order and kitchen displays/paper. Preserve variant inheritance; never substitute the parent offer’s context value for a child’s declaration. A burger sent yesterday keeps its recorded milk. Published versions can lag edits before sending; an old declaration can under-warn if the recipe changes before cooking. Keep current-recipe guest-clash checks explicit rather than silently changing them. **Estimate: 4–6 days. Your choice:** distinguish recorded declarations from current recipe checks, or retain live declarations throughout? Recommend the distinction, clearly labelled.
