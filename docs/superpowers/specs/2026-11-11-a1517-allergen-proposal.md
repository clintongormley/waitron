You need to distinguish recorded declarations from missing information. These are recommendations, not owner-approved choices. The watcher presents the choices below; nothing is authorised for implementation. Costs are estimates in developer days, including focused tests, English/Spanish wording and guides.

1. **Filtering unchecked dishes and extras.** The running filter retains both unreviewed dishes and a reviewed salad offering walnuts when nuts are excluded. The mounted unreviewed tile has no warning; a declared-allergen control has a mark (R1; `apps/till/src/menu-filter.ts:66`, `apps/till/src/widgets/menu-browser.ts:665`). Recommend hiding unreviewed dishes while an allergen exclusion is active. Keep optional extras separate, applying the same exclusion to choices in their picker. A guest says “no nuts”: the unknown soup disappears, while salad remains with walnut topping unavailable. **Estimate: 1–2 days. Owner choice:** hide unknowns and incompatible extras, or retain them with explicit warnings? I recommend hiding them under the active filter only.

2. **Kitchen paper.** A sent milk-declaring burger supplies milk to the kitchen queue but prints only its dish name on paper when no guest allergy is recorded. Recommend printing recorded dish declarations on every ticket, with selected extras separately and missing declarations labelled “Not reviewed”, reusing the print builder and saved menu data. A cook sees “Milk” beneath a burger without somebody first flagging a guest. **Estimate: 1–2 days, after point 5. Owner choice:** declarations on every ticket, or guest-specific instructions only? I recommend every ticket; keep those instructions prominent.
