## The problem and the choice

You cannot charge a restaurant bill above €3,000 by issuing the simplified invoice (F2) that Waitron currently issues for every sale. The amount is the **whole invoice total including VAT**, not the cash or card contribution being taken. This build lets you select a full invoice (F1) for any bill on request and requires it before a bill above €3,000 is charged. The €10 tolerance in an AEAT technical validation is not permission to issue an F2 over the regulatory ceiling. A230's entry refusal is a temporary barrier; the F1 path replaces it only when every issuance route is covered.

## Choose the invoice before money moves

Show **Full invoice** beside the bill's payment action, at any total. When you choose it, ask for the Spanish customer's NIF/NIE/CIF, complete legal name and full postal address (street, number or equivalent, postcode, locality, province, country). The same dialog opens automatically when the bill's VAT-inclusive total exceeds €3,000. In `invoice_first` mode the invoice is filed **when the order is placed**, before collection ([working-order.ts:5289](../../../apps/server/src/working-order.ts#L5289)); show the same choice/dialog and validate it **before Place**. Once placed, its invoice type and recipient cannot change; a later request for a full invoice from an issued F2 waits for the F3 build. In pay-first and ticket-then-pay modes validate before any cash submission or card capture. Reuse A230's tax-ID check-letter validator after A230 lands, on the client for guidance and on the server as the authority. The check-letter test is a format and checksum test, not evidence that a person owns that ID. If A230 is absent or its API differs, reconcile the plan before building.

Use the shared field primitives and the [forms contract](../../developers/design-system.md#forms): required markers, semantic names, inline errors and one bottom message after an invalid attempt, then disable only while the form's own fields remain invalid. A server refusal stays retryable. Show the choice and a short customer summary on the bill while it remains unissued, so a second till can see it. Record the decision and recipient details with the working order under a revision check; retain an issuance snapshot on the sale. Do not log tax ID, name or address in error params or incident text. The form must distinguish a customer request from the amount rule. A bill at exactly €3,000 may stay F2 unless F1 was requested; €3,000.01 requires F1.

One bill has one invoice regardless of how many **payment portions** settle it. Pay selected items, contributions, equal shares, cash and card payments keep their existing tender allocation. A payment portion of €1,000 against a €3,500 bill is not an F2. **Splitting items into new bills is different:** the current split path files one invoice per resulting bill ([split-bill.fiscal.test.ts:254](../../../apps/server/src/split-bill.fiscal.test.ts#L254)). On 2026-10-04 the owner clarified that a large bill may be split between different people; it must not be divided into smaller F2 bills for the same person to evade a full invoice. Staff confirm on the till that the resulting bills belong to different people before splitting a bill over €3,000 or moving lines off it. Cancelling leaves the bill unchanged. The confirmation adds no declaration record, audit row, table or column. Each resulting bill has its own invoice choice and threshold check; never copy one customer's identity to another bill silently. Recheck a bill whose lines later grow past the ceiling. Before **each** payment begins, reprice the whole bill and read its saved invoice choice in the same transaction that reserves money. An F1 choice may be added after partial payment while the bill remains unissued; changing identity needs the bill revision and must refuse while a card payment is pending. Once money has moved, do not silently downgrade from F1 to F2. If a line move, adjustment or card recovery would make a bill fully paid, its issuing transaction must either have a valid F1 snapshot or refuse before the change that triggers issue. A captured card cannot be uncharged by an invoice refusal, so pre-capture validation and persisted F1 details are essential. If a historical/old-client path arrives without them, stop issuance and expose a recovery task; never fall back to F2. This is a proposed product invariant, not a claim about existing behavior.

At issuance, one shared selector reads the priced **final bill** and the explicit choice. It selects F1 with a Spanish recipient when requested or over the ceiling, otherwise F2. The core recording boundary repeats the amount/recipient/series checks before allocation. Direct `/api/sales`, `/api/pay`, unpaid-invoice/collect, unpaid departure, card finalize/recovery, bill payments, zero-total paths and mutations that call `issueIfFullyPaid` all use that selector. Walk-up `/api/pay` and `/api/sales` requests carry the F1 choice and recipient details themselves; the first transaction validates and persists them before a provider call or capture, because no earlier bill choice exists. A stale client, replay or delayed card answer must read the committed choice; a replay shows the filed invoice and never recomputes its type. A foreign business customer's F1 is refused with `fiscal.foreign_recipient_unsupported` until an explicit `IDOtro`/`IDType` decision. Do not invent a Spanish NIF or silently issue F2 instead.

The current whole-invoice credit path calls a fiscal correction that explicitly refuses any original other than F2 ([backend.ts:380](../../../packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/backend.ts#L380)). Its R5 record must never be used to cancel an F1. The first F1 build therefore **refuses automatic whole-invoice credit, refund and cancellation on F1** with a clear operator message and a route to a reviewed manual remedy; it must not first settle the bill or refund tender. The owner must approve this interim limitation and obtain the asesor's remedy procedure before F1 issuance is enabled. A later R1–R4 correction design requires its own authorization because this item does not authorize changing the alta builder.

## Three series, one chain

Provision three **distinct codes** at setup: ticket/F2 (`standard`, default FS), full/F1 (`full`, proposed default FF), and rectificativa (`rectificative`, default FR). Store the new purpose in `invoice_series` and add its generated migration; preserve existing rows and counters. A node has one live series per purpose. Existing preproduction venues with only two series need an explicit reset and new setup under the repository's no-compatibility rule; do not guess a full-series code during an upgrade. The server chooses the live full series by node at issuance, so it does not rely on the boot environment's `WAITRON_TILL_SERIES_ID`, which currently names only the ticket series. Treat a missing, retired, duplicate or wrong-node series as a refusal before any invoice number is allocated. F1 draws from `full`; F2 from `standard`; R1–R5 correction paths remain `rectificative`. **Provisional F3 design:** guard its unused core path to `full` rather than `standard`, but do not expose F3 until the asesor confirms Q5(d)/Q17(b). AEAT's application describes a series for ordinary **or** substitutive invoices; that wording does not itself require F1 and F3 to share one. Series purpose is a numbering rule, not another hash chain.

The same purpose must travel through setup review/API/CLI, configuration transfer, reserved identities, promotion and cold restore. A restore retires the old live series and opens a disjoint code for **all three** purposes; no existing code or number is reused. Before build, reconcile the exact migration/rebuild method with the repository's no-compatibility rule and the upgrade test. This proposal does **not** authorize changes to the hash, invoice allocator, immutable rows, alta builders, or `registerSif`/`restoreFiscal` behavior. Change purpose selection around those seams and keep their golden tests untouched.

## Deliver a readable F1

| Medium | What it offers | Cost or obstacle now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing 58/80 mm receipt printer | Works at the till and can hand the customer paper immediately. The current formatter already prints a QR and VAT totals ([receipt-ticket.ts:165](../../../apps/server/src/receipt-ticket.ts#L165), [receipt-ticket.ts:232](../../../apps/server/src/receipt-ticket.ts#L232)). | Long address and item descriptions need careful wrapping; 58 mm print quality and QR size need a real-paper check. |
| Email PDF | Convenient for a long business invoice and later retrieval. | Requires a PDF renderer, trusted delivery/address handling and consent tracking for consumers; art. 9.2 has a 2026 B2B exception, so one generic consent switch is wrong. A PDF email alone is not the selected delivery guarantee. |
| A4 office printer | Room for conventional columns. | The print agent currently detects office printers ([ipp-probe.ts:121](../../../apps/print-agent/src/ipp-probe.ts#L121)); it does not provide this F1 print path. New routing, driver and paper-failure behavior would delay charging. |

**Recommendation:** print a distinct F1 on the linked receipt printer, supporting both 58 and 80 mm, with a manual reprint of the same filed invoice. Put one 30–40 mm fiscal QR first, before the invoice content, with its caption. Then show “Factura completa”, issuer legal name/NIF/**domicile**, F1 series and number, issue date and operation date if different, then customer legal name/NIF/full address. The operation date comes from a saved service date; for a service spanning calendar dates, the asesor must settle which date to use before implementation. Snapshot that decision at issue so a duplicate keeps it. Show each operation with quantity, unit price **without VAT**, discount if any, taxable base, rate and VAT; wrap text without dropping a value. End with a per-rate base/VAT summary, total, and payment/tip information visually separated from tax amounts. `tenants` holds legal name/NIF but no domicile ([tenants.ts:61](../../../packages/db/src/schema/tenants.ts#L61)); `locations` holds a venue address ([tenants.ts:92](../../../packages/db/src/schema/tenants.ts#L92)), which may differ. Capture a distinct taxpayer domicile during setup, require it for F1, and snapshot it alongside the customer address on the sale. Do not silently use the venue address. Project the filed net unit price, line base and discounts into the F1 view so a reprint does not reverse-calculate them from gross totals. The per-rate summary must reconcile with the stored fiscal VAT breakdown. Use the filed line and VAT snapshots, never live catalogue labels or recalculated rates. The on-screen view and duplicate paper copy use the same F1 data and mark a copy as such. If no print job is created or a job fails, show **F1 not delivered** with a reprint action until paper is handed over; neither case reverses the filed sale. Real 58 and 80 mm output, including the QR's measured physical size and scanning, must be inspected before approval to land the implementation.

## What reports count

An ordinary F1 is one sale, counted once in sales, daily close, category and VAT reports from the filed `sales.vat_breakdown`, exactly as an F2 is. The invoice detail and export identify F1/F2 and series, and include the recipient/address for F1 with appropriate access control. Tips remain outside the taxable invoice total. Do not count an eventual F3 as a second sale: it restates already-issued F2 operations, and its report behavior needs its own build. The current VAT aggregation reads the sale's stored breakdown ([vat-summary.ts:22](../../../packages/reporting/src/vat-summary.ts#L22)); acceptance tests must prove the F1 and F2 amounts each appear once, including mixed tenders and corrections.

## Later work and decisions to return

**F3 conversion is later.** `recordSubstitution` exists but has no route ([record-substitution.ts:73](../../../packages/core/src/record-substitution.ts#L73)); it already records a separate substitution with no tender and references F2 sales. A post-issue exchange needs lookup, recipient capture, a duplicate-exchange refusal, delivery and report tests, plus advisor Q17's series/identity confirmation. Adding it to this path would increase irreversible filing risk without helping a bill above the threshold, which must choose F1 **before** issue. Do not void the F2 or issue an ordinary F1 for a retrospective request.

Ask the asesor, without treating these as answered law:

1. Does the effective B2B electronic-invoice timetable/format apply to this venue and its Spanish business customers at launch? Which business cases, if any, may still receive paper F1? Confirm the art. 8 bis exception and its relationship to consumer consent.
2. May one restaurant visit or a large bill be split into several F2 invoices for distinct real customers/operations? What evidence distinguishes a genuine split from evasion of the €3,000 ceiling? Waitron must never auto-split to bypass it.
3. Confirm whether this F1 layout contains every required description, unit-price, discount, operation-date and address detail for the venue's actual services, and whether 58 mm paper is an acceptable delivery medium.
4. For later F3: may it share the F1 series, what `Destinatarios` structure is required, and which previously issued F2s may it exchange (Q5(d), Q17)? For foreign customers, which `IDOtro`/`IDType` and verification procedure apply (A1a/Q17(a))?
5. What reviewed manual remedy is available for an issued F1 that needs whole-invoice cancellation or a refund while automatic R1–R4 correction is outside this build? Approve the explicit F1 credit/refund refusal only if that remedy is acceptable. For a service spanning dates, which operation date belongs on the F1?

**Owner gate:** approve the F1-first scope, paper delivery recommendation, series name/default, customer-data handling and B2B deployment boundary before implementation. A separate owner approval is required before landing code that touches fiscal issuance.
