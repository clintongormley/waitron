## The amount a pick means

Today an extras item has a pick count and an optional price, but no portion (`packages/catalogue/src/schema/extras.ts`, `modifier-list-types.ts`). The offer resolver borrows the product's unit price for a blank item price (`packages/catalogue/src/extra-projection.ts`), and the server builds a child priced at dish count × picks (`apps/server/src/modifier-selection.ts`, `packages/catalogue/src/pricing.ts`). A product priced per kilogram therefore needs a stated amount per pick. These are code-path findings, not a run of the current system.

When you first add an extras list item for a weighed unit (`hardwareUnit` set) or a fractional unit (`precision > 0`), enter a positive **portion** in that product's effective unit. A newly entered or changed portion must fit the unit's precision and the database's thousandths quantity scale. For an Each product, the portion is one and the editor shows no portion field. Picks remain whole numbers and the existing per-item and per-list pick limits still count picks. The request still sends picks, never a customer-entered weight.

**2026-10-04, W75:** the list editor now asks for a portion for every unit but Each, and shows an Each row's portion as a fixed 1 in its own Portion column; see the W75 entry in [the backlog](../../backlog.md).

**2026-10-05, W75c:** the server now refuses an item with no portion, for every unit but Each, unless the list already holds that id for the same product; see the W75c entry in [the backlog](../../backlog.md).

**2026-10-05, W75d:** an item the list already holds under its id for the same product, sent without a portion, keeps its saved portion unless its product is Each; see the W75d entry in [the backlog](../../backlog.md).

**2026-10-05, W75e:** an item whose product is Each is refused with any portion but one, even one equal to the portion saved before the product became Each; see the W75d entry in [the backlog](../../backlog.md).

For example, put Jamón in grams at 0.10 €/g and set its portion to 50 g. Three picks add 150 g and cost 15.00 € on one dish; two of that dish add 300 g and cost 30.00 €. Alternatively put Jamón in kilograms at 100.00 €/kg and enter `0.050` kg: three picks add `0.150 kg` and cost 15.00 €. The amount is stored and printed in the product's own unit. There is no g↔kg conversion, and the per-gram price need not express every per-kilogram price exactly (A203).

**Price is per portion.** A blank price computes `portion × effective product unit price` and rounds that result once to a cent; a filled price is already the gross per-portion price, including zero. Multiply this one rounded price by picks and dish count. The dashboard's blank-price hint, the till picker, and the server use this rule. A rounding-sensitive case is 0.050 kg at 0.27 €/kg: one pick rounds from 0.0135 € to 0.01 €, so three picks cost 0.03 €, not a rounding of 0.150 × 0.27 €.

Keep the existing offer rules around the new amount. The current source and test assertions show a list-item price taking precedence over the product's price (`packages/catalogue/src/extra-projection.test.ts:103`), a blank price borrowing that product price (`packages/catalogue/src/extras.ts:17`), the till multiplying per-pick price by picks and dish count (`apps/till/src/state/order-line.test.ts:108`), and inactive lists being filtered from a live offer (`packages/catalogue/src/offered-modifiers.ts`). These tests were read for this handoff; dependencies are absent in this worktree, so this documentation pass did not execute them.

## Saved lines and presentation

The child `working_order_lines.quantity` records `portion × picks × dish count` at the quantity scale, and its `unit_name` and `unit_precision` snapshot the extra's effective unit. The child price remains the cent-rounded price **per portion**. A frozen `price_quantity` on the child records the amount that one stored price buys; ordinary dish and option lines use one. Carry that price basis to `sale_lines` so the filed physical quantity and informational unit price still have an explicit relationship. The amount due is `stored per-portion price × (physical quantity ÷ price_quantity)`, with an exact pick count on the extras path. This is needed because `grossLockedLines` currently multiplies unit price by line quantity, and both the working and sale line money columns hold cents (`packages/catalogue/src/pricing.ts:225`, `packages/db/src/schema/orders.ts`, `packages/db/src/schema/sales.ts`). Dividing a cent-rounded per-pick price by a 0.030 kg portion would not generally produce a representable cents-per-kilogram price.

One child keeps its own price basis when the catalogue portion or product unit changes. `editLineExtras` matches a kept pick using the child's frozen price basis and the stored dish count, so an unchanged pick retains its child id and price even if the live offer changes. A changed pick is a new selection under the live offer. Every proportional child operation (quantity edits, splits, transfers, voids, kitchen quantities, totals, adjustments, staff reports, and whole-invoice corrections) must use the frozen price basis rather than treating a physical amount as a whole pick. `packages/shared/src/quantity.ts`'s `perDishOptionQuantity` returns a rounded whole count and currently feeds receipt and edit reads; it cannot recover three picks from a 0.150 kg child. The till's held-order edit must recover picks from the frozen portion too (`apps/till/src/screens/till-table-order-screen.ts:2087`). Receipts, the till's filed-ticket view, and kitchen `extraLabel` instead show the **total physical amount** and the snapshotted unit abbreviation: `+ Jamón 150 g` or `+ Jamón 0.150 kg`, never `50 g ×3`. Each surface retains its established name language (`apps/server/src/receipt-ticket.ts`, `kitchen-print.ts`; `apps/till/src/screens/till-ticket-view.ts`).

**2026-10-03, W53:** The owner later chose `x3` on the receipt, kitchen ticket and filed-ticket view when the unit was Each as the line was added. A weight or volume unit keeps its total amount and saved abbreviation. The [W53 backlog entry](../../backlog.md) records the follow-up; the A203 decision above remains as written.

## Editing units and publishing menus

Changing an offered product's unit, changing a parent's unit inherited by a variant, or changing a unit's precision can change what a stored portion means or whether it fits display precision. Before Save, name the affected extras lists **and menus** in the product or unit editor. Find lists containing the product or affected variants, then menus whose working offers contain those lists, including menus that include another menu. Include directly assigned products and variants without their own unit, using the effective-unit resolution in `packages/catalogue/src/variant-fallback.ts`; `productsUsingUnit` currently lists direct assignments only (`packages/catalogue/src/units.ts:220`). The warning does not disable Save. Preserve the stored thousandths amount and make no unit conversion. (2026-10-03 (A222): a variant now always has its product's unit, so a parent's unit change reaches every variant, and `productsUsingUnit` lists products with no parent only, now at `units.ts:229`.)

The existing menu publication path builds a working document from `listMenuOffers`, stores that document on publish, and serves its offers with only live availability and reporting fields applied (`packages/catalogue/src/menu-document.ts:116`, `menu-publication.ts:402`, `menu-document.ts:502`). Extend each published extra item with its exact portion and effective unit snapshot, including precision and abbreviation. A till keeps selling each menu's published portion, unit and per-pick price until that menu is republished. A new publication uses the same stored numeric portion with the **new** effective unit and resolves its per-pick price for that publication. For example, changing g to kg leaves `50` as `50`, now 50 kg; it never converts to `0.050` kg.

If a later precision change from 3 to 2 leaves a saved `0.055` kg portion, preview warns with its list and product names. Publishing still succeeds and freezes `0.055 kg`, even though the unit now displays two places for newly entered amounts. The till, saved line, receipt and kitchen output use all three stored decimal places; none rounds it to `0.06`, drops the item or refuses a new sale for precision alone. The extras editor shows the saved value and warning. You can keep that exact value through an unrelated list edit; entering or changing a portion must pass current precision validation, so you can correct it to a valid amount.

Each affected menu's pre-publish changes list names the extras list, extra product and old and new unit or precision, whether the extra is also a dish on that menu or appears on several lists. Preview also shows a warning when the proposed snapshot's portion exceeds current precision. This needs a list-aware extra-unit comparison: the current extra-only diff keeps one entry per product, omits units and skips products also offered as dishes (`packages/catalogue/src/menu-document.ts:614`, `menu-document.ts:681`, `menu-document.ts:981`). The preview already has a warning area that does not stop publication (`apps/dashboard/src/widgets/menu-preview.ts:452`).

## Boundary and acceptance

The change reaches catalogue offers, pricing, working orders, adjustments, reporting, presentation, and the core line snapshots they use. No work on `computeHuella`, the hash chain, invoice numbering, `registros_facturacion`, alta builders, `registerSif`, or `restoreFiscal`. Line totals feed the sale, so the real sale path and its unchanged golden huella must be checked (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/write-path.e2e.test.ts`). Keep the append-only `inmutabilidad` guard green (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/inmutabilidad.test.ts`).

Accept the build when new portions are validated against the effective unit; blank and explicit prices yield the correct rounded per-pick charge in till and server; one, three, and dish ×2 picks produce the expected physical amount and total; held edits preserve old snapshots; receipts and kitchen output print the amount and unit; both warning paths name affected lists and menus and still save; each menu's changes list names the unit change; old published offers stay live until republished; and `0.055` survives a 3-to-2 precision change through preview, publish and sale without rounding or refusal. Keep the existing whole-unit behavior, VAT class, sale total, golden huella, and append-only guard covered. The full review path applies because these amounts reach a sale record (A203). The [implementation plan](../plans/2026-10-03-extra-fixed-portion.md) names the red-first checks and review gates.
