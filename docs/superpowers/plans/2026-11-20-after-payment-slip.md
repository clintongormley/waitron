# Print a slip after payment

Staff can choose **Print payment slip** in Customer document after a cash, reader or Other card machine payment. One press queues one original for that payment. Extra presses leave the same job in Print Queue, where staff can request a later copy. Other card machine starts on **This SumUp account** when the venue has connected SumUp, with **Paid outside Waitron** as the other choice. Without that connection the till records **Paid outside Waitron** directly.
