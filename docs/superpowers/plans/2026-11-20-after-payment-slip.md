# Print a slip after payment

Staff can choose **Print payment slip** after cash, reader or Other card machine payments. Customer document offers it after the final payment. The existing payment confirmation offers it to each earlier payer. One press queues one original and any later copy comes from Print Queue. Other card machine offers **This SumUp account** first when the venue has connected SumUp and **Paid outside Waitron** as the other choice. Without that connection the till records **Paid outside Waitron** without asking.
