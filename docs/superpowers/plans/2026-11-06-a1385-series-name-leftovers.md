## Decisions

**A1226 (the lane's decision, taken from the review):** nothing on a page sends `select` (restore) or `candidates` (setup) unless **Make the series was pressed on this page load and nothing has cancelled that press since**. If it was not:
- a countdown that reaches zero clears itself and re-enables Make the series; it sends no request;
- a "no second clock" explanation after Compare clocks starts no 15-second check; the person presses **Check again**.
The pressed path keeps exactly its current behaviour: its countdown's end, its 15-second second-clock check and its taken-minute countdown still check with the box and finish by themselves.

What cancels a press:
- Restore page: `#invalidate` (key, either declaration, Clear, disconnect: `recovery-hour.ts:186-193`) and **Compare clocks / Check again** (`#compare`). A press followed by Compare clocks is a fresh start: what Compare clocks shows is the unpressed case.
- Setup page: `#clear`, `#edit` (the details tick, `first-hour.ts:257-264`) and **Check again** (`#compare` unticks the details, `:292`).

Why nothing is lost: the box keeps a recorded wait current for a minute past its target (`hour-selection.ts:456-460`, `WAIT_REUSE_MS = 60_000` at `:67`), so a press within that minute takes the name at once; a later press records a fresh 90-to-150-second wait. The guides already say "Choose **Make the series** once the page allows it" (`apps/server/src/guide/en/backup-recovery.md:141-142`; Spanish `es/backup-recovery.md:153-154`).

**The Cloud entry (decided by M1):** no fix to the download path. Task 1 pins M1 and the reviewer's retry.

**A1223, wording at activation (built):** Task 9 words the setup app's refusal; Task 10 gives the start-failure page words for `sif.allocation_invalid` (Q2's recommended default).

**A1223, a new attempt by itself (Q1's default: not built).** If the owner keeps the default, A1223's first half becomes a recorded decision; until then it stays in the backlog (Task 11).

## Owner questions

**Q1. A setup or restore whose own chosen name turns up in the box's records before it finishes.** Today the hour pages say "The series cannot be made yet: this box's records already hold a series for the minute this box would use." and offer nothing; "yet" is false, because waiting never helps (`hour-selection.ts:428-430` refuses with no time). Which records could gain the minute in between is M6: other attempts' picks cannot; of the database's writers, the plan did not rule out a standby joining while an attempt is open. A1207's probe found no run outside hand-edited test data that reaches the refusal. A new attempt by itself would mean a new restore or setup event chosen by each of three screens, and for a restore already staged it would mean going back to the page after the restart.
*Recommended default (built in Tasks 8-9):* call it a fault in Waitron, in the words under "Words for the owner"; on the restore page also say that reloading and starting the restore again makes a new attempt with a new name (each page load makes a new restore event: `apps/setup/src/screens/recovery-allocation.ts:117-123`, and its only mount passes no event, `recovery-copy.ts:325-330`; a new event's choice skips the old pick's minute, M6). No automatic new attempt.
*Alternative:* build the automatic new attempt (a design of its own; not in this PR).

**Q2. A restore refused when the box places the backup after its restart.** Example: a Cloud restore downloads, the box restarts to place it, and at that moment the box cannot read the internet clock (M1). The refusal is `sif.allocation_invalid` (`second_clock_missing`). What happens today (M2): the staged restore is kept; Docker restarts the box and each start tries the placement again; after three failed starts in a row the start-failure page appears, saying "Waitron could not start. Ask whoever installed this box to look at it.", which sends the person to somebody else, though its "Retry a normal boot" button would finish the restore once the internet clock answers (M1, the reviewer's retry).
- (a) Give `sif.allocation_invalid` its own start-failure words (proposed under "Words for the owner"). The page chooses words by code alone (owner, 2026-09-26), so one sentence covers every reason that code carries at start, including a box clock more than 30 seconds from the internet's (`clock_disagreement`), for which these words give no way on: a box that is online but whose clock is off retries, meets the same refusal, and is shown this page again with nothing more to do. Only (b) can word that case.
- (b) Let the start-failure page choose words by code and reason. This changes the 2026-09-26 rule.
- Separately: should a start refused only because no internet clock answered keep retrying by itself instead of counting towards the three? Today it counts; changing it is a design of its own.
*Recommended (built in Task 10 as the default):* (a), and the restart behaviour unchanged. *Earlier recommendation withdrawn:* not asking for a second clock again at placement; M1's retry shows the refusal is not final, so the change to a fiscal check is not needed.
