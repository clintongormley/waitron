# A712 Staffing painters

## What staff can do

From either Staffing painter, open Opening hours for the same season or special day. Step between seasons and close either Staffing painter without adding a browser history entry. See the season's staffing on each special date that follows it, against that date's own business-day start. The [Staffing design](../specs/2026-10-13-a585-staffing-and-rota-design.md) supplies the painter switch and inheritance behavior.
