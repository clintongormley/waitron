# A712 Staffing painters

## What staff can do

Open the same season or special day in Opening hours from either Staffing painter. Step between seasons and close either Staffing painter without adding a browser history entry. See the season's staffing on each special date that follows it and paint against that date's own business-day start. See the [Staffing design](../specs/2026-10-13-a585-staffing-and-rota-design.md).
