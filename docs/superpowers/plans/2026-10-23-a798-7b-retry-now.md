**Words used in this plan.**
- The **outbox** is the Veri\*Factu module's queue of records waiting to reach AEAT: one `envios`
  row per record (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/schema/envios.ts`), sent by the **drain**
  (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/drain.ts`).
- A **pass** is one run of the drain; the **loop** is the server's background loop that runs a pass,
  then sleeps until the next one is due (`apps/server/src/loop.ts:54-94`).
- The **back-off** is the wait a record is given after a send that did not land, before it is tried
  again: its own `proximo_intento_en`, one minute after the first failure, doubling, at most an hour
  (`backoffMs`, `drain.ts:74-80`). This is what the design calls the "network back-off".
- **AEAT's wait** is the pause AEAT asks for between two sends (Orden HAC/1177/2024 art. 16.2, its
  `t`, sixty seconds until AEAT says otherwise), held in the one-row `envio_flujo` table as
  `proximo_envio_en` (`schema/envio-flujo.ts`).
- A **held chain** is a chain whose next record the drain stopped sending after the records before
  it were refused three times with one code (`SAME_CODE_REFUSAL_LIMIT`, `drain.ts:62`): that record
  and those after it are `detenido`, and the first is tried again only hourly (`claimProbes`,
  `drain.ts:992-1031`).
- The **seat** is a member of the fiscal module contract (`FiscalContribution`,
  `packages/fiscal/src/contribution.ts:110-161`), through which the server reaches the Veri\*Factu
  module without naming it.
- The **page** is the dashboard's Box status page (`apps/dashboard/src/screens/box-status-screen.ts`);
  the **Hacienda row** is its "Tax agency" / "Hacienda" row (`box_status.part.tax`).

**Goal:** on the Box status page a manager with `system.manage` sees when AEAT last accepted a
record and, while some records are waiting after a failed send, can press **Retry now** /
**Reintentar ahora** so they are sent at the box's next try — never before AEAT's own wait between
sends, and never releasing a chain held after repeated refusals.

**Architecture:** four pieces.
1. **The seat** (`packages/fiscal`): `FiscalContribution` gains an optional `outbox` member with two
   functions, `read` and `retryNow`.
2. **The module** (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/outbox.ts`, filled in `slot.ts`): `read` answers
   the last accepted time, how many records are waiting for a retry and when the first is due;
   `retryNow` makes those records due now, in the caller's transaction, and says when AEAT's wait
   lets the next send go.
3. **The server** (`apps/server`): a sleep the loop can be woken from (`loop-wake.ts`); a route
   `POST /api/box/fiscal-retry` (`fiscal-outbox-api.ts`) that clears the back-off in one transaction
   and then wakes the loop; and the page's read (`GET /api/box/health`) carrying `outbox`.
4. **The dashboard**: the Hacienda row's detail line and the Retry now button with its help
   tooltip, refusal and answer; the guides in English and Spanish.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Hono, `node:sqlite` behind drizzle, Lit, Vitest 4.1.12 (Node for
`packages/*` and `apps/server`; real headless Chromium for `apps/dashboard`).

**Spec:** [A798 — Degraded but trading](../specs/2026-10-19-a798-degraded-trading-design.md),
revision 6: §2.2 (the outbox: retries from a minute, doubling to an hour; AEAT's own wait; a chain
held after repeated refusals), §4.3 (the page: "**Reintentar ahora** clears the network back-off of

§4.4 ("The Box status row shows the unsent count, the last accepted record and **Reintentar

## Measured for this plan

Scratch test: `~/waitron-campaign-b/a798-7b-plan-probe/drain-probe.test.ts`, run as
`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/zz-a798-7b-probe.test.ts` with
`env -u AI_AGENT -u CLAUDECODE pnpm exec vitest run --project node src/zz-a798-7b-probe.test.ts`
(log `drain-probe.log`, `Tests 6 passed (6)`), then moved out (`git status --short` empty). It
drives the real `drain` against `@waitron/verifactu`'s fake AEAT through a fetch that throws while
"down", seeded with `seedPendingEnvios` (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/test/drain-fixtures.ts`), and
counts submissions by the request body naming `RegFactuSistemaFacturacion`. Its "press" is the
statement this plan builds (decision 2):
`update envios set proximo_intento_en = :now where estado = 'pendiente' and proximo_intento_en > :now`.

- **M1 — what a failed send leaves.** Three records, the network down, a pass at 00:01:00: all three
  `pendiente`, `intentos` 1, `incidencia` 1, `proximo_intento_en` 00:02:00; `envio_flujo`
  `proximo_envio_en` 00:02:00, `tiempo_espera_seg` 60. So a failed send sets AEAT's wait as well as
  each record's back-off (the `upsertFlujo` after the send loop, `drain.ts:369-370`, runs after the
  `catch` breaks out). With `intentos` raised to 6 and a second failure at 00:02:01, each record's
  retry is 01:02:01 (an hour) and AEAT's wait ends at 00:03:01.
- **M2 — the loop can sleep an hour after a failure, so a cleared back-off alone waits.** The pass
  after that second failure reported `nextDueAt` 00:03:01 (AEAT's wait); the pass at 00:03:02
  submitted nothing and reported `nextDueAt: null`. The loop sleeps the ceiling on `null`
  (`sleepMsFor`, `loop.ts:12-22`; the existing case "sleeps the max when there is no work
  anywhere", `apps/server/src/loop.test.ts:30`, `Tests 15 passed (15)`, `loop-test.log`), and the
  ceiling's default is an hour (`DEFAULT_MAX_TICK_MS`, `apps/server/src/config.ts:133`). Nothing
  else ends that sleep but shutdown: the loop's only sleep is `realSleep` on the shutdown signal
  (`boot.ts:2703`, `loop.ts:42-52`) **(read)**. So without a wake, a press could take effect up to an
  hour later (decision 4).
- **M3 — a press does not send before AEAT's wait; it does once the wait has passed.** After M1's
  second failure, the network back up, a press at 00:02:30 changed 3 rows and left `envio_flujo`
  as it was; a pass at 00:02:31 submitted nothing and reported `nextDueAt` 00:03:01; a pass at
  00:03:02 submitted once and AEAT accepted 3. **Control:** the same history with no press — the
  pass at 00:03:02 submitted nothing (`P2c`). The failing case, a press that skips AEAT's wait,
  would have printed `before-gate submits=1`; it printed `0`.
- **M4 — a held chain stays held, and a record behind it is not sent.** Three records refused with
  code 1100, a fourth appended and held (`detenido`), and a fifth appended with a back-off standing
  in for a failed send (`proximo_intento_en` 01:00:00, `intentos` 2). A press at 00:03:30 changed 1
  row (the fifth); a pass at 00:03:31 submitted nothing, held 1 (`recordsHalted: 1`: the fifth went
  `detenido`, `intentos` 3, `enviado_en` stamped), and left the fourth's `enviado_en` (00:02:00,
  from which its hourly retry is counted) unchanged. The failing case would have printed
  `submits=1` or the fourth no longer `detenido`. The fifth was claimed and held by the press,
  which is what its own retry would have done an hour later; decision 3 keeps the press off such
  records so its count says only what can go.
- **M5 — a press during a pass leaves the batch being sent alone; a second press finds nothing.**
  Two records; a pass whose send waits on a signal; while both rows were `enviando`, two presses
  each changed 0 rows; the send then failed and both rows were backed off to 00:02:00 (not to the
  press's 00:01:01); a press after the pass changed 2.
- **M6 — a full sending batch goes before AEAT's wait, by AEAT's own rule.** With the batch cap set
  to 2 (`maxRegistrosPorEnvio`, the drain's test seam, `drain.ts:98-103`) and three records, a failed
  pass backed two off; a press at 00:01:10 changed those 2, and a pass at 00:01:11, before AEAT's
  wait ended at 00:02:00, submitted one batch of 2. That is the drain's existing rule
  (`drain.ts:226-230`: it waits for AEAT only while fewer than a full batch are due), which is art.
  16.2 c's: «deberá esperar a que transcurran «t» segundos desde el anterior envío o deberá esperar
  a tener acumulados un número de registros de facturación igual al límite establecido en el diseño
  de registro para cada envío, la circunstancia que ocurra primero» (BOE-A-2024-22138, fetched
  2026-10-23, `boe/art16.txt`). In production the cap is 1,000 (`MAX_REGISTROS_POR_ENVIO`); a press
  only brings forward a send the back-off would have allowed later (decision 5).
- **M7 — what art. 16.4 asks of a retry.** «se deberá proceder a la remisión de los registros de
  facturación en cuanto sea posible» and «deberá reintentar periódicamente, al menos una vez cada
  hora» (same file). A press asks for "as soon as possible" sooner; the hourly duty is the drain's
  and is unchanged.
- **M8 — what sets a back-off, in the whole tree.** `grep -rn -E 'proximo_intento_en *=|proximoIntentoEn'`
  over `packages` and `apps`, outside tests: only two writers set a time later than now —
  `backoffBatch` (`drain.ts:1098-1112`, a send or the saving of its answer threw) and
  `awaitReadableAnswer` (`drain.ts:1551-1570`, AEAT answered but the answer for a record could not
  be read or trusted). The others set `now`: `requeueClaims` (`:515`), `releaseSettledBrakeHolds`
  (`:1071`), reconcile's resubmission (`reconcile.ts:384`), and the column's default at insert
  (`schema/envios.ts:28`) **(read)**.

## Departures from the design and from picture 4

1. **The press also wakes the loop.** The design says the press "clears the network back-off … so
   the next pass sends them". M2 shows the next pass can be an hour away, so the press ends the
   loop's sleep as well (decision 4). Without it the button would do nothing visible for up to an
   hour.
2. **"los envíos fallan desde 17:31" is not drawn.** The row's detail shows the last accepted time
   and, while records wait, when the next try is (decision 10). No column says when sends started
   failing; the technical incident's open time could, but that is a second read for one phrase.
3. **Records behind a held record are not cleared** (decision 3). The design is silent; M4 shows
   such a record is held, not sent, so clearing it would only inflate the count the answer gives.
4. **The button is drawn only on a box that files** (decision 9). A demo or prepare box, and a
   pre-production box without test submissions, never run the drain
   (`fiscalDrainEnabled`, `apps/server/src/onboarding-policy.ts:11-16`).

## Decisions this plan makes that the spec does not

Each is a DEFAULT to build; the owner or the plan review may overrule any.

1. **The seat is an optional `outbox` member of `FiscalContribution`, not of `FiscalBackend`.** The
   contribution already carries the submission side — `drain` and `resetInFlight`
   (`contribution.ts:126-131`) — while `FiscalBackend` is the sale-path backend
   (`contribution.ts:123-125`, "it records locally and never contacts an authority"). It also keeps
   this slice out of `packages/fiscal/src/backend.ts`, which PR #2025 changes. Shape:
   ```ts
   /** What the outbox holds, as the Box status page shows it. */
   export interface FiscalOutboxState {
     /** When the authority last accepted a record, with or without errors; null if it never has. */
     readonly lastAcceptedAt: string | null;
     /** Records waiting to be sent again after a send that did not land: what `retryNow` clears. */
     readonly retryWaiting: number;
     /** The earliest of their retry times; null when none waits. */
     readonly nextRetryAt: string | null;
   }
   export interface FiscalOutboxRetry {
     /** How many records `retryNow` made due. */
     readonly cleared: number;
     /** When the authority's own wait lets the next send go, if that is after `now`; null when
      * nothing holds the next send back. */
     readonly sendsResumeAt: string | null;
   }
   export interface FiscalOutboxSeat {
     /** Select-only; the host calls it in a write-lock turn with no change-log drain. */
     read(tx: Transaction, now: Date): Promise<FiscalOutboxState>;
     /** In the caller's transaction: makes every record waiting for a retry due at `now`. It never
      * moves the authority's own wait between sends, never releases a record held after repeated
      * refusals, and never touches a record being sent. */
     retryNow(
       deps: { readonly tx: Transaction; readonly environment: DeploymentEnvironment },
       now: Date,
     ): Promise<FiscalOutboxRetry>;
   }
   // on FiscalContribution:
   readonly outbox?: FiscalOutboxSeat;
   ```
   No word in it is on `packages/fiscal`'s forbidden list (`no-regime-vocabulary.test.ts:55-71`).
   `fiscal-none` fills nothing; the server then mounts no route and reads no outbox.
2. **"The network back-off" is every `pendiente` record whose `proximo_intento_en` is later than
   now** — the only per-record back-off there is (M8). Both writers are cleared: a send that threw
   and an answer that could not be read. Why both: either way the record's next step is the same
   resend, which the drain makes safe against AEAT's duplicate check (`handleDuplicate`,
   `drain.ts:1726`) **(read)**; the press only makes it sooner; and the table cannot tell the two
   apart without reading the incidents. The clear writes `now` in the column's one spelling,
   `now.toISOString()`, as `requeueClaims` does (`drain.ts:515`; CLAUDE.md §3, a time compared as
   text).
3. **What the press leaves alone:** `envio_flujo` (AEAT's wait; M3); every row not `pendiente` —
   `enviando` (being sent; M5), `detenido` (held, with its `enviado_en`, from which the hourly retry
   is counted; M4), `retenido` (set aside for a filing case) and the final states; and on the rows
   it clears, `intentos`, `incidencia` and `technical_incidence`. Keeping `intentos` means a retry
   that fails again waits as long as before, and keeps `haltOpenChainClaims`'s first-claim test
   (`own.intentos = 1`, `drain.ts:819`) meaning what it means. Keeping the two flags keeps the next
   send marked as following a technical incident, as art. 16.4 asks (M3's rows kept `incidencia`
   1). **It also leaves a `pendiente` record that sits behind a `detenido` record on its own chain**
   (an earlier `secuencia` on the same `sif_id`): M4 shows the drain would hold it, not send it.
   `read`'s `retryWaiting` and `nextRetryAt` count the same set `retryNow` clears, through one SQL
   fragment both use.
4. **A press wakes the loop; the route never runs a pass itself.** A new `createLoopWaker`
   (`apps/server/src/loop-wake.ts`) gives `runLoop` its `sleep` and the route a `wake()`. A wake ends
   the sleep under way, or the next one if a pass is running, but never sooner than `minTickMs`
   (5 s by default, `config.ts:135`) after that sleep began, so presses cannot run passes back to
   back. One wake is spent by one sleep. Shutdown still ends any sleep at once. The shape follows the
   content-suggestion runner's wake (`apps/server/src/content-suggestion-runner.ts:189-228`:
   `AbortSignal.any` over the shutdown and a wake signal, and a flag for a wake that arrives while
   no sleep is under way). Why not run a pass in the request: the drain would then have two callers
   in one process, the request would wait on AEAT, and the pass's own containment (`attempt`,
   `apps/server/src/pass.ts:161-190`) would be bypassed. A woken pass also runs the card
   reconciliation duty early; it runs only what is due (`runDue`), so this is accepted.
5. **The answer says when the next send can go.** `retryNow` answers `sendsResumeAt`: AEAT's wait's
   end when it is after `now` and fewer than a full batch are due after the clear, else `null` — the
   drain's own condition (`drain.ts:226-230`), computed with the drain's own `readFlujo` and
   `countDue` (`drain.ts:400-421`), which Task 2 exports unchanged, against
   `MAX_REGISTROS_POR_ENVIO`. `retryNow`'s inner function takes the cap as a parameter defaulting to
   that constant, as `drain` takes `maxRegistrosPorEnvio` (`drain.ts:98-103`), so a test can show the
   full-batch case with three records (M6).
6. **The route is `POST /api/box/fiscal-retry`, `system.manage`, in a new file
   `apps/server/src/fiscal-outbox-api.ts`**, beside the page's own routes (`/api/box/health`,
   `/api/box/free-space`, `box-health-api.ts:40-53`) but not in that file, which PR #2025 changes. One
   `withTransaction`: `authorizeManager` with `system.manage` (as `box-health-api.ts:28-29`), then
   the filing check, then `seat.retryNow` (CLAUDE.md §3, one transaction per route; queries awaited
   in turn). After it commits: `wake()`, then the log line, then the answer. Mounted only when the
   seat has `outbox`, as the certificate route is only when the seat has `certificate`
   (`boot.ts:1963-1978`). The body is ignored (`{}` from the dashboard, as Free sends).
7. **Refusals.** A box that does not file now (`filesRecords()`, `boot.ts:1528-1529`, asked per
   request because the role can change) answers a new code **`fiscal.not_filing`** (409), thrown
   inside the transaction before anything is written, with no wake; it is registered in
   `apps/server/src/errors.ts` as `Record<string, never>` and worded in the dashboard's `codes.ts`
   ("This box does not send records to the tax agency." / "Este equipo no envía registros a
   Hacienda."). A full disk answers `storage.disk_full` (507) through the shared error boundary
   (`packages/server-kit/src/error-boundary.ts:34-40`), with no wake: the press writes, so unlike
   the page's read it has no full-disk fallback. A mirror or fenced node never reaches the route:
   the read-only gate refuses every POST there (`boot.ts:1537-1556`) **(read)**, and the page draws
   the button quiet there (decision 11). The session codes are the page's (`box-health-api.ts:17-22`).
8. **Two presses, or a press during a pass, need nothing shared.** Each press is its own
   transaction behind the write lock; a second finds nothing to clear and answers `cleared: 0`
   (M5); a batch being sent is `enviando` and untouched (M5). No in-flight sharing like Liberar's
   (slice 7 decision 10): unlike a deletion, a clear that finds nothing is harmless and its answer
   true.
9. **The page's read gains `outbox`.** `BoxHealth.outbox` is
   `(FiscalOutboxState & { canRetry: boolean }) | null`: `null` when the seat has no `outbox` or the
   box does not file now; otherwise the seat's `read`, in its own write-lock turn with no change-log
   drain (as the read's other turns, `box-health.ts:68-93`; CLAUDE.md §3's stated exception for
   reads that must answer on a full disk), awaited after the staff notices. `canRetry` is
   `!isReadOnly()` (`boot.ts:1537`), the same condition as Free's `canFree`, kept under its own name
   because it governs another action. The last accepted time is the newest `confirmado_en` of an
   `aceptado` or `aceptado_con_errores` record, ever, through the certificate card's existing
   `lastAcceptedQuery` (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/certificate-card.ts:41-56`) with
   `since = new Date(0)`; "accepted with errors" counts because AEAT accepted it.
10. **The Hacienda row's words.** Its line, dot and Go to stay as slice 7 built them (the unsent
    count comes from the `fiscal.records_unsent` notice, `box-status-rows.ts:184-198`). Its detail
    line, new, is "Last accepted {time}" / "Último aceptado {time}" (picture 4), or "None accepted
    yet" / "Aún no se ha aceptado ninguno", then, while `retryWaiting > 0`, "next try {time}" /
    "próximo intento {time}" from `nextRetryAt`. No detail when `outbox` is `null`.
11. **The button.** "Retry now" / "Reintentar ahora", in the Hacienda row's actions, drawn only when
    `outbox` is not `null`, with a help-mode "?" (`mode="help-mode"`, CLAUDE.md §3) opening the
    guide's new `{#retry-now}` section through `guideHref("box-status.retry-now")`. The button rule
    (`docs/developers/design-system.md:2707-2716`, A416 and A427): `secondary` and disabled while no
    record waits for a retry (`retryWaiting === 0`), on a read-only node (`canRetry` false, with
    "This box only keeps a read-only copy, so it can't retry here." / "Este equipo solo guarda una
    copia de lectura, así que aquí no puede reintentar." under the row), and after a press until the
    box is read again; `primary` once it can act, keeping `primary` with `loading` while its own
    press is sent. No confirmation: it changes only when records already due to be retried are
    tried. After a press the row shows the answer (`role="status"`) — "{count} records will be sent
    at the box's next try." / "{count} registros se enviarán en el próximo intento del equipo.";
    with `sendsResumeAt`, "{count} records will be sent at {time}, when the tax agency allows the
    next send." / "{count} registros se enviarán a las {time}, cuando Hacienda permita el siguiente
    envío."; with `cleared: 0`, "No record was waiting to be sent again." / "Ningún registro esperaba
    para volver a enviarse."; each count with a one-record variant — and calls
    `api.liveData.refresh()` as Free does (`box-status-screen.ts:222`). **A refused press shows
