# Print a full invoice again from Find a bill

After you close Customer document, reopen the paid bill’s full invoice from **Find a bill**. If the office printer may have missed the original, the window names that printer, explains what happened and offers **Print it again**. Check the printer before sending it again. The window follows the result as Customer document does.
