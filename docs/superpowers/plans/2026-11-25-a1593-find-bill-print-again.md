# Print a full invoice again from Find a bill

When you reopen a paid full invoice from **Find a bill**, the window names the office printer if its original may have failed, explains what happened and offers **Print it again**. Check the printer before sending it again. The window follows the result as Customer document does. This applies to paid invoices you open from **Bills** or **Transactions** when the invoice has a bill.
