## Review Focus

1. The invoice creator, line-credit recipient, later collector and current device operator differ: attribute only the actual recorded fact; passive traffic changes nothing. Owner: Task 2 `attributes facts rather than adjacent rows` and its required gap controls.
2. Cutover changes, DST, exact endpoints and days missed while offline: use historical half-open business-day bounds, never fixed 24-hour subtraction. Owner: Tasks 3/5 `catches up across changed cutover and DST`.
3. An old open shift has no later activity, or later same-person clock events already exist: invent nothing and never break the newer live state. Owner: Task 4 `old open shift and historical append matrix`.
4. Processing, live clock-out and two worker passes compete: commit one end/question identity or none, including rollback after an append. Owner: Task 4 `racing close and rollback`, Task 5 `replays committed day`.
5. SMTP accepts before the connection/process dies; an old unanswered question survives several days and restore: avoid automatic ambiguous resend and retain the question. Owner: Task 6 `ambiguous acceptance and restart`, Task 1 `old own questions`.
