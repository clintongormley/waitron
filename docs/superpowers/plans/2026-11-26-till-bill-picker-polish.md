# Match the bill picker to the approved design

Staff can read the current bill and its balance or status at a glance, then open a roomy list to switch bills or start a new one. The watcher assigned this follow-up in Lane F’s `questions.md` on 26 November at 22:23 after Lane P reviewed #4431. [Design §§14.1–2 and 14.4](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md) · [Initial bill picker](2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-pass-1.md#pr-1-find-and-switch-bills-from-the-bottom-of-the-screen).

1. **Closed picker.** In `apps/till/src/screens/table-bill-controls.ts`, put the bill number and optional name in bold, the amount or status in muted text and the smaller muted position at the right before `chevron-down`. Keep the localized accessible name and Paid check. Keep a 44 px minimum for the trigger. A long name may wrap and the unsent hint sits on its own line below, with room for both to enlarge the trigger. Pin the painted trigger in `till-table-order-screen.bill-switcher.test.ts`, including a named bill at a narrow width. Keep the app’s Spanish label and bill-ID assertions in `till-app-table-service.test.ts`, using the trigger’s accessible name for the full label.
2. **Open list.** Use 13a-0’s `wt-row-actions` menu sheet at narrow widths, with one visible small-capitals heading naming the sheet and that same text as the native radio menu’s accessible name. Keep the anchored list at the existing wide breakpoint. Ask the watcher to assign the missing shared row support in 13a-0 before the build. Its plain action button lives inside the shared row’s shadow DOM, beyond the existing menu’s keyboard search. The shared row should also paint a supplied light-DOM button so the menu can see its role and checked state, with room for the right-aligned amount and unsent details. Use 56 px minimum rows at 16 px with dividers, a teal `check` and a teal **+ New bill** with the registered `plus`.
3. **Checks.** See the new browser assertions fail first. Run the existing bill-switcher and `till-table-order-screen.bill-controls.a11y.test.ts` cases. Keep the narrow and wide placement, focus return, keyboard bill choice and one event per action checks. The shared UI suite checks sheet scrolling. Run the bill switch and draft-save cases in `till-app-table-service.test.ts` and `till-app-drafts.test.ts`. Send the watcher the built and approved `j` frames side by side at 390 px with differences listed, plus phone and till captures in portrait and landscape, light and dark, EN and ES. Get Lane P’s review before the watcher sends them to the owner.
