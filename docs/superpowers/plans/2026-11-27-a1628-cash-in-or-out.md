# A1628 · Cash in or out

The queue asks for “Cash in or out with one button that records and opens the drawer.” This slice
covers frame `cd`, panel 1, in the [till redesign](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md#714-pass-3-clocking-in-and-out-hours-the-cash-drawer-and-a-waiters-float).

- **Server and data.** In `apps/server/src/drawer-api.ts`, keep `POST /api/drawer/entries`, its
  request and reply, the assigned-printer check, `open-cash-drawer` and `cash.drawer`. In the same
  transaction as a new `appendDrawerEntry`, look up the active `cash_drawer` and call
  `enqueueManualDrawerOpen` with `requestCfg(deps.cfg, session)`, the operator and the result of
  `authorize`. The existing `manual` audit row identifies the drawer, device and operator. The
  cash entry has its own row and the two have no stored link. An identical retry returns the saved
  entry without another job. If the assigned printer is switched off, record the movement for staff
  using the key. `/api/drawer/open` remains available to the close flow and other callers.
- **Till.** In `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-cash.ts`, remove the separate Open drawer button
  and call. Use `wt-full-screen-panel` within the existing dialog's focus and dismissal flow,
  `wt-choice-tile` for Cash in and Cash out, `till-numeric-pad` and the required reason field. In
  `till-app.ts`, take the drawer's name from the equipment role for the header. Keep the Bills and
  cash entry in `widgets/till-shell.ts`. The amount-labelled button says it opens the drawer only
  when the equipment answer allows it. Otherwise show the key instruction. After saving, say to use
  the key if it stays shut. Keep the draft and same-submission retry. Update `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`,
  both `table-service.md` guides (`drawer-cash-entry`, using the Spanish `caja`), and `docs/backlog.md`.
- **Test first.** Update the movement case in `apps/server/src/drawer-close-api.test.ts` to check
  signed amounts, one matching audit row and one `drawer` job across a retry, with no `document`
  job. Preserve invalid-field, wrong-drawer, permission and switched-off checks, with no job on
  those paths. In `till-drawer-cash.test.ts`, drive the keypad and reason from the signed-in menu
  fixture and check one POST, no separate open call and the key fallback. Keep the missing-reason
  and same-submission retry assertions. Run `till-drawer-cash.a11y.test.ts`. Save 390 px light and
  dark comparisons beside `cd` in Lane I's receipts, with English and Spanish checked. Lane P
  reviews them before the owner's picture decision.

Estimate 220–320 implementation lines added and 50–100 removed, and 80–140 test lines added and
20–60 removed. Drawer close and A240 are separate slices. Close uses the current count and slip
flow for `cd` panels 2–4, including last close's carry-over, one recount and Clock out. A240 uses
`ce` to take, add and settle a person's float, credit their guest cash across devices and show open
floats in the daily email. This slice needs no schema, payment or fiscal changes. Begin after the
watcher clears the Claude-reviewed plan.
