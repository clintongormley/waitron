# A1628 · Cash in or out

Staff on a device assigned to a drawer can enter the amount and reason, then press one button to
record the cash movement and open that drawer. This is frame `cd`, panel 1, in the
[till redesign](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md#714-pass-3-clocking-in-and-out-hours-the-cash-drawer-and-a-waiters-float).

- **Server and data.** In `apps/server/src/drawer-api.ts`, keep `POST /api/drawer/entries`,
  its request and reply, the assigned-printer check, `open-cash-drawer` and `cash.drawer`.
  Inside the existing transaction, after `appendDrawerEntry` for a new submission, use the
  active cash-drawer lookup and `enqueueManualDrawerOpen` from `receipt-print.ts`. The drawer
  opening has its own audit row and job. An identical retry returns the saved entry without
  queuing again. Keep recording against the assigned drawer when its printer is switched off
  and staff use the key. Leave the standalone Open drawer action in place for its other callers.
- **Till.** Rework `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-cash.ts` under the existing drawer menu in
  `till-app.ts` and `widgets/till-shell.ts`. Use the frame's header, Cash in and Cash out tiles,
  `till-numeric-pad`, the required reason field and one amount-labelled action. Keep the existing
  draft and retry behaviour. After success say to use the key if the drawer stays shut. Update
  `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts` and the English and Spanish `table-service.md` guide sections
  (`drawer-cash-entry`). Keep the close and float work visible in `docs/backlog.md`.
- **Test first.** Extend `apps/server/src/drawer-close-api.test.ts` to check the signed movement,
  the matching audit row and one drawer job across a duplicate request, with no document job.
  Keep its invalid-field, wrong-drawer, permission and switched-off cases, checking for no
  job on those paths. In `till-drawer-cash.test.ts`, use the existing signed-in browser fixture
  to drive the keypad and reason to one POST and check the key message. Keep the existing
  missing-reason and same-submission retry assertions and run `till-drawer-cash.a11y.test.ts`.
  Capture the built panel beside `cd` at 390 px in light and dark, check English and Spanish,
  and send the comparison for Lane P's review before the owner's picture decision.
