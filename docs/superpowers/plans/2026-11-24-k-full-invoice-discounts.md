# Full invoice discount rows (A960, A961)

On an 80 mm Basque full invoice, `Deskontua 100%` fits beside the amount and VAT rate. A full invoice row that combines reductions shows `Descuento` and the same amount. A single recorded comp can still name a row when it accounts for the reduction the row shows. See the [receipt backlog](../../backlog/printers.md#bundle-receipt-layout-pass) and the owner’s [28 October rule for a row joining several steps](../../compliance/asesor-questions.md).

1. **A960.** In `apps/server/src/receipt-ticket.test.ts`, require `Deskontua 100% -1,00 10%` on one decoded raster line for the Basque 80 mm case of “prints F1 reductions before VAT, at their rate”. Keep the existing other-width and locale assertions. In `packages/orders-sales/src/receipt-table.ts`, after watching it fail, let a wrapped, indented discount caption stretch into the empty unit-price cell in a single-line VAT table if its complete caption and indent fit there. This can also keep another qualifying discount caption whole, including a shorter Basque percentage when the normal cell would wrap. Preserve amount and rate alignment and the usual wrap if it still will not fit. Use `receipt-table.test.ts` to pin the fit boundary and the existing layout of priced rows and two-line tables.
2. **A961.** In `packages/orders-sales/src/receipt-figures.test.ts`, add failing cases for two reductions that make a single-rate dish free, two separate full comps rolled into one row, and a combined dish reduction plus a bill comp. An unnamed row can say 100% only if its filed base is zero and a single recorded full comp explains the whole row. Take the family’s only full adjustment if it equals the family’s gross loss, otherwise the only adjustment on lines at that rate if full and equal to their gross loss. Reuse `lost` before VAT rounding and preserve the allocations. Keep the actual full comp in the A960 fixture and test one full comp on a dish that covers its extra at another VAT rate. When a bill reduction and a comp share one unnamed row, the caption stays plain. Verify the caption and exact amounts in `receipt-facts.test.ts`. These facts also feed A4 and emailed PDFs, reprints and the till’s receipt preview. Run the receipt-document, receipt-ticket and invoice-document suites.
