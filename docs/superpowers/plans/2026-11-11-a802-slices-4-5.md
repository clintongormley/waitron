**Words.** A **corrective invoice** (type R5, _factura rectificativa_) is a new negative invoice taking value off an
earlier simplified one. The **split** is the base (price before VAT) and the VAT at each rate. The **stored
figures** are `sales.total`, `sales.vat_breakdown` and `sale_lines`, written once at sale time. A **fix**
(_subsanación_) is a second version of a filing AEAT (the Spanish tax agency) refused. The **AEAT test system** is
its preproduction service. A **replay record** makes a request sent twice (a double tap, a lost reply) return the
first answer. A **drawer job** is the queued command that opens the cash drawer.

## 1. Outcome, and what the plans say today

- **Slice 4 (server only; staff see nothing):** in one step the server files a corrective invoice for an amount
  of a paid all-cash invoice, records that cash going back, opens the drawer once, and answers a repeat with the
  same corrective. A €12.10 steak at 21% (base €10.00, VAT €2.10): asking for €6.05 files −€5.00 base and −€1.05
  VAT and records €6.05 cash out; the same request again files nothing.
- **Slice 5 (staff can use it):** Find bill → paid receipt → **Correct invoice**, beside Refund a payment. Staff
  pick dishes (optional at one VAT rate), type the money back with VAT, see the split, pick a reason, give a PIN
  if needed; the corrective prints and the drawer opens. EN/ES guide.
- **Today's plan.** Slice 4: a route called with a submission id, permission and PIN checks, one transaction
  filing the correction and recording the cash refund, a replay record, the drawer job, and an AEAT test-system
  run of the final-cent shape (VAT alone, base €0.00) before it lands (watcher 2026-11-09 19:42, lane L
  `questions.md`); plus whether cash may differ from what was paid "when a fix changed the total". Slice 5: the
  screen, the corrective paper (the corrective's split, not sums of its lines), the guide, the demo-seed choice
  (plan `:620`).

## 4. Open questions (defaults recommended)

1. **Less cash left than the correction.** €10.00 paid in cash, €3.00 given back earlier as goodwill; staff
   correct €10.00. Default: refused, "€7.00 is left to give back in cash"; counting an earlier refund toward a
   correction comes later. Alternative: file €10.00 and give €7.00.
2. **Several cash payments.** €40.00 paid €20.00 + €20.00 cash; correct €25.00. Default: refunds of €20.00 and
   €5.00 under one corrective, drawer opened once. Alternative: refuse unless one payment covers it.
3. **Card and cash.** €30.00 paid €10.00 cash + €20.00 card; correct €5.00. Default: refused in slices 4–5 (the
   owner's cash first, 2026-11-09 16:45). Alternative: give cash when the cash payments cover it.
4. **Slice 5 size.** About 1,260 lines, past "about 1,000". Default: one PR, stop at 1,500. Alternative: 5.1–5.2
   (about 560), then 5.3–5.5 (about 700).
5. **Demo sales** store no line gross, so every one is refused (plan `:620`). Default (b): the seeder stores each
   line's gross and VAT as gross minus base, its golden file re-recorded with the owner's say-so, demo venues
   reset; about 15 lines. Alternative (a): leave them refused.
6. **Slice 4 landing alone,** though staff cannot use it. Default: yes, as slices 1–3 did.
