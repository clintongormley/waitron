**Words.** A **simplified invoice** (type F2) is the ticket a venue gives; a **full invoice** (F1) names the
customer. A **corrective invoice** (type R5, _factura rectificativa_) is a new negative invoice taking value off an
F2; the **rectificative series** is the numbering it takes. **Gross** is a price with VAT; the **split** is the
base (price before VAT) and the VAT at each rate. The **stored figures** are `sales.total`, `sales.vat_breakdown`
and `sale_lines`, written once at sale time. A **fix** (_subsanación_) is a second version of a filing AEAT (the
Spanish tax agency) refused. The **AEAT test system** is its preproduction service; it answers each record
`Correcto` (accepted), `AceptadoConErrores` (accepted with warnings) or `Incorrecto` (refused). Each filed record
carries a **fingerprint** (_huella_) of itself and the one before, forming the **chain**; the **golden** test pins
known fingerprints. A **ledger** table is one whose rows are never changed or deleted, enforced by triggers
(`appendOnly`). A **replay record** makes a request sent twice (a double tap, a lost reply) return the first
answer. A **drawer job** is the queued command that opens the cash drawer.

## 1. Outcome, and what the plans say today

- **Slice 4 (server only; staff see nothing):** in one step the server files a corrective invoice for an amount
  of a paid all-cash invoice, records that cash going back, opens the drawer once, and answers a repeat with the
