# Choose Cloud staff email during test setup

In fresh Prepare and development Live on a configured HTTPS test box, an installer with a provisional Cloud registration can choose **Send email through Waitron Cloud**. Saving a checked choice gets the grant while setup is open. After venue save and restart, the three staff email types arrive via Cloud. Leaving it unticked uses the box's mail server, or the existing local capture in development without SMTP.
