# Put automatically issued bills in the trading day they close

The owner kept one changeover time on 20 November at 13:59. In their example, the Monday trading day ends at 03:00 on Tuesday. Tuesday's automatic close issues Monday's outstanding bills. At about 03:30 the manager can open Monday in **Sales & takings** and see those bills. A new 04:00 sale belongs to Tuesday. The invoice and its operation date keep their existing rules, including the calendar date agreed with lane L for [#3398](https://github.com/clintongormley/waitron/pull/3398).

The daily report is on demand through `apps/server/src/report-api.ts`. Select Monday once the close pass has run. `readDailyClose` sees each bill after its transaction commits, for days without a saved snapshot. A bill waiting for a payment or an unsent dish appears once issued. Nothing prints or emails the daily report at 03:30. Keep the existing close job, report and backup schedule.

## Work and checks

1. In `packages/reporting/src/business-day.ts`, use the existing dated changeovers to build two issued-time ranges. Ordinary sales use the requested range. Sales with source `business_day_close` use the same range shifted by one trading day, so a Tuesday 03:01 close belongs to Monday. Reuse the dated boundaries for the shift. A multi-day catch-up on Thursday would put the bills on Wednesday. The watcher needs to confirm this choice before the build. Both ranges use `sales.issued_at`; verify the index in the box's SQLite. Share the predicate between counted sales and void reversals, so a Tuesday void of a Tuesday 03:01 close bill reverses the Monday sale on Tuesday. This covers close counts, daily and period VAT summaries, top sellers, and category sales and print.
2. In `packages/reporting/src/closed-bills.ts`, use the same shifted range for a zero-total bill that the automatic close issues and settles together. Other closed bills, payments, refunds and deferred closures use their actual event times. The Orders list uses ordering times. The tax-office list, accounting export and VAT return keep their civil or operation dates. Leave issuance and saved daily-close snapshots alone. Past live reports may move these bills between days. Record in the reporting notes that any future frozen close must run after the automatic pass or it can omit these bills.
3. In `apps/server/src/close-of-day-job.test.ts`, first make the real pass fail a report assertion. Check that the Tuesday 03:01 bill appears in Monday's existing report after the pass and the ordinary 04:00 sale appears in Tuesday's. Pin the approved catch-up rule with the existing two-day case.
