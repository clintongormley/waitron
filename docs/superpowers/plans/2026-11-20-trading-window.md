# Put automatically issued bills in the trading day they close

The owner kept one changeover time on 20 November at 13:59. In their example, the Monday trading day ends at 03:00 on Tuesday. Tuesday's automatic close issues Monday's outstanding bills. At about 03:30 the manager can open Monday in **Sales & takings** and see those bills. A new 04:00 sale belongs to Tuesday. The invoice and its operation date keep their existing rules, including the calendar date agreed with lane L for [#3398](https://github.com/clintongormley/waitron/pull/3398).

The report is already available on demand through the daily-close route in `apps/server/src/report-api.ts`. `readDailyClose` reads committed sales for days without a saved snapshot. The close job checks every minute and commits each due bill before a subsequent report can read it. Confirm this sequence in a test. A delayed or refused bill appears once it issues. Keep the existing job and report. There is no scheduled delivery, new report, second setting or change to backup timing.
