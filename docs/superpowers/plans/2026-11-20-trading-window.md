# Count and email the trading day's Sales & takings report

A Monday bill issued by the automatic close at Tuesday's 03:00 changeover, or on Thursday after an outage, counts in Monday's **Sales & takings**. A new Tuesday 04:00 sale belongs to Tuesday. A venue can opt in to email its daily report after the close. The owner asked for the original trading day and optional email on 20 November at 14:43. The watcher kept one changeover.
