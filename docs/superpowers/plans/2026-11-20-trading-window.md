# Count and email the trading day's Sales & takings report

When a Monday table bill is issued by the automatic close at Tuesday's 03:00 changeover, or on Thursday after an outage, Monday's **Sales & takings** includes it. A new Tuesday 04:00 sale belongs to Tuesday. A venue can opt in to email the same daily report to selected staff, around 03:30 in this example, once the close pass has run. The owner asked for the original trading day and optional email on 20 November at 14:43. The watcher kept one changeover in the 13:59 and 14:37 notes. [#3398](https://github.com/clintongormley/waitron/pull/3398) owns invoice operation dates, including Tuesday for the Monday bill.

## 1. Put automatic close bills in the original report

- Use the same trusted opening time and dated changeover boundaries that `closeOfDayEnd` uses in #3398. It only finds bills that have yet to be issued, so read the issued sale's existing working-order link and `trusted_opened_at` in the report. Use bounded searches on `sales.issued_at` for ordinary sales and `working_orders.trusted_opened_at` for automatic close sales, joined through the unique sale order key. Add the trusted-opening index in `packages/db/src/schema/orders.ts`, rebuild the core starting migration and check the emitted query plans on the box's Node/SQLite build. This changes the report selection and requires a venue reset. Wait for #3398 to land before building.
- In `packages/reporting/src/business-day.ts`, use the same sale-day rule in `issuedSalesClause` and `reversedSalesClause`. Their callers cover counts, daily and period VAT, top sellers, and category sales and print. In `closed-bills.ts`, use that day for the zero-total bill the automatic close settles on issue. Later settlements, payments and voids use their event time. Keep the saved close snapshots and the fiscal and accounting periods.
- First fail `apps/server/src/close-of-day-job.test.ts` on a Monday bill issued Thursday missing from Monday's report. Check the normal overnight close and the 04:00 sale alongside it. Use reporting's `daily-close.test.ts`, `closed-bills.test.ts`, `vat-summary-period.test.ts`, `top-sellers.test.ts` and `category-sales.test.ts` to pin the cross-day void and the changed totals. Run the existing #3398 close-date check and the repository's five schema suites. Update the reporting entry in `docs/backlog.md`. Estimate 100 production lines and 160 test lines, plus generated schema and notes.

## 2. Offer one daily email after the close

- Add an off-by-default venue setting and recipient picker under **System → Email**, in `email-settings-api.ts` and `email-settings-panel.ts`, with English and Spanish guide steps. Use the existing settings save and `listActivePersonsWithPermission`. An editor needs box administration and report access. Recipients are selected active staff who can view reports. Recheck the setting and recipient's access at send time.
- Have `close-of-day.ts` call a small `daily-report-mail.ts` after a completed pass, once 30 minutes have elapsed since changeover. On a late start, the most recently ended day can still send after its pass. Earlier catch-up days are available on demand. A clock hold or interrupted pass delays sending. Bills already left open for staff appear in later on-demand reads once issued. Reuse `enqueueMail` and `mail-sender.ts` with one key per trading day and recipient, so restart and repeated passes reuse the same queue entry. Add the mail kind to the core schema. Pull the daily report read out of `report-api.ts` for both the dashboard and a localized email with the same figures. Delivery runs outside close and uses the existing SMTP notice route.
- The existing Cloud relay accepts account messages only (`cloud-email.ts`, `mail-sender.ts`). Recommend SMTP for this slice. If reports must also go through Cloud, agree a separate change to the Cloud service and its box client before claiming that route works.
- First fail `close-of-day-job.test.ts` on the missing queue entry for a completed day. Test one correctly dated message after the close, no entry while disabled or held, and no duplicate after restart in `daily-report-mail.test.ts`. Compare its daily figures with the dashboard reader and check a removed recipient is skipped. Test the settings gate in `email-settings-api.test.ts` and the form in `email-settings-panel.test.ts`. Run the schema checks and the English/Spanish guide and browser checks. Estimate 300 production lines and 240 test lines, plus generated schema and notes.
