# Shared table controls implementation plan

**Outcome.** Click or tap anywhere in a sortable heading or tick-box cell, including the select-all heading. Help and drag grips keep their own targets. Products and the menu editor can both opt into shorter rows when their redesigns land. This is PRODUCTS-LIST-1, piece 1 (§2, §7.2 and Delivery) of the [Products list design](../specs/2026-11-24-products-list-redesign-design.md).

**Scope.** Ship the shared behavior and density option in one change. Products and Menu Structure use the click change through the shared table. Their redesigns will turn on density. No schema changes, venue reset or dependencies.

## Whole-cell targets

- [ ] **Red.** Add “sorts once from heading padding and the keyboard” in `packages/ui/src/components/wt-data-table.head-and-cells.test.ts`. Use `userEvent.click(cell, { position })` at a measured blank point beyond the visible controls, following `wt-data-table.test.ts`. Assert one event per click or key activation and `aria-sort` only on the active heading. Keep the real badge click case in `packages/ui/src/components/wt-data-table.help.test.ts`. Check that the blank heading space still sorts with the badge visible.
- [ ] **Red.** Add “ticks once from cell padding and the keyboard” in the same head-and-cells suite. Cover row and select-all padding in flat and tree tables, Space, and mixed-to-checked select-all. Check one `wt-selection-change` per action, zero row activations, and zero ticks from grip-only padding. Add “ticks from padding beside the grip” to the existing fixtures in `apps/dashboard/src/widgets/product-list.test.ts` and `apps/dashboard/src/widgets/menu-structure-table.test.ts`. Click the padding and real grip separately and check that the grip still works independently.
- [ ] **Green.** In `packages/ui/src/components/wt-data-table.ts`, stretch the native sort button and associated checkbox labels across their cells, with styles in new `packages/ui/src/components/wt-data-table-controls-styles.ts`. Keep the existing sort and change handlers, accessible names, focus path and selection rules. Layer help and grip siblings above the hit areas. Keep the help anchor and `part="tree-heading"` wrapper so the badge and heading text stay aligned. Preserve sticky headings and pinned columns.

## Dense rows

- [ ] **Red.** Add “changes only opted-in body padding at the table's own width” in the head-and-cells suite. Measure wide and narrow flat and tree tables, normal and dense, with real `--wt-tap-min` contents, baseline-aligned text and wrapping. Override the spacing and tap tokens and check computed padding and geometry, including header and column alignment.
- [ ] **Green.** Add `denseRows` / `dense-rows` (default off) in `wt-data-table.ts`. Reuse its width observer for opted-in flat tables and trees. In the sibling stylesheet, start with half `--wt-space-1` per side on wide bodies and half `--wt-space-2` on narrow bodies. Measure against the roughly 48/52 px targets and allow wrapped content to grow. Keep inline padding and the existing default widths, including Departments and Devices.
