2. **Emergency TLS and lease.** Cloud supplies certificate/key files for the operation's exact separately registered emergency hostname, publicly trusted by browsers; Waitron terminates TLS. The code-entry origin stays separate. No issuance endpoint or widening of opt-in, test-only staff-certificate-v1. Cloud's stored operation mapping restricts it to this read-only copy, without changing lease fields or granting sale/fiscal/writer authority. Production qualification remains required; local certificates are fixtures.

3. **Copy status read.** Propose machine-v1 POST `/api/installations/emergency-copy-status`, action `emergency-copy-status`, outer `{payload,signature}`, signed body exactly `{version:1,copyOperationId}`. Proof `operationId` is a fresh request UUID, distinct from the copy UUID. Response has exactly `{version:1,installationId,venueId,environment,sourceInstallationId,sourceNodeId,copyNodeId,copyOperationId,startedAt,expiresAt,asOf,eligibility,sourceLastHeardAt}`. Only the fresh copy installation can read its stored operation; IDs/dates match its binding. `eligibility` is exactly `{status:"ready",open:boolean,closedReason:null|reason}`, with open iff reason is null; reuse part 1’s ordered reasons `setting_off|level_inactive|no_payment_method|not_provisioned`, or exactly `{status:"unavailable",reason:"state_not_ready"}`; known closure wins over unrelated unknowns. `sourceLastHeardAt` is Cloud's observed source heartbeat `UtcInstant`, or explicit unknown `null`. At declaration in part 4, freshly read closed/unknown/unavailable facts refuse; heartbeat age at `asOf` of 0–120 seconds inclusive refuses, and future timestamps are unavailable. No cached authority, sale-path read or declaration endpoint. Reuse stored-key/post-lock revalidation, Origin/selector refusal, errors, shared rate budgets, 60-second proofs, 12,000/86-character payload/signature, 4,096-code-unit lease/body, 16,384-byte request/response, five-second request/eight-second complete-read bounds, no redirects and no-store.

Exhaustion returns 429 `{error:"emergency_hosting_start_limited"}` with `Retry-After` seconds until the next allowance. No owner reset flow, Waitron record or charge before part 4 confirmation.
