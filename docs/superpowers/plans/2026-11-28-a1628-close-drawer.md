# A1628 · Close the drawer

Staff can count the drawer before seeing the expected cash, check the difference, decide what
stays for the next service and see the saved close. This covers frame `cd` panels 2–4 and
[§7.14–15](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md#714-pass-3-clocking-in-and-out-hours-the-cash-drawer-and-a-waiters-float).
A240 waiter floats follow in a later slice.

- **Count.** In `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-close.ts`, use the full-screen dialog, top bar,
  amount block, keypad and foot bar from `till-drawer-cash.ts`. Show the drawer name, step 1 of 2,
  the key reminder, “Count all the cash in the drawer”, “Waitron shows what it expected after you
  save.” and Save the count. Keep first-read retry and automatic opening for a new count.
  A saved count resumes without opening the drawer. Start Count again with the same blank step 1
  keypad and the key instruction, with no new opening. Back returns to the floor and keeps any
  saved count. An empty entry disables save. €0.00 is valid for both counted and left. Reveal the
  comparison after a successful save, and hide it during recount.
- **Review.** Show step 2 of 2, counted and expected cash and “Short by” or “Over by” with a positive
  amount, or “No difference”. Under “Short by”, offer “Count again once if you think you missed some” when the
  optional recount is available. Reuse the existing one-recount rule and the required fresh count
  when withdrawals make the saved one stale. Prefill the editable cash left from `openingCash`,
  show “Same as the last close.” beside that amount while it matches, show available cash minus
  cash left in For the bank, and ask for a smaller amount if the prefill
  exceeds what is available. Keep the sequence check, refusals and retry handling. Use “Finish
  and print the slip” with an active drawer printer, otherwise “Finish close”. Reuse the current
  endpoints and document print job.
- **Drawer closed.** Show the check circle and “Drawer closed”. Show counted cash from the latest
  saved count and take the difference, cash left and bank withdrawal from the finish reply. Show the slip as queued for the named printer,
  unavailable, or already closed with the Sales reprint route. List queued wording as a difference
  from the frame's printed wording at the owner's picture review. In `apps/till/src/till-app.ts`, pass
  the drawer name and use More's working-time availability, active shift and busy state. Back to
  the floor dismisses. Clock out dismisses and calls the existing `#onClockAction` confirmation path.
  Update `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`, both table-service guides and `docs/backlog.md`.
- **Test first.** In `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-close.test.ts`, drive the keypad including
  Enter. Check the blind pending save, €412.30 against €415.00, a recount showing Over by without
  opening the drawer, and a zero/zero close. Check the carry-over, the too-large leave refusal,
  bank preview and saved summary. Preserve the existing observable assertions on permissions,
  recoveries, duplicate submits, stale and foreign counts, late responses, decimals and axe.
  In `apps/till/src/till-app.clock.test.ts`, check Clock out is absent for a clocked-out operator
  and opens the confirmation for a working operator, with no write before confirmation.
  Run both till files and `apps/server/src/drawer-close-api.test.ts` for the existing audit and slip.
  Keep 12 390 px captures of the three screens in English and Spanish in both themes in Lane I’s
  receipts, with frame `cd` beside them. Lane P reviews them before the owner's picture decision.

Estimate 210–330 implementation lines added and 90–160 removed, plus 90–145 test lines added and
25–55 removed. The existing drawer and clock permissions, records and print routes serve this slice.
