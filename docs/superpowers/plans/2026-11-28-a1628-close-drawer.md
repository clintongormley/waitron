# A1628 · Close the drawer

Staff can count the drawer before seeing the expected cash, check the difference, decide what
stays for the next service and see the saved close. This covers frame `cd` panels 2–4 and the
[drawer decisions](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md#714-pass-3-clocking-in-and-out-hours-the-cash-drawer-and-a-waiters-float)
and look in §7.14–15. A240 waiter floats are a later slice.

- **Count.** In `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-close.ts`, compose the full-screen dialog,
  top bar, amount block, keypad and foot bar used by `till-drawer-cash.ts`. Show the drawer name,
  step 1 of 2, the key reminder and Save the count. Keep the first-read retry and the existing
  automatic drawer request after a new read for the assigned drawer. Resume a saved count without
  opening it again. Use an empty input for a new count or recount. Hide the count comparison until
  saving succeeds and while a recount is in progress.
- **Review and finish.** In the same screen, show counted and expected cash and “Short by” or
  “Over by” as appropriate, with Count again and the existing recount eligibility. Keep the
  required fresh count if withdrawals make the saved count stale. Prefill the editable cash to
  leave from `openingCash` and show the bank amount as available cash minus cash left. Keep the
  sequence check, inline amount refusal and retry behavior. Reuse the current save, recount and
  finish endpoints and the existing document print job. Keep the current data and money rules.
- **Drawer closed.** Show the saved count, difference, cash left and bank withdrawal. Describe a
  queued slip as sent to the named drawer printer. Use the reply’s unavailable or already-closed
  state when it sends no new slip. In `apps/till/src/till-app.ts`, pass the resolved drawer name and
  the existing clock-action eligibility to the screen. Back to the floor dismisses it. Clock out
  dismisses it and opens the existing confirmation when the operator can clock out. Update
  `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`, both table-service guides and `docs/backlog.md`.
- **Test first.** In `apps/till/src/screens/till-drawer-close.test.ts`, drive the keypad and assert
  that a pending save hides the expected cash, €412.30 against €415.00 shows “Short by €2.70”,
  and a successful recount above the expected cash shows “Over by”. Check the saved carry-over,
  editable leave amount, bank preview and completed summary with and without Clock out. Keep the
  resume, stale-count, retry and duplicate-submit assertions. Run that file and
  `apps/server/src/drawer-close-api.test.ts` to check the audit and printing paths. Capture the
  matched states at 390 px in English and Spanish in both themes. Lane P reviews the pictures
  before the owner's decision.

Estimate 210–320 implementation lines added and 90–160 removed, plus 65–110 test lines added and
25–55 removed. Use the existing drawer and clock permissions and printing routes.
