# A1308 fk4 — today's map lives on the live table and the zone: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** use superpowers:executing-plans inline. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`)
> syntax and follow [the A1308 slice rules](2026-11-08-a1308-slice-rules.md) ("rules").

**Goal:** `floor_today_tables` (a live table's place, seats, name and "taken off" on today's map)
becomes columns on `dining_tables`, and `floor_today_zones` (each room's business day, revision and
the layout its last reset used) becomes columns on `floor_zones`.

**Architecture:** both tables are unique on their parent (`floor_today_tables_table_key`,
`floor_today_zones_zone_key`), so the parent row holds them. "Has a today's row" becomes
`dining_tables.on_floor = 1`; a CHECK keeps every today column empty while it is 0. "No today's
record" becomes `floor_zones.today_business_day is null`, with `today_revision` defaulting to 0, which
is what an absent row read as (appendix §1.1). Two small helpers in `@waitron/db` keep the today
columns' old field names in one place, so most call sites change only their table.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Drizzle on `node:sqlite`, Vitest, the slice-1a rebuild command, slice
0's bench (periodic, not per slice).

**Spec:** [README](../specs/2026-11-02-rationalisation/README.md) §4 ("a physical table is three rows
with one job each"); [appendix](../specs/2026-11-02-rationalisation/tables-floorkitchen.md) §1.1
(`today_*` on `floor_zones`), §1.3 (the "today" group of `dining_tables`), §5, §8 items 2, 6 (the
today half) and 7.

**First line of the pull request:** "Every venue is reset: core; backups and exports from before
this cannot be restored or imported."
