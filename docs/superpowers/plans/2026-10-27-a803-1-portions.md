   The master plan requires the fiscal controls F to pass byte-unedited. Three F cases build their
   bills through `createOpenOrder` (`apps/server/src/working-order.ts:1517`) with "2 ×" or "3 ×"
   lines and then pin the grouped line shape:

     `working_order_lines` and expects exactly two rows (one dish of quantity 2, one extra).
   - `apps/server/src/split-bill.fiscal.test.ts:194-207` opens a bill with `agua × 3` (line 1) and
     `jamón 0.300` (line 2) through `openPartyTab` (`apps/server/src/testing/serve-line.ts:38`,
     which calls `openTab`, `working-order.ts:1764`, which calls `createOpenOrder`), then splits
     `{lineNo: 1, quantity: "1"}, {lineNo: 2}` and later `{lineNo: 1}` again. With agua expanded,
     line 2 is an agua and line 1 has already left.
   - `apps/server/src/bill-payments.test.ts:605-629` pays `{lineNo: 1, quantity: "2"}` of
     `caña × 3` and expects the line left at quantity 2000.

   Predicted from reading, not run. **Decision D1:** only the table-service writers become portion
   writers. `createOpenOrder` keeps writing grouped lines for walk-up sales
   (`apps/server/src/till-sale.ts:734`, `:1272`) and for `openTab` with lines, which only the test
   helper `openPartyTab` uses (the product's caller, `apps/server/src/parties.ts:112-115`, passes no

   `till-api.fiscal-sale-paths.test.ts:852-912` parks `quantity: "2"` (`:884`) and pins
   `{ items: 1, fired: 1 }` kitchen ticket items after payment (`:902`, `:908`); as portions there
   would be two. Expanding counter orders is an owner decision, recorded in Task 33's backlog
   residual.

2. **No fiscal-core change is needed, as far as reading shows.** VAT is rounded once per filed row
   (`packages/catalogue/src/pricing.ts:179-205`: `baseFromGross` per line, tax by difference), so
   filing each portion as its own row would change VAT on cheap items. The plan regroups portions
   into today's rows before `grossLockedLines` (`pricing.ts:236`), inside the stored-line readers
   (`working-order.ts:1271-1344`), and links sale rows to portions after `recordSale` returns.
   `pricing.ts`, `packages/core/src/record-sale.ts`, `computeHuella`, the chain, invoice
   allocation, `registros_facturacion`, `recordCorrection`, `recordSubstitution`, `registerSif` and
   `restoreFiscal` are untouched. Tasks 2, 3 and 6 are the proof; until they pass this is a claim
   from reading.

3. **Bill discounts spread differently over portions, which changes filed amounts and VAT.**
   `spreadBillDiscount` (`packages/adjustments/src/spread.ts:308`) shares a discount by line, so
   splitting one line into three moves cents between lines and between VAT rates. Run during
   planning, with lines shaped as `planAdjustment` builds them (`exact` per
   `apps/server/src/adjustments-apply.ts:461`), each case taking €1.00 off:
   - 3 × €3.33 drinks plus 1 × €5.00 dish. Grouped: drinks −0.67 (rows `2@3.11, 1@3.10`), dish −0.33
     (`1@4.67`). As four portion lines: drinks −0.22 each (all `@3.11`, −0.66), dish −0.34 (`1@4.66`).
   - 3 × €9.50 burger with €1.00 cheese, plus €2.00 water. Grouped: burgers `3@9.22`, cheese
     `3@0.97`, water `1@1.93`. As portion lines: burgers `9.21, 9.22, 9.22`, cheese `0.97` each,
     water `1@1.94`.

   So Task 6 makes `planAdjustment` spread a whole-bill discount over the same groups the readers
   file, then hands the group's rows to its portions in order. That reproduces today for **one**
   discount but, as far as the planning run shows, **not for two in turn**, so the slice is expected
   to park at Task 6 for an owner decision:
   - Today a two-price result splits the line, and the split row takes the bill's next free line
     number (`working-order.ts:4531-4540`, via `splitLinesWithinOrder` from `reprice`,
     `adjustments-apply.ts:835-845`); a later discount breaks equal remainders by line number
     (`addedOrder: row.lineNo`, `adjustments-apply.ts:382`; "`addedOrder` decides a tie between equal
     remainders, lower first: the line's `line_no`", `spread.ts:34`). A group sits at its first member's position instead, so after one split
     it can win a tie that today's split row loses.
   - Run during planning through the real `spreadBillDiscount`, simulating both line-number rules:
     bill 2 × €2.00 (line 1), 2 × €1.00 (line 2), 1 × €1.50 (line 3); €0.59 off, then €0.62 off.
     Today: `L1 1@1.68, L4 1@1.68, L2 1@0.84, L5 1@0.83, L3 1@1.26`. As portion groups:
     `1.68, 1.67, 0.84, 0.84, 1.26`. Same total (6.29), different filed rows: one cent moves from
     the €2.00 dish to the €1.00 dish, which changes VAT where their rates differ.
   - The round-3 review reports the same rule over 40,000 random bills without extras: one discount,
     0 differences in 20,000 (an ungrouped control differed in 10,379, so the probe can fail); two
     discounts, 188 differences in 20,000. That run is the reviewer's, not repeated here.
   I found no tie order, built only from what the portion rows hold, that gives today's numbers:
   today's number for a split row depends on how many lines the grouped bill had when the split
   happened, which the portion bill does not record. That is a judgment from reading, not a proof;
   Task 6 runs the case, states the stop and the options (D29).

## Decisions

- **D1** Table-service writers only; walk-ups, `openTab` with lines and parked counter orders are
  all held grouped by F (finding 1).
- **D2** A portion is allocated when a working line is written, never at draft save; draft lines
  keep a quantity (`orderDraftLines.quantity`, `apps/server/src/order-drafts.ts:1753`). A lost-send
  replay writes no line, so it allocates nothing. **One exception:** raising a recalled line writes
  working lines at draft save today (`apps/server/src/draft-recall.ts:129-149`), so the added
  portions are written there too (D6, Task 16).
- **D3** Expansion splits the priced rows before they are inserted (a pure `expandPortionRows`),
  then `registerPortions` writes the registry and bindings in the same transaction. This departs
  from the master plan's proposed `expandPortions(tx, orderId, lines, soldByUnitLineIds)`, which
  splits inserted lines: before the insert no ticket item, context or allergy tick exists yet, and
  the existing in-database unit split refuses a dish with extras unless `splitExtras` is passed
  (`working-order.ts:4484-4485`).
- **D4** The first portion keeps the priced row's id, so `priceOrderLines`' identities
  (`working-order.ts:1044-1049`) stay real line ids for `issuancePass`
  (`apps/server/src/issuance-pass.ts:30-35`, contexts by line id).
- **D5** Only dish lines are bound. An extra belongs to its dish's portion through
  `parent_line_id`; bindings are unique by line and by portion.
- **D6** A pricing cohort is the set of portions expanded from one priced request line. A rise
  that today raises the stored row in place (`applyLineEdits` action `"free"`,
  `working-order.ts:5976-5987`; `saveRecalledLine`, `draft-recall.ts:129-149`) adds portions to the
  same cohort, copying the stored row, so the filed row equals today's raised row. A rise that
  today inserts a separately priced row (`"raise"`, `"change"`, adjusted lines,
  `working-order.ts:5720-5722, 5750-5771`) starts a new cohort. Whether a rise expands follows the
  **line**: a bound line always rises by new portions, whichever order it is on (D30).
- **D7** Readers group bound dish lines by cohort plus every `LockedLine` field except `quantity`
  and `parentLineNo`, plus product id, classification and list price; their extras must be equal
  as a set, each extra compared on the same fields (price and list price included) and its product
  id. Quantities add. Unbound lines stay one row each. A group sits at its first member's
  position; its identity id is that member's line id, and it carries every member's line id and
  portion id.
- **D8** The comparison is on the multiset of filed rows (composition, quantity, gross, base, VAT
  rate, list gross), parent–extra pairing, total and VAT breakdown. Row order is not compared:
  today's order after a split is an artifact of the split row taking `max(line_no) + 1`
  (`working-order.ts:4531-4540`).
- **D9** Moving portions out and back regroups them into their cohort's original row. Today a
  carved-off part stays a separate row. Recorded for the owner.
- **D10** Each filed dish and extra row is linked to every portion it represents, inside the
  issuance transaction straight after `recordSale`, by `(sale_id, line_no = position + 1)`.
  `saleLineRows` copies each line's `lineNo` (`packages/core/src/sale-line-rows.ts:18-22`) and
  `grossRows` numbers lines `i + 1` (`pricing.ts:145-146`); Task 5 tests that `recordSale` keeps
  that order (an assumption until then).
- **D11** No new remainder-cent allocator for pricing. A dish's gross is `unit × quantity` with no
  price quantity (`pricing.ts:162-164`; `grossBasketWithOptions` sets none on a dish, `:301-317`),
  and an extra's quantity is a whole multiple of its price quantity (`modifier-selection.ts:155-156`),
  so a portion's gross is exact in cents. Task 2 checks it. Discount cents are a different matter
  (finding 3, D29).
- **D12** The partial-quantity server paths stay (partial transfer and split in `carveLines`,
  `splitUnitsWithinOrder`, partial serve, cancel, adjustment and item payment). They still serve
  measured lines and grouped counter lines. Their tests keep their assertions; where a test's writer
  now expands, the test builds the grouped shape with `priceTabRound` + `insertTabRound` and no
  expansion, as `apps/server/src/testing/kitchen-table-order.ts:23-24` already does. **Not** in
  files that pin discount, receipt, refund or item-payment amounts (listed under Changed test
  checks): there only a request's target may change, to name the same dish's portion, and a changed
  amount is a stop.
- **D13** Refusing a partial cut of a measured line (spec §3.1, "never cut") waits for slice 4's
  picker.
- **D14** The till groups identical portions in identical state into one row (`state/portion-groups.ts`).
  An action whose request takes a list sends the member ids for a count: serve (`items[]`),
  Transfer items and Split bill (`transfers[]`). Cancel and the adjustment dialog take one line,
  so they act on one portion — the group's first member by line number — and the "all n" choice
  goes for grouped rows until slice 4.
- **D15** The Pay items list (`apps/till/src/state/bill-payment.ts:345-362`,
  `widgets/bill-pay-dialog.ts:1579-1648`) shows one row per portion only while A739 Cut B holds
  those files; once released, Task 10 groups it with a units stepper sending member line numbers.
- **D16** MOVED slips stay one row per line: `printMovedSlips` lays out each line alone because "a
  link … loses its reprint mark, so which ticket last carried a line cannot be told"
  (`apps/server/src/kitchen-print.ts:1586-1590`). Kitchen screens, the expo screen and the
  dashboard's order detail group identical portions (spec §3.1), Tasks 12–13.
- **D17** Receipts and the till's sale view group filed rows that print identically and share
  unit price, VAT rate and list price and carry no adjustment entry, summing filed amounts. The
  full invoice's line table is unchanged.
- **D18** The till's `soldByTheUnit` (`apps/till/src/widgets/product-name.ts:85-88`) stays for the
  weighing view, steppers, price wording and late-send. The split consumers switch to the existing
  `takesExtras` (`apps/till/src/state/order-line.ts:69-71`, adds `measure !== true`), so a 200 ml
  line is never offered "split into 200".
- **D19** Events: `moved` for each bound dish line whose order changes in `moveOrderLines`
  (`working-order.ts:3877-3886`); `cancelled` when a bound dish line is deleted with its charge
  (`removeFromLine`, `working-order.ts:3084-3093`; `applyLineEdits` removal, `:6213-6225`). A
  keep-charge cancellation records none. `actor_id` is nullable: `moveOrderLines` takes no operator
  (`:3820-3826`), so it gains an optional trailing `{ actorId }`, which its three product callers
  fill from the operator they already hold (`bill-actions.ts:260` from `known.operatorId`;
  `order-drafts.ts:228`; `carveLines` `:4527` through a new `opts.actorId` that `splitBill` and
  `transferItems` pass from their command).
- **D20** Allergy ticks reach every portion of the dish they were on: a draft line's tick moves to
  the first portion (`moveAllergyDraftTicks`, `packages/venue-service/src/party-allergy.ts:250-272`)
  and is copied to the others with `setAllergyLineTicks`, as a split already does
  (`apps/server/src/draft-source.ts:89-94`); a direct send's patch targets every portion of its
  input line (`apps/server/src/order-groups.ts:215-222`).
- **D21** The four tables go in the core migration set: `working_order_lines` and `sale_lines` are
  core, and both counter and table service share permanent order identity
  (`docs/developers/conventions-data.md:365-372` requires the commit to say so). Registry, sale links
  and events are append-only `ledger`, as the master plan's contract says. Their order-history
  siblings `order_amendments`, `order_group_events` and `order_draft_events` are append-only
  `state` (`packages/db/src/classification.ts:153-157, 195-199, 220-224`); the portion tables are
  `ledger` like `unpaid_departures` (`classification.ts:78-82`, keyed to `working_orders` at
  `packages/db/src/schema/unpaid-departures.ts:40-44`), because filed sale rows point at them and the
  pre-bill trail must come back with those sales from a returned box. Bindings are `state`.
- **D22** Guide Spanish ships only with owner approval, given at the slice's land sign-off.
- **D23** The split help "Dishes with options must be moved in full" (`table.split_options_together`,
  `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts:1260`, shown at `till-table-order-screen.ts:5269`) shows only while
  the bill holds a dish of quantity above 1 with extras — a grouped line, which a counter order moved
  to a table still brings (`apps/server/src/move-bill.ts:58, 128-136`) and which `carveLines` still
  refuses to part (`working-order.ts:4484-4485`). `table.split_modifier_error` (`:1261`) stays.
- **D24** `scripts/append-only-triggers.test.ts` `EXPECTED` is a whole-set pin; adding the three new
  names is allowed by the owner's whole-shape rule (the A795 plan files the same kind of addition
  under "Allowed by the rule",
  `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-a795-aeat-certificate-after-setup.md:254-270`).
  `scripts/schema-constraints.test.ts` is not a whole-shape pin: its lists are "checked one way
  only … a constraint nobody listed passes unseen" (`:13-15`). Adding the new keys and unique indexes
  there only makes the guard stricter, so a regeneration that drops one fails; no existing entry
  changes. Both additions are named in the PR.
- **D25** Bindings do **not** cascade on line delete. A drizzle rebuild of `working_order_lines`
  (two so far: `packages/db/drizzle/0026_line_vat_class.sql`, `0092_positive_price_quantity.sql`)
  runs with foreign keys on, so a cascading child loses every row silently (CLAUDE.md §3); with no
  action, the rebuild fails loudly instead. Stated at the column. Product code deletes the binding
  before deleting a bound dish line, at the only two dish-deleting sites
  (`working-order.ts:3086-3094`, `:6213-6225`; the other line deletes, `:5989` and
  `draft-recall.ts:169`, remove extras only, which are never bound).
- **D26** `addTabRound` stays grouped. Outside `working-order.ts` only test helpers call it
  (`rg -n 'addTabRound\(' apps/server/src -g '!*.test.ts'` lists its definition at `:2867`,
  `testing/party-venue.ts:242` and `testing/order-writer-fixture.ts:171`), and 26 test files order
  more than one unit through it. It is a fixture writer of grouped table lines from here on; its
  doc comment says so.
- **D27** A weighed or measured line never merges with another in a draft (spec §3.1: "'100 g
  jamón' ordered twice is two lines"). Today the shared merge rule exempts only fractional
  quantities (`packages/shared/src/draft-merge.ts:31-40`), so two "200 ml" adds become one line;
  both the till (`apps/till/src/state/draft-lines.ts:74-77`) and the server
  (`order-drafts.ts:24, 638, 1139`) apply it.
- **D28** Kitchen correction slips (HOLD CHANGED, HOLD CANCELLED, ALLERGY CHANGED/REMOVED), today one
  slip per line (`kitchen-print.ts:1227-1266`), print one slip per group of identical portions at a
  station with the same correction, whatever the station's grouping — as a grouped line of 12 prints
  one slip today.
- **D29** Only a **whole-bill** discount uses groups: `planAdjustment` spreads it over groups of bound
  lines formed as D7 forms them, each group's tie order being its first member's line number, then
  gives each group's rows to its portions in ordinal order: the first k portions take the
  higher-priced row ("the higher price first", `spread.ts:52-55`), the rest the other; each extra's
  group price goes to every member's extra. A **line** target spreads over that portion's own dish
  and its extras only, never its group (`family`, `adjustments-apply.ts:478`), so Task 3's
  one-portion discount is today's carved part. A bound portion is never carved. This is exact for
  one discount and not, as far as planning shows, for two in turn (finding 3); Task 6's stop decides
  between: (a) accept that a later discount can place a cent differently between dishes, totals
  unchanged; (b) keep today's line numbering for each group as a stored state column, kept by every
  write that today allocates a line number (inserts, price splits, moves), and use it as the tie
  order; (c) per-portion fiscal rows, the master plan's unresolved alternative.
- **D30** A line that is bound rises by new portions on any order (a party bill moved to the
  counter, `move-bill.ts:58`, keeps its bound lines); an unbound line rises in place as today. Fresh
  and replacement lines on a party bill (`updateHeldOrder`'s new-line path,
  `working-order.ts:6445-6466`) expand; on a counter order they do not.
