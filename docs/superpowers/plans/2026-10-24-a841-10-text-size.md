# A841 slice 10 — text size: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A manager moves a **Text size** slider on the Look card, from 100% to 130% in 5% steps,
sees the four text sizes it gives and a till button and bill line in the preview at that size, and
on saving every dashboard and till draws its text that much bigger. The equipment label, the
recovery sheet and Google's sign-in button keep Waitron's sizes. On the tills, long product names
break at a syllable with a hyphen where the venue's default content language allows it, and layout
cases at 130% hold the till's and the dashboard's screens to "nothing cut off".

**Architecture:** Two pull requests (D1). **10a, the theme and the editor:** `textScale` joins the
theme document (absent reads 100); `deriveTheme` writes the four size tokens after it settles the
colours, so the stored stylesheet carries them and the delivery slice 3 built takes them to every
screen with no new request; Google's button reads two new tokens of its own; the equipment label
and the recovery sheet take their type from Waitron's own token file instead of the page;
`wt-slider` gains a value format, a spoken value, a hint and step marks; the Look card gains the
slider, the line of sizes, the preview's till button and bill line, the saved card's row, and the
switch dialog's "Text on the tills changes size too.". **10b, the tills at 130%:** `lang` and
`hyphens: auto` on the till's product button names and bill line names, and the layout cases at
130% in the till's and the dashboard's suites, the hyphenation case among them.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Lit 3, Vitest 4.1.x in real headless Chromium (`packages/ui-core`,
`packages/ui`, `apps/dashboard`, `apps/till`), Node test runs for `apps/server` and
`packages/layouts`, Markdown guides served by `apps/server`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-a841-theme-editor-design.md` — section 16 item 10
(lines 2593-2605) is the scope. Detail: section 4 step 1 and step 2 and the product-text table

## Decisions this plan makes (the design or the brief left them open)

**D1. Two pull requests.** The work is 14 implementer tasks and two visual checks, past the brief's
twelve. **10a** (Tasks A1-A10, visual check A11) stores, derives, delivers and edits the text size
and keeps the fixed-type surfaces on Waitron's type; main is whole after it — every screen draws the
saved size, and long names break as they do today (mid-word, from 115%, design lines 803-805).
**10b** (Tasks B1-B4, visual check B5) adds the till's hyphenation and every layout case at 130%.
Before go-live any venue may be reset, so the window between the two (a theme saved at 115% or more
breaking long names mid-word) costs nothing a reset does not fix.

**D2. Where `textScale` lives and what it accepts.** A top-level key of the document, beside
`corners`, shared by both versions (design line 1302: type has no light or dark). Allowed values:
the numbers `100, 105, 110, 115, 120, 125, 130` and nothing else (`TEXT_SCALES`). Required in the
type (`readonly textScale: TextScale`), as slice 7 made `logo` required while an absent key reads as
`null`: an absent `textScale` reads as 100, so every stored theme and every configuration copy made
before this slice stays valid and the bundle format does not move (the recorded rule,
`docs/backlog.md:2896-2898`; the reasoning of slice 7's D10 applies unchanged, because the import
check runs `readThemeDocument` over each row, `theme-configuration.ts:54`). Anything else present —
`112`, `"115"`, `null`, `95`, `135` — is `theme.invalid { field: "textScale", reason: "bad_value" }`
on save, `setup.request_invalid { field: "custom_themes.theme" }` on import, and an unreadable
stored document (served as Waitron's, `venue-theme.ts:54`). It is checked **last** in the fixed
order, after the logo, so every existing first-refusal case keeps its answer. A newer box's copy
carrying `textScale` is refused by an older box as an unknown key; copies only go forward between
releases, as they did for `logo`.

**D3. The four sizes are written after settling, not inside it.** `deriveThemeFrom` adds
`fontSizes(choices.textScale, defaults)` to each mode's settled tokens. Inside `settleMode` they
would be remembered under a `settleKey` that has no text size (`derive.ts:420-431`), so a derive at
130% followed by one at 100% with the same colours would answer 130%'s sizes from the memo; adding
the text size to the key instead would split a colour cache that the size never affects.
`ModeResult.tokens`' comment (`derive.ts:350`) narrows to "every colour and radius token".

**D4. Rounding.** Drawn size = Waitron's size × step, rounded half up to a whole pixel, computed in
whole numbers: `Math.floor((px * textScale + 50) / 100)`, never through `px * 1.15`. Waitron's size
is read from `WAITRON_DEFAULTS.structure` and must be `<digits>px` (anything else throws a
`RangeError` naming the token). Every step, small · normal · large · largest:

| Step | sm | md | lg | xl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 12 | 14 | 18 | 22 |
| 105 | 13 | 15 | 19 | 23 |
| 110 | 13 | 15 | 20 | 24 |
| 115 | 14 | 16 | 21 | 25 |
| 120 | 14 | 17 | 22 | 26 |
| 125 | 15 | 18 | 23 | 28 |
| 130 | 16 | 18 | 23 | 29 |

(115% matches the design's line, `small 14 px · normal 16 px · large 21 px · largest 25 px`, line
751; 130% the design's limits 16, 18, 23, 29, line 971; 120% picture 25's line, 14, 17, 22, 26.
125% is where half-up matters: 17.5, 22.5 and 27.5 go up.) The order of `THEME_TOKENS` becomes the
22 colours, the three radii, then `--wt-font-size-sm`, `-md`, `-lg`, `-xl`; both versions carry the
same four values, so every theme keeps one shape and the list stays the allowlist (design lines
1033-1035).

**D5. Delivery needs nothing new.** The four sizes are in `/api/theme`'s `css`, so its hash moves
when a theme's text size changes (`venue-theme.ts:21-23,57-58`), a signed-in till follows within a
poll, an open dashboard at once, a kitchen screen in device mode at its next start (already listed,
`conventions-ui.md:1575-1578`). The sheet sits inside `@media screen` (`sheet.ts:24`), so a page the
browser prints from the app takes Waitron's sizes. One effect to state in the PR: every themed
venue's sheet text gains the four lines, so each hash moves once on upgrade and each till fetches
the sheet once.

**D6. The equipment label and the recovery sheet take Waitron's type from `WAITRON_DEFAULTS.structure`**
(`@waitron/ui-core/theme/waitron`, already imported by the dashboard, `look-card.ts:38`), not from the
page: the label all four of its tokens, the recovery sheet `--wt-font-family`,
`--wt-font-family-mono`, `--wt-font-size-md` and `--wt-font-size-xl`; the recovery sheet keeps copying
its spacing, colours and radius from the page, as design section 8 leaves it ("Partly", line 1398).
Not taken: a theme-sheet selector that skips print frames (design line 1399's example) — the
recovery sheet's source is the panel itself, not a frame, so a selector would cover the label only;
reading Waitron's file covers both, and slice 11's typeface and weights with them.

**D7. Google's button.** `--wt-google-button-font-size: 14px` and `--wt-google-button-font-weight: 500`
join `structure.css` beside `--wt-google-button-line-height` (`:33`), outside `THEME_TOKENS`;
`login-screen.ts:163-164` reads them.

**D8. `wt-slider`'s additions, all opt-in, no new event.**
- `format` (property only): `(value: number) => string`, default `String`; draws the `value` part.
- `valueText` (property only): `((value: number) => string) | null`, default `null`; when set, the
  range input carries `aria-valuetext` of the shown value, so a screen reader hears "115 percent".
- `hint` (attribute): text under the control, in `--wt-color-text-muted` at `--wt-font-size-sm`,
  `part="hint"`, with an id; the input's `aria-describedby` lists the hint's id then the error's.
- `marks` (boolean attribute): draws one mark per step under the range (`part="marks"`, each a
  `part="mark"`, hidden from assistive technology) and moves the `value` part from the label row to
  the end of the range's row. The marks row is inset by half the native thumb's width,
  `calc(var(--wt-space-4) / 2)`: Chromium 156 draws the thumb 16 px wide, its centre 8 px in from
  each end at min and max (probe P2).
Without these, the slider draws exactly as today, so the Menus screen, the photo zoom and the demo
are unchanged. `wt-change` keeps `{ value: number }`, `bubbles: true, composed: true`.

**D9. The Look card's slider updates the draft on release or a keyboard step** (not `continuous`):
each change derives the whole theme again (`look-card.ts:320-326`); during a drag the slider's own
number follows the pointer (`wt-slider.ts:71-79`).

**D10. Words.** The visible value is the screen language's percent, `Intl.NumberFormat(locale,
{ style: "percent" })` ("115%" in English, "115 %" in Spanish); the spoken value, the hint, the line
of sizes and the saved row come from the strings. "px" is joined to its number by a no-break space
(` px`, ` px` in the source), as the round-7 pictures were redrawn for (design lines 2794-2795).
New keys, English / Spanish:

| Key | English | Spanish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `appearance.text_size` | Text size | Tamaño del texto |
| `appearance.text_size_hint` | 100% is Waitron's standard size. Larger text wraps long names onto more lines. | El 100 % es el tamaño estándar de Waitron. Con texto más grande, los nombres largos ocupan más líneas. |
| `appearance.text_size_spoken` | {n} percent | {n} por ciento |
| `appearance.text_sizes` | Text on screens: small {sm} px · normal {md} px · large {lg} px · largest {xl} px | Texto en pantalla: pequeño {sm} px · normal {md} px · grande {lg} px · muy grande {xl} px |
| `appearance.text_size_standard` | {size} (standard) | {size} (estándar) |

(`{size}` is the formatted percent. In `text_sizes` each space before "px" is a no-break space.)
