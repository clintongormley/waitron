# A1587. Name the emailed invoice and say what it contains

After this lands, customers can identify an emailed invoice or receipt from the name of its PDF and read the amount and the business name in the message. An original and a copy both use `FF-0EBGA-4.pdf` for invoice `FF-0EBGA/4`. The copy already has a duplicate mark in the PDF and keeps a translated duplicate mark in the message.
