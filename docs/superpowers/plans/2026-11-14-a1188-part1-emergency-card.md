## Agreed contract values

1. Code: exactly six hyphen-separated groups of four uppercase symbols from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`, 29 ASCII characters. Accept `ABCD-EFGH-JKLM-NPQR-STUV-WXYZ` and `2345-6789-ABCD-EFGH-JKLM-NPQR`; the wire reader rejects lowercase, O/I/0/1, missing groups and spaces. Human entry normalization is Cloud’s; do not constrain its page here.
2. Additional code-read cap: 6/authenticated installation/60 seconds, using Cloud limiter semantics; inherited 30/installation, 60 machine/IP and general 120/IP per minute and display 1/minute stay unchanged. Return 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After`.
3. Exact 403 `emergency_hosting_closed` plus reason `setting_off|level_inactive|no_payment_method|not_provisioned` maps to `cloud.emergency_hosting_closed`; exact 403 `emergency_hosting_installation_ineligible` maps to `cloud.unavailable`, without revocation. Actual 403 `revoked` retires registration. Keep machine-v1 encoding/body unchanged; add `emergency-hosting-display` and `emergency-hosting-code` in slice 1a to `packages/contract/src/signing/installation-proof.ts` and its test. Follow `docs/developers/shared-ui-preparation.md`: normal publisher, installed-consumer qualification and exact version/source/integrity handoff to Cloud before its adapters build; no manual publishing. The box encoder already accepts action strings; it need not await this verifier release. After merge, the driver records its published version/source/integrity for Cloud.
4. Published `hostedMonthlyCents/hostedDayDivisor` (30), rounded once half-up to whole cents using exact integer/Decimal arithmetic: 9000→300, 10000→333, 10005→334, 14→0, 15→1. Use exactly “About {amount} per day, excluding VAT ({vat})” / “Aproximadamente {amount} al día, sin IVA ({vat})”; reuse the billing money/VAT formatter, with one percent sign. Unpublished prices affect only the price line; retain the other facts. Change no invoice calculation.
