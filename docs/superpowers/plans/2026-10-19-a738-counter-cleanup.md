## Review Focus

1. Same product under two menus, hidden sections and searches: filters must use the right offer, leave unfiltered indexes intact, and never change an existing order (Task 1).
2. A long label, many counts, narrow viewport or reduced motion: top actions remain reachable, attention never depends on animation, and the last product row is not covered (Tasks 2, 6).
3. An order changes while its preview/window is open: names/extras/notes stay with their actual order/line; actions neither toggle the row nor target a replacement (Tasks 3–5).
4. Unknown cereal/nut kinds and null/empty diet data: retain each sentinel's meaning, conservatively exclude every selected kind for a declared but unspecified cereal/nut kind, and make no allergy-safety claim (Task 1).
5. A recalled/sent line, required extra or fractional unit: no accidental reprice, invalid removal, new sent edit or quantity rounding; A773 ownership and save/leave behavior survive (Tasks 4–7).
