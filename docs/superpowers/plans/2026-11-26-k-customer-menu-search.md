# Lane K · Customer menu search and section links

[Delivery 3, section 3](../specs/2026-11-24-customer-menu-design.md#3-the-customers-page). Builds on #4440, including the owner’s 20:20 allergen wording. Claude reviewed the plan on 26 November. The watcher cleared the plan at 20:44 on 26 November, with the placement and scrolling changes below.

In **Customer menu**, staff can find a dish by name, jump to a top-level section and see which section they are reading in either preview size. Keep the published preview read and its permission. Filters, details, Online settings, public routing, print and styles follow separately. The preview’s language picker stands in for the page’s own language choice until the page is public.

1. **Search.** In `apps/dashboard/src/widgets/customer-menu-page.ts`, use `wt-input` with `type="search"` and consume its `wt-change` event. Below 640 px put the compact field on its own row under the menu name. At 640 px and above put it at the end of the name row. Match a trimmed, case-insensitive substring of the displayed dish or set-menu name. Use the existing tree walk, keeping matches in menu order and dropping empty sections. Clearing restores the menu. Keep focus in the field. The query resets when changing language or loading a new edition, when the preview page is replaced. Keep the allergen advice below every result. Add “Search dishes”, “No dishes found” (as a status) and “Menu sections” to `packages/shared/src/i18n/guest-menu.ts` in EN, ES, CA and GL. Apply the wording bundle’s language to that copy.
2. **Section links.** In the page widget, render a sticky horizontal strip under the search and above the menu contents. Style its links like tabs, with primary text and an underline on the current one. Keep that link in view by scrolling only the strip. Use original positions to distinguish duplicate names. Skip the strip when there are no section links. In `menu-online-preview.ts`, remove canvas overflow where the preview fits. When the Computer canvas is under its 720 px minimum content width, cap its height to the dashboard content area so both axes scroll there and the region remains keyboard reachable. Find the scroll area through the composed ancestors, falling back to the document. Bring the linked heading below the strip, focus it without a second scroll and keep the dashboard address. As people scroll, mark the section they are reading with `aria-current="location"`. Keep the last section marked when the footer scrolls into view.
3. **Test first.** In `apps/dashboard/src/screens/menus-screen.customer-preview.test.ts`, cover nested matches, set-menu names, misses, clearing, advice and language change. Use a bounded scrolling parent like the dashboard. Test Phone and Computer at 390 px and Computer at 1200 px. Prove keyboard activation moves the correct scroller and focuses the visible heading, then scrolling back updates the sticky strip. Use duplicate names and a short final section. At 390 px use more links than fit and check that the current link is inside the strip. Check search results and no results with axe in both page themes. Run `pnpm --filter @waitron/dashboard exec vitest run src/screens/menus-screen.customer-preview.test.ts` for the failing assertions first, then the passing suite.

Update both Products guides at `{#customer-menu}`, the preview paragraph in `docs/developers/design-system.md` and the remaining work in `docs/backlog.md`. Check 360 px. Send full-size 390, 1280 and 1440 px pictures to the watcher for the owner before landing, in EN/ES, both dashboard themes and the preview’s light and dark palettes, with and without photos. Include search and a scrolled section. Estimate **150–200 implementation lines and 150–200 test lines**.
