- `wt-top-bar` has a centred title with optional subtitle, an optional Back button whose label names the previous screen, and an optional slot for the 44 px device or menu control. Long Back labels truncate at 390 px. A title slot lets the order screen supply a button with its chevron. Reuse `wt-button` and `wt-icon`, registering `chevron-left` once in `ui`. - `wt-foot-button-bar` draws the top border and lays out existing `wt-button` actions in a stack or a row. Set their 52 px minimum with the existing `--wt-tap-min` on the slotted action hosts. Row buttons size to their labels and the caller can give the primary action the free space. Callers choose action variants. A separate slot puts a text action below the stack. Keep the bar outside the scrolling body.
