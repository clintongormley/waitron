## Survey and chosen boundary

Under `apps/server/src/`, `till-api.ts` and its mounted children cover session/order/bill/payment/receipt, table/floor/station, adjustment/refund, clock and attendance changes. `schedule-api.ts` and `device-api.ts` mount separately; device levers and station routes also accept device-only proof. Sign-in needs a device cookie; sign-out reads the session cookie directly. These writes use `/api/*`.
The optional device capability reader in `orders-api.ts` is under management authentication. Stripe webhooks and print agents use separate paths with machine credentials. No till/device credential consumer was found in the separate waiting/recovery/first-setup apps; widening the existing check also reaches the waiting app through `pageCheck`, without a new mount.
**Source survey, not an exploit receipt:** signed-in paths require the person header (`till-session.ts:106–109`); CORS controls its preflight (`cors.ts:15–23`). Battery PUT, sign-out DELETE and signed-in schedule calls are therefore mount coverage, not plain-form attack demonstrations. Device-only POST candidates are sign-in (also needs person id/PIN), Ready (`device-levers.ts:57,74`), advance, acknowledge and KDS pass release (`device-api.ts`). Ready needs no body. No old-version browser attack is claimed; the fixed-version Chromium observation is recorded in step 4.
Keep `requireManagementChangeFromOwnPage(trusted: OriginAllowlist, log: Logger): MiddlewareHandler` at its existing `*` mount. Select `management.origin_refused` for a present management cookie, otherwise `till.origin_refused` for a present till/device cookie, including empty values and values with the wrong credential shape accepted by the authentication cookie readers; no credential lookup or body read. Reuse the current private page policy, safe methods, logging and absent-header tool handling. A required trusted-origin lookup failure refuses the change.
