## Review focus

Tests below must distinguish missing kinds from malformed kinds, cereals from nuts, and an absent `kinds` property from an invalid one on another code. They must also catch parser loss of kinds, order-dependent derivation, picker reseeding/lost kinds, and a reader accidentally using its parent or an extra. These are future checks, not results of this planning session.
