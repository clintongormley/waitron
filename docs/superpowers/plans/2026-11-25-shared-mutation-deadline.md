# Balance Shared mutation jobs by measured cost

Shared pull requests need all four mutation reports inside their existing ten-minute job limits so CI can check the combined 90% score. This is Lane F's `SHARED-MUTATION-DEADLINE` queue item.

Two GitHub-hosted jobs ran `set-menu-placement.ts` and `set-menu-search.ts` together. The first finished mutation testing in 9m27s, uploaded its report and was cancelled at 10m02s. The second was cut off mid-mutation, and the retry of that job also reached the deadline. In the completed comparison, placement and search weighed 414.1 and 258.1 recorded test-runner seconds. Eight slow mutations marked `Timeout` accounted for about 520 of the two jobs' 973 recorded seconds. One hit Stryker's execution-count limit in 65.6 seconds. Both 16-diner cases and at least one 21-diner case cover all eight mutations. Stryker uses the covering tests' initial times to set each mutation's time allowance.
