# Send three staff notices through Cloud email

On an opted-in test box with no venue mail server, staff receive the existing old-address warning, account security notices and role change notices through Cloud. The old-address warning keeps its current trigger, when someone else changes a staff member's address. The box chooses the recipient and Cloud writes the subject and text in English or Spanish. The remaining notice classes await the free-plan decision. Daily reports and invoices stay on the venue mail server.
