**Proposed simpler build, pending the choices below.**

1. Record actual successful money in the existing payment row, retaining requested/actual mismatch evidence once for recovery and alerts. Apply `min(actual, requested bill amount)` to the bill; the rest is tip. A short capture therefore reduces the entered tip first; a bill already covered stays paid. Zero records failure plus an alert, not a captured zero. Exact money, currency/bounds checks, replay, concurrent calls and full-disk recovery stay intact. Expected excess requires Stripe's reported reader tip to equal the excess; all other excess, including SumUp, records as tip and alerts with provider, reference, order and both amounts.
2. Let bill `applied`/`tip` change **only** as a pending card payment becomes received, through a new trigger migration; received rows stay fixed. Open whole-order shortfalls become received contribution payments linked to the captured card, clearing the ordinary payment-attempt mark so the remainder can be paid. Background recovery lacking an operator waits for the next operator Pay to attach that contribution; processor reads continue. For placed/already-invoiced orders, propose cash/manual-card remainder settlement with both tenders, leaving invoice totals and fiscal chains unchanged. Item/share lines retain their existing allocation; the money remainder is collected by amount/share, verified through the real till. No new ledger or reader retry-key scheme.
