# A980 staff data actions implementation plan

The existing staff ZIP adds the remaining non-fiscal actions attributed to the person and workforce decisions they made about others. Both routes use the same parts and retain their authorisation and final recheck. The [A980 backlog](../../backlog/data-protection.md#a980--data-protection-waitrons-side), [design §2.1, row s12](../specs/2026-10-22-a980-data-protection-design.md#21-staff) and Lane K's 23 November 02:25 queue entry set the scope.

## Read the recorded actions

- Add `apps/server/src/personal-data-staff-actions.ts` and register `staff-actions.json` in `personal-data-export-api.ts`. Export current bill editor, draft owner and line credit by order or draft and line reference. Export handled alert code and time, activity kind and time, receipt handover time and job id, and current settings module and save time. For outgoing mail, export kind, status, time and the subject and requester roles, including both on one row. Invoice mail stays in the fiscal remainder. Attendance reminders remain in the workforce part, where the reminder writer sets the subject and no requester. Leave out customer data, notes, raw activity and settings data, mail addresses and parameters, and print bytes and claim tokens.
- Add `apps/server/src/personal-data-workforce-decisions.ts` for `workforce-decisions.json`. Use the existing workforce gate pattern from `personal-data-time-record.ts`. Read decisions matching the subject as decider or authoriser. Include absence outcome, dates and decision time, swap outcome and decision time, published rota period and time including superseded versions, and attendance action and time. Use record references and dates to identify the decisions. This keeps other staff's names, absence kind and notes, shift times, and attendance reasons and changes out of this file. Existing staff files keep their current content. The new parts add no logs or error details.
- Export the existing `readHistory` from `personal-data-service-actions.ts` within the server. Reuse its rowid pages and yields after the first write-lock turn in both new parts. `packages/workforce/src/personal-data.test.ts` checks that reads inside that turn use indexes. The four decider columns have no declared indexes. Export `attendanceAnswers` in `packages/workforce/src/index.ts` alongside the other workforce tables. Sort the projections deterministically and omit empty files. Keep schema, permissions, sales, invoice and document tables, unpaid departures, deferred closures and recovery packs outside the change.

## Prove what the download contains

- Test first in `apps/server/src/personal-data-staff-actions.test.ts`, “exports only the subject's remaining non-fiscal actions”, and `personal-data-workforce-decisions.test.ts`, “exports only decisions made for other staff”. Assert exact rows with different actors, no file for an uninvolved person, both mail roles and mail exclusions, a superseded rota, a draft and an attendance self-confirmation. Update `personal-data-actions.coverage.test.ts` for the nine covered columns, identifying mail's two exceptions, and the workforce roster classification and public export inventory. Preserve the existing indexed-read and service paging tests.
- Use `personal-data-export-api.workforce.test.ts` to prove both parts reach the ZIP and no workforce decisions appear with workforce disabled. Run the changed suites and `personal-data-readme.test.ts` with `pnpm --filter <package> exec vitest run <files>`. In `i18n/personal-data-readme.ts` explain both files in English and Spanish, including current attribution, activity-log pruning after 30 days and decision record references. List the files in both `guide/*/staff.md` copies.
- Update `docs/backlog.md` and `docs/backlog/data-protection.md`. Remove the finished actor and decision work, retain fiscal mail and other open work, and include the new history scans in the existing box measurement entry.
