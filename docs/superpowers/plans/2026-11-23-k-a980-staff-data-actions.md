# A980 staff data actions implementation plan

The existing staff ZIP adds the remaining non-fiscal actions attributed to the person and workforce decisions they made about others. Both routes use the same parts and retain their authorisation and final recheck. The [A980 backlog](../../backlog/data-protection.md#a980--data-protection-waitrons-side), [design §2.1, row s12](../specs/2026-10-22-a980-data-protection-design.md#21-staff) and Lane K's 23 November 02:25 queue entry set the scope.

## Read the recorded actions

- Add `apps/server/src/personal-data-staff-actions.ts` and register `staff-actions.json` in `personal-data-export-api.ts`. Export current bill editor, draft owner and line credit by order or draft and line reference. Export handled alert code and time, activity kind and time, receipt handover time and job id, and current settings module and save time. For outgoing mail, export kind, status, time and the subject and requester roles, including both on one row. Attendance reminders remain in the workforce part. The invoice mail writer sets neither staff role. Leave out customer data, notes, raw activity and settings data, mail addresses and parameters, and print bytes and claim tokens.
- Add `apps/server/src/personal-data-workforce-decisions.ts` for `workforce-decisions.json`. Use the existing workforce gate pattern from `personal-data-time-record.ts`. Match the subject as decider or authoriser and exclude their own absences and attendance answers, and swaps they requested or were offered. Include absence outcome and dates, swap outcome, published rota period including superseded versions, and attendance action, each with its recorded time. Identify decisions by record reference and date. This keeps other staff's names, absence kind and notes, shift times, and attendance reasons and changes out of this file. Existing staff files keep their current content. The new parts add no logs or error details.
- Export the existing `readHistory` from `personal-data-service-actions.ts` within the server. Reuse its rowid pages and yields after the first write-lock turn in both new parts. `packages/workforce/src/personal-data.test.ts` checks that reads inside that turn use indexes. The four decider columns have no declared indexes. Export `attendanceAnswers` in `packages/workforce/src/index.ts` alongside the other workforce tables. Sort the projections deterministically and omit empty files. Keep schema, permissions, sales, invoice and document tables, unpaid departures, deferred closures and recovery packs outside the change.

## Prove what the download contains

- Test first in `apps/server/src/personal-data-staff-actions.test.ts`, “exports only the subject's remaining non-fiscal actions”, and `personal-data-workforce-decisions.test.ts`, “exports only decisions made for other staff”. Assert exact rows with different actors, no file for an uninvolved person, both mail roles and the reminder exclusion, a superseded rota, a draft and an excluded self-confirmation. Move the nine columns to `READ` in `personal-data-actions.coverage.test.ts`, noting where the reminders are read. Add the `attendanceAnswers` name to `packages/workforce/src/index.test.ts`. The workforce part's existing column classification and the indexed-read and service paging checks stay intact.
- Use `personal-data-export-api.workforce.test.ts` to prove both parts reach the ZIP and no workforce decisions appear with workforce disabled. Run the changed suites and `personal-data-readme.test.ts` with `pnpm --filter <package> exec vitest run <files>`. In `i18n/personal-data-readme.ts` explain both files in English and Spanish, including current attribution, activity-log pruning after 30 days, decision record references, and that attendance reminders belong to the workforce part when enabled. List the files in both `guide/*/staff.md` copies.
- Update `docs/backlog.md` and `docs/backlog/data-protection.md`. Remove the finished actor and decision work, retain fiscal and other open work, and include the new history scans in the existing box measurement entry.
