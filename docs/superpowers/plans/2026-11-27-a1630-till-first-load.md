# Keep the till floor visible during its first read (A1630a)

[Design §§7.16–7.17, 9.11 and 13](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md). Lane F queue, A1630. The design records the 27 November decisions. Claude reviewed the plan, and the watcher cleared it with the corrections below on 27 November 2026 at 23:50.

**What staff get.** Staff see the Floor frame once sign-in has established its starting screen. The PIN screen still shows “Logging in…” while sign-in reads device zones on a till and menu offers wherever that read is needed (`till-app.ts:6066–6103`). The Floor banner and strip start only once the Floor read starts. A1630b moves the earlier reads. The same applies when first opening Floor from Counter. At 300 ms grey shapes appear, at 5 s “Still waiting for the box…” appears above them, and at 15 s the “Can't reach the box” strip takes its place with WiFi help and **Try again**. A quick success shows no shapes. A refused first read shows the strip immediately. Refresh keeps the floor already shown.

1. **Share the appearance and timing.** Add `packages/ui/src/components/wt-placeholder.ts`, export it from `packages/ui/src/index.ts`, and show it in `packages/ui/index.html`. It is a decorative, motionless shape. The parent sizes it and it reads `--wt-color-surface-sunken` and `--wt-radius-md`. Share the 300/5,000/15,000 ms transitions and cancellation in `apps/till/src/state/first-read.ts`. Share the notice markup in `apps/till/src/widgets/first-read-notice.ts` so the same two states fit each content area. Reuse the decorative `wt-spinner`, in teal, and `wt-button`. Keep retry outside the status announcement and match frame cf's local overlay. `wt-banner` has one text field and announces its primary action inside the status. Define the spec's English and Spanish copy in `i18n/strings.ts`.
2. **Use it on Floor.** In `till-app.ts`, show the permitted Floor before awaiting its data in `#enterSignedIn` and preserve the current session, destination and resume checks. Use a full first-read attempt in `#loadFloorData` until tables and zones have been shown this session. Route first-load Retry in `#onFloorRefresh` through that attempt, aborting the previous read and disregarding its reply. Add optional `ReadOptions` to `FloorApi.listZones` so both requests share the abort signal. Ignore extra floor polls while a first attempt is pending. After it settles, let refresh recover using the existing read ordering. In `screens/till-floor-screen.ts`, show a neutral map placeholder since table positions arrive with the first read. Keep the existing refresh behavior after the first floor display, including an empty floor. Leave the Counter queues' retry in place.
3. **Test first.** The placeholder's sibling Chromium tests check token painting, keyboard exclusion, axe in both themes and forced colours. `state/first-read.test.ts` checks timing boundaries, quick success and cancellation. `first-read-notice.a11y.test.ts` checks both notice states in both themes and keeps Try again outside the spoken message. `till-app-floor-empty.test.ts` checks wiring: a deferred Floor reply after sign-in, immediate refusal, timeout reaching the strip, retry replacing both reads, a stale reply, a poll during the first read and refresh after success. Compare against both `cf` frames at 390 px and send Lane P and the owner both themes and languages at phone and till widths in both orientations. Beside those pictures, name the neutral grey map area in place of table rectangles, the teal spinner ring with a gap in place of a grey ring with a teal top, and what the zone control shows before its name arrives. Update the bilingual table-service guide, design system and backlog.

**Size and follow-through.** A1630a aims for 280–360 product and 200–250 test lines. A1630b reuses the pieces for the rest of sign-in, order and menu loading, and the drawer's first read. Floor draws rectangles where tables stood at this device’s last read, with a plain grey area on the first-ever load. It checks frame cf's busy button and keeps drawer actions as they are. First estimate for b is 260–340 product and 180–240 test lines. With docs, the full ask may exceed 1,000 lines. Give each PR a three-hour scope.
