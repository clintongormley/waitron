# Keep the till floor visible during its first read (A1630a)

[Design §§7.16–7.17, 9.11 and 13](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md). Lane F queue, A1630. The design records the 27 November decisions. This plan awaits the watcher’s clearance.

**What staff get.** Once sign-in establishes Floor as this device's starting screen, staff see its frame and available navigation while the floor data loads. The same applies when first opening Floor from Counter. At 300 ms grey shapes appear, at 5 s “Still waiting for the box…” appears above them, and at 15 s the “Can't reach the box” strip takes its place with WiFi help and **Try again**. A quick success shows no shapes. A refused first read shows the strip immediately. Refresh keeps the floor already shown.

1. **Share the appearance and timing.** Add `packages/ui/src/components/wt-placeholder.ts`, export it from `packages/ui/src/index.ts`, and show it in `packages/ui/index.html`. It is a decorative, motionless shape. The parent sizes it and it reads `--wt-color-surface-sunken` and `--wt-radius-md`. Share the 300/5,000/15,000 ms transitions and cancellation in `apps/till/src/state/first-read.ts`. Share the notice markup in `apps/till/src/widgets/first-read-notice.ts` so the same two states fit each content area. Reuse the decorative `wt-spinner`, in teal, and `wt-button`. Keep retry outside the status announcement and match frame cf's local overlay. `wt-banner` has one text field and announces its primary action inside the status. Define the spec's English and Spanish copy in `i18n/strings.ts`.
2. **Use it on Floor.** In `till-app.ts`, show the permitted Floor before awaiting its data in `#enterSignedIn` and preserve the current session, destination and resume checks. Use a full first-read attempt in `#loadFloorData` until tables and zones have been shown this session. Route first-load Retry in `#onFloorRefresh` through that attempt, aborting the previous read and disregarding its reply. Add optional `ReadOptions` to `FloorApi.listZones` so both requests share the abort signal. Ignore extra floor polls while a first attempt is pending. After it settles, let refresh recover using the existing read ordering. In `screens/till-floor-screen.ts`, show a neutral map placeholder since table positions arrive with the first read. Keep the existing refresh behavior after the first floor display, including an empty floor. Leave the Counter queues' retry in place.
3. **Test first.** The placeholder's sibling Chromium tests check token painting, keyboard exclusion, axe in both themes and forced colours. `state/first-read.test.ts` checks timing boundaries, quick success and cancellation. `till-app-floor-empty.test.ts` checks PIN-to-Floor with a deferred reply, immediate refusal, the 15 s strip, retry replacing both reads, a stale reply, a poll during the first read and refresh after success. Compare against both `cf` frames at 390 px and send Lane P and the owner both themes and languages at phone and till widths in both orientations. Update the bilingual table-service guide, design system and backlog.

**Size and follow-through.** A1630a aims for 280–360 product and 200–250 test lines. A1630b reuses the pieces for the rest of sign-in, order and menu loading, and the drawer's first read. It checks frame cf's busy button and keeps drawer actions as they are. First estimate for b is 260–340 product and 180–240 test lines. With docs, the full ask may exceed 1,000 lines. Give each PR a three-hour scope.
