# A1308 build order

The owner agreed this order on 2026-11-08. Every slice follows
[the slice rules](2026-11-08-a1308-slice-rules.md). One slice is one pull request. "Owner lands"
follows the rules' §6. The pull-request counts in batch 2 and 3 are estimates from the sizes
measured on 2026-11-08 (`git grep -c` over `apps`, `packages` and `scripts`), not plans yet.

## Lanes

Two Codex lanes, split by area (rules §7):

- **Lane 1:** the shared tables, the location sweep, venue and box, devices and printing, orders and
  sales, refunds.
- **Lane 2:** the mail queue, the dropped features, floor and kitchen, menu and photos, people and
  working time, payments and adjustments, then fiscal once lane 1 has finished.

Slice 1a comes first for both lanes, because every later slice uses its rebuild command.
