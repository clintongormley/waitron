**Finish line:** After approval of a €14 steak cancellation, an admitted operator can refund its remaining €14 without another PIN. Increasing the amount requires independent refund authority. The till retains no approval credential.

**Read trace:** `line-cancel-api.ts:242–262` and `till-api.ts:2987–3005` check `sale.refund`; `finish-party-service.ts:190` passes the result to `line-cancel.ts:464–469`. Its adjustment stores `refundHandoff`, `refundValue`, `approvedBy` and `requestedBy`. `packages/adjustments/src/refund-links.ts` counts pending/completed links, excluding failed refunds. `tender-refund-reservation.ts:218` currently caps `viaOverride: true`; `tender-refund-authorization.ts` checks permission before reservation/replay. Refund `authorizedBy` also reaches drawer audit and provider accounting; adjustment `approvedBy` reaches reports and personal-data exports. Preserve those meanings.

## Build in one slice

- [ ] **Server, with failing tests first.** Extend `packages/adjustments/src/refund-links.ts` to expose the existing authorisation fields. For a valid handed-off cancel, `approvedBy ?? requestedBy` identifies its recorded authority: null `approvedBy` means the requester held the permission, not missing approval. Keep cancellation writers and report semantics intact. No live reapproval of that historical decision.

  In `apps/server/src/tender-refund-authorization.ts`, `tender-refund-reservation.ts` and `tender-refunds.ts`, distinguish session permission, freshly checked PIN and cancellation authority explicitly; never use `viaOverride` to choose the cap. Prefer independent authority when supplied. Permit cancellation authority only with nonempty, validated `cancellationIds` for this sale’s bill, each a refund handoff with a recorded value. In the reservation transaction, allocate in request order, subtract pending/completed links across all refunds/tenders, and require the entire applied amount within the remaining sum and zero tip. Failed refunds release capacity. Missing IDs grant nothing. Independent authority may exceed cancellation value and include tips under existing payment limits; links still stop at each cancel’s remaining value.

  Keep session/person, device admission, tender capability, zones, clock and trading checks. The caller need not hold `sale.refund` when cancellation authority covers the request. Hand-keyed cards still require confirmation and a freshly checked refund PIN. Carry the first contributing cancel’s recorded authoriser into `authorizedBy`; links retain each contributing approval, including mixed approvers. Set drawer delegation from authoriser versus requester, separately from authority mode.

  Resolve matching submission replay before remaining-cap checks; retain original audit facts and never duplicate links, money or drawer work. In `tender-refund-api.ts`, retry recovers authority from stored links covering the entire applied amount with no tip, never from an unlinked refund. Preserve fingerprint mismatch refusals.
