> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task, inline by the driver. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. The owner has already approved the flow and build; no further design approval is required.

**Goal:** Give Counter and table operators one payment window, optional eligible card slips, and an independent final document choice whose dismissal means None.

**Architecture:** Reuse bill allocation, local issuance, A796 equipment and the retained branch's payment/slip recovery. Separate immutable payment intent from accepted document commands; keep the final question transient, with no pending-choice record. Reuse delivery history for separately requested Print and Email, preserving A948's enabled F1 paths and leaving F2 replacement with A556.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Lit, shared `wt-*` primitives, exact Decimal money, Drizzle/SQLite, Vitest with real Chromium and axe.

## Global Constraints

- Owner answers, 27 October about 02:01/02:05/02:12 CET: dismissal means **None**; no durable pending state, recovery item, timer or zone-policy fallback for a document choice.
- Each reader's **Prints its own slip automatically** defaults **false**. True excludes Waitron slips; false offers **Print card slip**, initially unticked. No provider print toggle, native-control checkbox, unknown-capability fallback or hardware-control qualification prerequisite. Cash and manual card have no Waitron slip.
- **An amount / Split equally / Items / Whole bill**, with **Whole bill** selected on the right. Payment first; remaining amount and next tender after each part; final settlement or settling edit asks **Receipt / Full invoice / None**, with independent **Print** and **Email**. Table closure remains separate.
- Build after **E/A948 #2122 lands** and preserve F1 enablement in **every environment**, including production. Keep A948's actual route scope and A555's counter residual. Issued F1 delivery is available; F2-to-F3 replacement is **A556**, not implemented here.
- Do not modify or add calls to protected fiscal writers, hash/chain/number allocation, `recordSubstitution`, alta builders, `registerSif` or `restoreFiscal`. Existing issuance calls stay in their existing orchestration. Golden/fiscal tests stay unchanged. The only authorized guard edit is adding `bill_payment_slip_copies` to `scripts/append-only-triggers.test.ts`'s `EXPECTED` (owner, 21 October).
- Preserve money, VAT, paid units, pending reservations/refunds, session/person/device/profile/zone checks, clock-behind stops, drawer audit and existing behavioral assertions. Replay returns original facts and adds no money, issuance, slip job or drawer action.
- A790 retains its routing, original/copy/recovery contracts, separate Equipment Invoice setting and dashboard memory. A796 retains one Equipment Receipt setting, takeover, QR/NFC and save concurrency. A582 payment badges move into the rebuilt controls. Do not implement their unrelated residuals.
- `3e45abdc69c0305100304d4501ca96812ba87a0b` (P) is a read-only retained-work reference, not the new merge base. Regenerate needed migrations from fresh main; never copy P's numbered migrations or edit shipped migrations/snapshots/journals. No compatibility migration for an unpublished branch.
- Every implementation/test change follows observed red, minimal implementation, observed green, then `git commit -s`. Keep each changed-test inventory from P and record intentional expectation moves against these answers. No new guard changes, weakened checks, coverage exclusions or lowered thresholds.

## Review Focus

1. A paid result or settling edit arrives after disconnect/logout: Task 6/9 tests must show no pending document work, auto-paper or choice transferred to the last payer/new session.
2. Reader setting/equipment changes during an unanswered charge: Task 4 tests must replay frozen intent and never create a second charge or slip.
3. Print and Email race across devices or lose an answer: Task 5 tests must reconcile the same command and derive designation from shared history, retaining uncertainty.
4. A placed Counter bill has sent dishes or reserved money: Task 2/8 tests must preserve kitchen/collection facts and never equate zero available balance with settlement.
5. An enabled F1 or already-issued collection meets the new final screen: Task 5/6/9 tests must preserve its recorded identity and delivery; an F2 must never be mutated into F1 or charged for replacement.
