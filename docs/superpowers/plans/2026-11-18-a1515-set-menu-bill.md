# Set menus, piece 4: paying a set-menu bill, its receipt and its invoice — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Every implementer is dispatched as `subagent_type: "implementer"`.

**Goal:** A bill holding set menus can be paid, comped and discounted, like any bill. Its ticket and
full invoice list the set menus and surcharges, not every covered dish at 0.00, and its sales
reports come out right.

**Architecture:** A set-menu dish's place and price are written once, when piece 2 places it, and
change only by a move the waiter chooses. Nothing reprices a bill, so nothing needs freezing at
payment. Piece 2 refuses money on a bill holding set menus; this piece lifts that refusal for
bills eaten here. It stops removing a set menu that was paid by items, by − as well as Cancel. A comped
or discounted dish moved alone to another bill is handled as decision 3 settles. Sale
lines gain the set-menu role, so every document and report can tell a set menu from a covered
dish. Covered dishes are left off the printed documents, never out of `sale_lines`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 7, drizzle (core migration set, `migration-baseline rebuild`), Hono
routes in `apps/server`, `packages/core` (sale recording), `packages/adjustments`,
`packages/reporting`, Lit screens in `apps/till` and `apps/dashboard`, Vitest 4.

## Decisions

1. **Money on a set-menu bill eaten here works as on any bill** (my call). The refusal is lifted
   for every bill eaten here at the four places listed above. A whole payment writes no
   `bill_payments` row (lane M F1, run), which is why piece 2 refuses at the ticket path too.
   Nothing new runs at payment: places and prices never change by themselves (spec §3).
   **A takeaway bill holding a set menu keeps the refusal until VAT-RATES piece B lands** (my
   call, for the watcher). Today a takeaway set-menu line files at its set menu's own takeaway
   class. Every other line on the bill files at its product's own takeaway rate
   (`apps/server/src/line-vat-rates.ts:28-39`). So a surcharge or a paid extra on a wine would
   file at 21% inside a 10% set menu. A covered wine would add a 21% row with a 0.00 base, because
   `rateLines` makes one breakdown row per rate whatever the amount
   (`packages/menu/src/pricing.ts:219-255`). The owner's VAT design forbids exactly that: such a
   set menu "cannot be taken away". Piece B's payment check replaces this refusal.
2. **Removing an empty set menu follows today's rule for cancelling a line** (owner, 15:15:
   "Allow, like any cancel"). The removal, by − or by Cancel, is refused only when the set menu
   was paid by items (`refusePaidLines`, `bill-payments.ts:532-545`, code `bill.line_paid`), and −
   takes an unpaid empty set menu before a paid one (Task 2b). A comp or discount
   does not stop it. A cancel deletes a comped line today, and `adjustment_lines.line_id` has no
   key so that the delete goes through (`packages/db/src/schema/adjustments/adjustment-lines.ts:5-9`).
   A bill-wide discount is spread onto every priced line, so refusing adjusted set menus would
   stop − on every set menu after one. Removing an empty set menu from a bill that holds money
   stays allowed while the money received fits the new total (`assertBillInvariant`,
   `bill-payments.ts:552-570`).
3. **A comped or discounted set-menu dish moved to another bill without its set menu moves alone
   and loses its comp or discount** (owner, 15:15: "Allow; the comp is dropped"). It is placed and
   priced again on the target bill, as any dish moved alone is (spec §4). A manager applies a new
   comp there if one is wanted. The till warns the waiter before the comp is lost. Moved with its
   set menu, the dish keeps its place and its adjusted price (piece 2). An item-paid dish still
   cannot move (`working-order.ts:4355-4360`). The offered move still never names a comped,
   discounted or item-paid dish (spec §2; `movable`, above).

   **How the comp is dropped: two ways, ANSWERED (b).** The owner chose (b), refuse the move, on
   2026-11-23 (relayed by the watcher at 11:50, lane C questions.md, heading "WATCHER 2026-11-23
   11:50 — A1515 decision 3: OWNER ANSWER — refuse the move"), so Task 2e is built and Tasks 2c,
   2d, 7c-i and 7c-ii are not. The options as they were put follow.

   Nothing takes a comp or discount off a line today.
   `adjustments` and `adjustment_lines` are append-only ledgers: "nothing may change or remove
   it" (`packages/db/src/classification.ts:384-397`). The only way a comp stops applying today is
   a cancel, which deletes the line and leaves the comp's rows pointing at a line that no longer
   exists. A moved line keeps its id, so the rows cannot be left behind like that.

   What an ordinary comped dish does today when moved, run on `d02298ab9a` with a throwaway test
   (one case, made to fail on purpose so that it printed what it read, then deleted): a Burger comped from 12.00 to 0.00 and
   moved by `moveDishes` arrived on Bill 2 still at 0.00, with its list price 12.00. Its
   `adjustments` row and its `adjustment_lines` row stayed on Bill 1 (`working_order_id` = Bill 1),
   pointing at the moved line. `readBillAdjustments` listed the comp on Bill 1 and nothing on
   Bill 2. No existing move test names a comp: a grep of `move-dishes`, `move-merge`,
   `transfer-lines`, `bill-editing.split-transfer` and `set-menu-moves` tests for
   `applyAdjustment`, `"comp"`, `discount_*`, `adjustmentLines` and `listUnitPriceGross` found
   none. This piece leaves the ordinary case as it is.

   **(a) Drop the comp and record it** (my call). For a set-menu dish moved alone, the move
   records the drop and prices the line again, in the move's own transaction:
   - a new append-only ledger table, `adjustment_drops`, gets one row for each adjustment that
     took something off the moving dish or its extras (Task 2c). The row records the adjustment,
     the line, the amount that stops applying and the bill it moved to. The comp's own row, with
     its reason and its approver, is unchanged, as the ledger requires.
   - the dish takes its new place and price (`setMenuDishColumns`). Each of its extras goes back
     to its list price, and every one of those lines loses its list price
     (`list_unit_price_gross` null), so nothing reads it as adjusted any more.
   - who moved it is the `dish.moved` event the move already writes for the line
     (`working-order.ts:4419-4424`, with `actorId`).
   - every reader of `adjustment_lines` and `adjustments` that would otherwise count the dropped
     part leaves it out (Task 2c).

   **How the till knows to warn, under (a)** (my call): the pattern of the full-invoice question on a move.
   The till works out from what it holds whether the move drops a comp, and asks before sending.
   It sends `dropAdjustments: true` once the waiter agrees. The server refuses a move that would
   drop one without that flag, `set_menu.move_drops_adjustment`. On that refusal the till reads
   the bill again and asks, as it does for `bill.merge_invoice_choice_required`
   (`apps/till/src/till-app.ts:20334-20347`, the server's `checkMoveInvoice` at
   `bill-actions.ts:269-284`). A warning on the till alone would be simpler, but it would leave a
   gap. A party's bill is guarded by the party revision, which a comp moves on
   (`partyAfterEdit`, `working-order.ts:3575-3586`) and a move checks (`guardPathParty`,
   `bill-actions.ts:96-111`). A takeaway set-menu bill has no party, and `moveDishes` checks no
   revision for it (`bill-actions.ts:308-330`). Until piece B such a bill takes no comp (decision
   1). Once it can, a comp made on another till between the till's read and the move would be
   dropped without a warning; the server's refusal closes that gap now rather than in piece B.

   A fresh-context review of this plan found two traps in (a), now written into Task 2c. A comp
   stays recorded on the bill it was made on, so `placedSetMenuDishes` (which reads adjustments by
   bill) already sees a moved dish as movable on the target, while `readReasonStanding` (which
   joins each row to the line's current bill, `packages/adjustments/src/record.ts:111-115`)
   counts it there. Readers must be tested after a real move. And a bill-wide discount with one
   dish moved away would still reach the source bill's receipt at its full amount through
   `readBillAdjustments`, because the receipt's fallback does not correct it
   (`apps/server/src/receipt-adjustments.ts:246-258`).

   **(b) Refuse the move** (the plan's earlier choice, which the owner's 15:15 answer replaced): a
   comped or discounted set-menu dish moves only with its set menu, and moving it alone is refused
   `set_menu.move_adjusted`. No new table, no reader changes and no till question; staff move
   the set menu with it or leave the dish where it is. It does not do what the owner asked,
   which is why (a) is the plan's default.

4. **Task 3 is not built** (watcher, 23 November, applying the VAT design; lane C `queue.md`,
   A1515-SET-MENU-BILL). VAT-RATES piece B takes over a covered dish's VAT. What a covered dish
   files at today, on main `d02298ab9a`:
   - **Eaten here:** the dine-in rate, like every line on the bill
     (`line-vat-rates.ts:24-27`; set-menu line pinned by
     `apps/server/src/dine-in-vat-rate.test.ts:244-276`, run: Tests 1 passed, 3 skipped).
     One rate for the whole bill, so a covered dish adds no row.
   - **Taken away:** its product's own takeaway rate. The set-menu line files at its set menu's
     takeaway class (`line-vat-rates.ts:28-39`). Decision 1 keeps money refused on such bills
     until piece B.

   Nothing in Tasks 4a to 7c reads or writes a VAT class for set menus.
5. **The till's pay-by-items list leaves out a set-menu dish whose amount, extras included, is
   0.00** (owner, 15:15: "Leave €0 dishes off"). The list stays flat: each set menu by its name
   and price, supplements and dishes with paid extras at their amount. It is not grouped by set
   menu. A covered soup with a paid extra is listed at the extra's amount, because paying a dish
   pays its extras (`bill-payments.ts:1346-1425`).
6. **Receipt layout** (owner, 15:15: "Yes, this layout"):
   - A covered dish with no priced child is left off the ticket, with its free option answers. A
     **priced child** is one with gross above 0.00, a list price or an adjustment, so a comped
     bread under a covered soup still prints.
   - A covered dish with a priced child prints its name with the amount left blank, and its
     children under it as today.
   - A surcharged dish prints its own name with the amount written "+5,00" ("Solomillo +5,00").
     No new receipt label is needed.
   - Identical set menus merge.
   - The full invoice follows the same rule (owner, 15:25: "it can, confirmed"). The fallback,
     covered dishes listed under their set menu with no amount, is not built.

   ```text
   Menú del día            14,00
   Solomillo               +5,00
   Sopa
     Pan                    1,50
   ```
7. **Category sales put set-menu lines in a "Set menus" group of their own** / «Menús» (owner,
   15:15). Today they would fall under "Not recorded"
   (`packages/reporting/src/category-sales.ts:110-130`). Supplements count under their dish's
   category, and covered dishes count at €0 under their own. Top sellers needs no change: it
   groups by the sale line's name, so each set menu is its own item, and covered dishes count at
   €0 (spec §4).
