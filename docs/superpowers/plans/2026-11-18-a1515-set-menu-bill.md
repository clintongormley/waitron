# Set menus, piece 4: paying a set-menu bill, its receipt and its invoice — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Every implementer is dispatched as `subagent_type: "implementer"`.

**Goal:** A bill holding set menus can be paid, comped and discounted. The set menus on it stop
changing once money or an adjustment counts them. Its ticket and full invoice list the set menus
and surcharges, not every covered dish at 0.00, and its VAT and sales reports come out right.

**Architecture:** Piece 2 stores the fit's outcome on the open bill and refuses money on it
(`refuseSetMenuMoney`). This piece replaces that refusal with `freezeSetMenus`. On an open bill it
refits and marks every set-menu line and set-menu dish **frozen**, at each place a payment row or
a comp/discount is written. From then on `refitBill` fixes each frozen set menu's type, pins each
frozen dish to its place and never rewrites a frozen line. Lines added later stay free until the
next money event freezes them too. A printed (placed) or settled bill is not frozen at all: its
ticket is issued, the spec makes the outcome final there, and the line update trigger refuses any
change to its lines (`packages/db/triggers/core.sql:369-425`). Sale lines gain the set-menu role,
so every document and report can tell a set menu from a covered dish. Covered dishes are left off
the printed documents, never out of `sale_lines`.

routes in `apps/server`, `packages/core` (sale recording), `packages/reporting`, Lit screens in
`apps/till` and `apps/dashboard`, Vitest 4.

## Decisions this plan makes (the builder's, for the owner)

Marked **my call** where they first appear in a task. The owner has not seen them. Decisions 3 and
4 are the ones the owner would notice at a table.

1. **What freezes, and when.** On an open bill, the first payment row of any kind freezes the bill's
   set menus. That covers items, contribution or share, cash or card, and money kept from a short
   capture. So does the first comp or discount. A cancel never freezes, since it fixes no amount.
   Each later payment, comp or discount freezes whatever was added since. Freezing is per line
   (`set_menu_frozen`), so "the set menus already on the bill keep their types and dishes; a dish
   added later goes into an open place or into a set menu added for it" (spec) falls out of the
   fit's pins. The freeze happens when a payment starts, so a card payment that then fails leaves
   it in place: nothing harmful is fixed by that, and undoing it would mean knowing whether
   anything else had frozen the bill since.
2. **A printed or settled bill is never frozen.** Its ticket is issued, so the spec's outcome is
   final, and its lines cannot change. A part payment on a printed bill (MEAL-END-TICKET §2.2)
   writes no line. Every issue path prices from the stored lines. Those hold the current outcome,
   because every writer refits before its transaction ends: `issueWhenFullyPaid`,
   `fileImmediateSale`, `finalizeCapture`, `finalizeRecovery`, `issueUnpaidInvoice` and Print the
   bill through it, and `issueIfFullyPaid` after a cancel, edit, move or adjustment.
3. **A frozen set menu can be removed only while nothing is fixed on it.** The count, the − button
   and Remove may take off a frozen set menu that carries no adjustment and no item payment, when
   the dishes still fit without it. Removing it is a staff action, never a silent refit, and
   `assertBillInvariant` keeps the money received within the new total. Any other frozen set menu
   is refused `set_menu.frozen`; to charge nothing for it, comp it. Fix and Free always refuse a
   frozen set menu, which keeps its type.
4. **Frozen lines move like dishes, unless an adjustment or item payment is on them.** A frozen
   set menu or frozen dish carrying an adjustment is refused `set_menu.frozen_move`, because the
   target's fit would rewrite a price that holds the adjustment. An item-paid line is already
   refused today (`working-order.ts:4462-4466`). Any other frozen line arrives on the target
   unfrozen. After the move, on both bills, a frozen dish whose cover line is no longer a frozen
   set menu on its bill loses its frozen mark. Both bills refit as piece 2 says.
5. **Cancelling a frozen dish opens its place.** The dish goes under today's rules: a served one
   needs `sale.refund` (`packages/adjustments/src/policy.ts:177-188`). Its set menu stays frozen
   and charged, and a later dish can take the open place.
6. **Editing a frozen dish's variant unfreezes it.** A change of variant, such as glass to bottle,
   clears the dish's frozen mark, and the refit prices it again. Its set menu stays frozen. A dish
   carrying an adjustment is refused `set_menu.frozen` instead, since the refit would rewrite the
   adjusted price. Options and extras do not refit (piece 2).
7. **A covered dish takes its set menu's VAT class while covered.** Today a 0.00 line at a rate no
   other line has adds a zero-base row to the VAT breakdown (probe above). That row reaches AEAT as
   its own Desglose line (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/backend.ts:132-140`) and puts rate letters
   on the receipt. Dropping such rows instead would make every later correction of the invoice
   unusable (`packages/core/src/correction-allocation.ts:191-207`). So `refitBill` writes the set
   menu's class into a covered dish's `vat_class`, and the dish's own eat-in class back when it
   carries a surcharge. `takeaway_vat_class` is never touched (set menus are eat-in only while T17
   is open). Lane L, 18 November: this is right for food and drink served with the menu, which
   LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.2.º puts at the reduced rate («el suministro de comidas y bebidas para
   consumir en el acto»). A product really sold at another rate, such as a closed bottle to take
   home, is open. The adviser's answer 1.10 says a free item at another rate takes a share of the
   price (`docs/compliance/2026-11-15-adviser-answers.md`, B4), and question 26(c) asks about this
   case. Lane L recommends that, until 26(c) is answered, the set-menu editor refuse a product whose
   eat-in rate differs from the set menu's. That is piece 1's editor and the owner's call; this
   plan does not build it.
8. **The till's pay-by-items list leaves out a set-menu dish whose amount, extras included, is
   0.00.** It lists each set menu by its name and price. A covered soup with a paid extra is listed
   at the extra's amount, because paying a dish pays its extras (`bill-payments.ts:988-990`).
9. **Category sales put set-menu lines in a "Set menus" group of their own**, not under "Not
   recorded" (`packages/reporting/src/category-sales.ts:111-123`). Top sellers needs no change: it
   groups by the sale line's name, so each set menu is its own item, and covered dishes count at
   €0 (spec §4).
10. **Receipt layout** (lane L's recommendation, 18 November 10:56; the wording is the owner's to
    confirm):
    - A covered dish with no priced child is left off the ticket, with its free option answers. A
      **priced child** is one with gross above 0.00, a list price or an adjustment, so a comped
      bread under a covered soup still prints.
    - A covered dish with a priced child prints its name with the amount left blank, and its
      children under it as today.
    - A surcharged dish prints its own name with the amount written "+5,00", the owner's form
      ("Solomillo +5,00"). No new receipt label is needed.
    - The full invoice follows the same rule only if the adviser says yes to question 26. If the
      answer is no, covered dishes print under their set menu with no price (spec §4 fallback).

    ```text
    Menú del día            14,00
    Solomillo               +5,00
    Sopa
      Pan                    1,50
    ```
