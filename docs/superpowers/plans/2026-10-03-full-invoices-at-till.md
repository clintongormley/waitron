## 2. Capture a bill's invoice choice before payment

**Files to inspect/change:** `apps/till/src/till-app.ts`, the bill/payment dialog under `apps/till/src/screens/` or `widgets/`, `apps/till/src/api/client.ts`, `apps/server/src/till-api.ts`, `apps/server/src/bill-payments.ts`, `apps/server/src/working-order.ts`, `packages/db/src/schema/orders.ts`; use A230's validator where it lands. Add only the recipient snapshot fields needed in `packages/db/src/schema/sales.ts`. Keep the address on the bill until issuance and on the sale after issuance; the existing fiscal `Counterparty` still carries tax ID, name and country only ([backend.ts:33](../../../packages/fiscal/src/backend.ts#L33)).

- Red first in the till browser suite: at €3,000.01 the customer dialog opens **before Place in invoice-first mode** and before the first cash or card request in pay-first modes; at exactly €3,000 F2 remains available; **Full invoice** can be chosen on a smaller bill. An already placed F2 offers no ordinary F1 conversion. Invalid tax ID/name/address show localized field errors and bottom message under the forms contract. Correcting the fields enables the action. A server refusal remains retryable. Check both themes, keyboard/focus, phone width and real Chromium; add axe coverage for the changed dialog states.
- Red first in server route suites: placing an invoice-first €3,500 order without recipient is refused before an F2 or number exists; payment against an unissued €3,500 bill with no saved F1 recipient is refused **before** a payment row or provider call; three contributions against one €3,500 bill create one F1 when fully paid, not three F2s. A €3,500 bill with a €1,000 item payment is still a €3,500 bill. Splitting items creates separate bills/invoices: until the asesor answers the large-bill question, a split **or `/api/bills/:id/transfer`** from a bill over €3,000 is refused; use before-and-after totals as controls, while mixed payments of that bill work. A split of a smaller bill gives each child its own recipient choice and threshold; a later increase past €3,000 requires F1. A recipient can be added after a first partial payment on a placed but unissued order, but cannot be silently downgraded or edited after sale issuance. A stale revision, pending card, concurrent till update, changed bill total and a line move that makes the bill fully paid all take the documented path. Test an already captured card's recovery with the persisted choice. Test a new walk-up `/api/pay` card request and `/api/sales` request with F1 details, as well as stale clients omitting them. Test unpaid departure of an unissued €3,500 bill: no F2 number may be allocated.
- Implement a single persisted bill choice and Spanish recipient snapshot with revision checks. Make F1 choice and recipient fields part of walk-up `/api/pay` and `/api/sales` request validation; their first transaction stores them **before** the payment-provider call. Revalidate the whole bill at every payment reservation, cash commit, card start, unpaid departure and any mutation that can cause `issueIfFullyPaid`. Revalidate again at issuance. Keep tax ID/name/address out of error params, logs and alerts. Reuse A230's check-letter routine rather than copying it. A non-ES recipient refuses before payment. Preserve existing tender allocation, tips, drawer jobs and print-job separation. For F1, refuse automated credit/refund until the separately approved R1–R4 remedy exists; do not settle or refund first.

Inspect keyboard focus, error placement and the cash drawer's behavior.
