1. **Storage.** Create `packages/db/src/schema/refunds.ts`; remove the refund block from
   `schema/bill-payments.ts` and delete `schema/tender-refunds.ts`. Keep the column union and add nullable `drawer_opened_at`. Enforce
   exactly one parent: `bill_payment_id`, or `sale_id` plus `tender_id`. Both require node/method
   and store manual/provider metadata. Apply tender-strength amount, reason, source/device,
   state/date, send-count, provider, reference-JSON and attestation CHECKs to both. Keep restrict
   parent/device/node keys; add three person keys. Keep parent submission uniques, pending-tender,
   shared pending-capture/provider-event uniques, recovery and `created_at` indexes. In
   `packages/db/triggers/core.sql`, `refunds_guard_update` freezes request/parent fields,
   allows outcome progress only while pending, preserves first-send/reference freezes and
   makes `drawer_opened_at` set-once; `refunds_no_delete` refuses deletion. No per-kind guard branch.
2. **Consumers.** Create idempotent `scripts/a1308/os12-refunds-rename.mjs`; verify its second
   run changes nothing before hand edits. Export `refunds`. Collapse duplicate imports and alias
   the table where a local `refunds` binding remains. Mechanical files/tests, including raw SQL:
   `git grep -l -E 'billPaymentRefunds|tenderRefunds|bill_payment_refunds|tender_refunds' 3e57d6891a -- apps packages scripts ':!*/drizzle/*'`.
   Hand edits: bill reservation metadata in `bill-refunds.ts`; both senders use `tr_${refund.id}`
   from this reset onward, preserving pending keys through os13. Keep parent views and reports scoped.
   Until os13, filter each sender’s pending and by-id recovery reads to its own parent and keep
   the two live registries. Both senders exclude references attributed to other refunds on the
   same capture across both parents. Keep the current refund’s own reference available.
   Preserve prior bill deductions/provider mirrors without double-counting. Update exports,
   classification, privacy/live/backup/write-path registries and fixtures with real people.
3. **Links/drawers.** In `packages/db/src/schema/adjustments/refund-links.ts`, rename
   `tender_refund_id` to `refund_id`, preserving restrict FK/pair unique and renaming lookup index;
   update `packages/adjustments/src/{refund-links,reports}.ts`. Repoint
   `schema/{invoice-correction-refunds,drawer-opens,print-jobs}.ts`. Their `tender_refund_id`
   column names remain to keep the change local.
   Two insert guards reject bill-parent adjustment/correction links. The claim in `receipt-print.ts`
   takes a refund id after finding an allowed printer. Keep the existing three drawer callers
   in `bill-refunds.ts`, `tender-refunds.ts` and `invoice-correction-cash.ts`. Only the first leg
   of a till cash correction can claim the drawer, and dashboard corrections never open it. Claim,
   parent-shaped audit and separate drawer job share one transaction. Leave the stamp empty
   when no printer is available. Keep receipt printing and refund replay free of drawer side effects.
   Update fiscal `handover-return.ts` SQL/conflict matching: carry/restore refund actors before
   inserting person-keyed rows, preserving recovery-progress comparisons. Update
   `scripts/box/upgrades/host.mjs` quiet check and server demo-seed Madrid mappings, never captures.
