- **D67 One held dish fired as the table's first food is its New order** (owner, 2026-11-02 ~15:50).
  `fireHeldLine` reads `nextFireKind(tx, partyId)` before `startGroup` and passes `new_order` when it
  answers `new_order`, else `next_course`. Read before the insert, because the new group is fired and
  would itself count. B1's case at `order-groups.request-kind.test.ts:134-146` changes (Changed test
  checks); a new case pins `next_course` when the party already has a fired group.

- **D68 A Requested item on the pass board ages from its request.** In `readPassRows` the band and
  the item's `queuedAt` field stay as they are for every row except one with `fired_at` null and
  `requested_at` set, whose band is measured from `requested_at`. A released row keeps `queued_at`
  (the release time, B3 D49), so a station's age and the pass's agree once the pass fires; a held row
  keeps its insert time (it shows no age on a Held card). This is B3's "B4 must fix before PR C"
  (option one of two; the other, stamping `queued_at` at the request, would also move the station
  queue's order and age for Requested rows, which D2 leaves to the release).

- **D69 A group card's party is the group's own party.** `readPassRows` reads the revision of the
  group's party (a correlated subquery on `order_groups.party_id`, beside `partyFirstGroupAt`), and
  `buildPassBoard` sets `party: { id: group.partyId, revision }` on a group card. A bill card has no
  `party`, as today. So a group card after a merge names the surviving party, whose revision the
  group Ready needs.
