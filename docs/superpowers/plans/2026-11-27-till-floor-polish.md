# Till floor polish implementation plan

On the narrow floor, the zone chooser and More should open above one continuous surface down to the bottom of the screen. In landscape, make a small plan easier to read and keep the Spanish drag hint on one line. Use the approved `ag` and `aj` HTML in both themes and [design §§9.1, 9.4, 9.9 and 14.3](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md). This follows [PR 2](2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-floor-bar.md) and the [13a-0 shared components](2026-11-26-till-redesign-13a-0-menu-primitives.md).

1. **Dock both sheets.** In `apps/till/src/widgets/floor-bar.ts` use `sheet-variant="menu"` for the zone chooser. In `till-shell.ts` use it for More. Feed `--wt-menu-sheet-bottom-offset` from the existing footer measurement and remove the floor's duplicate handle and scrim. In portrait, let the sheet rows and their dividers reach the edges while keeping the content clear of safe-area insets. Let the solid footer paint to the bottom of the screen, with the reference's 24 px lower padding inclusive of the existing page gutter or a larger safe-area inset. Where the menu meets the bar, use its existing top border as the single divider. Keep the bar tappable under the list. Retain the landscape side gutters and the existing wide toolbar.
2. **Use the shared rows.** In `till-shell.ts`, `floor-bar.ts` and `floor-zone-menu.ts`, use `wt-menu-row` for ordinary More actions, with the shared chevron for destinations and the existing counts at the right. Keep the supplied controls for clock actions, settings disclosure, help and language. Preserve claims, help badges, Transfer count, existing events and dialog opener focus. Use the 12 px label inset and about 11 px chevron gap measured for the zone trigger. In `packages/ui/src/components/wt-language-chooser.ts`, use regular weight for the segmented layout used by More. Keep the active language's colour.
3. **Tidy the landscape map.** In `floor-bar.ts` limit the card's width for narrow landscapes when the map's published plan aspect ratio is slim. Let wider plans take the available width. Keep the existing scale, 44 px touch targets and pan behavior. In `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`, shorten the Spanish drag hint to «Arrastra para ver más». Keep clock status, kitchen entries and the wide redesign in their scheduled slices.
