In landscape, keep the Spanish drag hint on one line and the same tables within reach. Use the approved `ag` and `aj` HTML in both themes and [design §§9.1, 9.4, 9.9 and 14.3](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md). This follows [PR 2](2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-floor-bar.md) and the [13a-0 shared components](2026-11-26-till-redesign-13a-0-menu-primitives.md).

1. **Dock both sheets.** Use `sheet-variant="menu"` in `apps/till/src/widgets/floor-bar.ts` for the zone chooser and in `till-shell.ts` for More. Measure the distance from the bar's top to the bottom of the viewport for `--wt-menu-sheet-bottom-offset`. Remove the old `--wt-row-actions-bottom-offset`, hand-drawn handle and scrim. Let the portrait rows reach the edges and continue the bar surface through the existing bottom gutter. Keep safe-area padding around the bar's controls and at the sheets' sides. Remove the sheets' bottom safe-area padding because the bar covers that area. Remove the last row's divider in both sheets. Use the bar's top border as the single divider and keep the bar tappable. Preserve the landscape gutters and the wide toolbar.
2. **Use the shared rows.** In `till-shell.ts`, `floor-bar.ts` and `floor-zone-menu.ts`, use `wt-menu-row` for ordinary More actions, with its chevron for destinations and counts at the right. Keep the clock controls, settings disclosure, Help, permission checks, counts, events and dialog opener focus. Give the More sheet opt-in initial focus on the dialog itself through `packages/ui/src/components/wt-row-actions.ts`. Enter on More opens the sheet and a second Enter leaves the clock alone. Tab reaches the first action. Keep the zone list's native choice rows, the leading check and arrow-key selection. Name the dialog Zones and keep the current zone on the trigger. Match the zone button's 12 px label inset and roughly 11 px chevron gap. Use regular weight for the segmented layout in `packages/ui/src/components/wt-language-chooser.ts`.
3. **Shorten the Spanish hint.** Use «Arrastra para ver más» in `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts` and the Spanish guide. Lane P identified empty sides on the landscape fixture. The published ratio follows the area the tables cover and moves as a table is dragged. Keep the map geometry.
