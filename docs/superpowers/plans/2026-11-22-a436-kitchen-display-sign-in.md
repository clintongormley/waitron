# A436 · Sign in on the kitchen display

A cook can sign in above the live station or pass queue. The header shows their name and **Sign out**. Signing out returns control to the display. The owner's [8 October request](../../backlog/till.md#a436--a-kitchen-display-with-someone-signed-in-logged-out-only-after-a-long-idle-time) gives 30 minutes as an example. Propose **Never** by default and at least 30 minutes when set.

1. **Profile and sign-in.** In `packages/layouts/src/device-profile.ts` and `packages/db/src/schema/device-profiles.ts`, allow a kitchen timeout of null or at least 1,800 seconds. Rebuild the core starting migration and disclose the preproduction reset. Show the existing timeout and job-role admission controls in `apps/dashboard/src/screens/device-profiles-screen.ts`. The management API already saves admissions. Reuse `GET /api/staff` and the PIN route in `apps/server/src/till-api.ts`. Keep throttling, current admission, and the in-transaction device proof and reject a kitchen/till mode change during the PIN check.
2. **Kitchen authority.** Keep kitchen sign-ins out of ordinary till routes at `till-session.ts` because `take-orders` also permits a kitchen station move. In `device.ts`, `device-api.ts` and `management-api.ts`, end sessions when device reassignment or a profile edit crosses kitchen/till modes. Keep the profile action, station and pass-zone checks in `device-api.ts` and `device-levers.ts`. A named write verifies the open session, tab person and current admission on that device in the write transaction. A stale named write is refused. A signed-out tab uses the display's existing kitchen authority.
3. **Action names.** Propose attributing station Start, Ready and Undo, pass Fire and bill/group Ready, and Move to station. Move already stores the person and device. For the other named changes, use the existing activity log and actor fields in the transaction that makes the change, inside the command's replay boundary where applicable. Reuse the existing attendance capture for kitchen work. Notice dismissal acts as the display, Close/Reopen for today keeps its manager PIN, and the pass monitor stays read-only. The queue keeps its current card layout.
4. **Board and idle.** In `apps/till/src/till-app.ts` and `screens/till-lock-screen.ts`, reuse the staff picker and PIN pad in a section that opens immediately above the board. Keep the board mounted and keep the display's language. Send the tab's person header on actions and sign-out. Apply timeout edits through the kitchen setup poll. In `session-activity.ts`, arm the timer for a signed-in cook with a timeout and keep the display awake after sign-out. Taps and keys reset the timer. Refreshes leave it alone. Update the English and Spanish kitchen guides and their developer references.

If it exceeds about 1,000 lines, land optional sign-in with existing Move attribution and session confinement first, then the remaining attribution and configurable timeout.
