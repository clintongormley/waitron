# Open the product editor from Menu Structure

**Finish line.** **Edit product** or the photo where shown opens the product editor over Structure. Save the product immediately or close the window and return to its row. The menu address, folders, search and selection stay in place. The photo starts at the image control. [Design, piece 2(c) and §4](../specs/2026-11-24-menu-editor-redesign-design.md#delivery).

- Move open, save, refusals and related forms from `screens/products-screen.ts` into `widgets/product-editor-dialog.ts`, with `product-editor-missing-choice.ts` beside it. Follow `ProductChildCreate` so the forms render in the caller's tree. Share `openExisting(id, initialField?)`, `openNew(categoryId)`, `close()` and `render(choices)`. Keep draft scopes. Products keeps its list, address, messages and Add to menus.
- `screens/menus-screen.ts` supplies loaded products, categories, full VAT settings and languages. Load units, courses and modifier lists through the background client on request. Missing lookups stop opening and show the failure above the tree. Close on leaving the menu or Structure. A pending read gives way when another menu action starts. Show save refusals at their field or above Save. Use `#reportRefused` after leaving the menu and a product-specific shell notice after leaving Menus (`dashboard-app.ts`, `i18n/strings.ts`). Refresh library products after a save. Late answers leave later windows and focus alone.
- In `widgets/menu-structure-table.ts`, enable in-place editing only when the shell permits Products access. Set `data-own-click` on that row link and use `productMedia`'s callback on the photo. Send `wt-edit-product` with the row key and optional `image` field. Retain the Products links and default routing. Set `#returnFocusTo` on open. Capture close from the editor's own modal, skipping its temporary close for a variant. Let `focusRowSwatch` reach the photo anchor.
- Update both `apps/server/src/guide/*/products.md`, `docs/developers/{conventions-ui,design-system}.md` and `docs/backlog/menu.md`. The new table, included rows and whole-menu draft belong to 2(d). Price overrides and other Products links keep their current routes.
