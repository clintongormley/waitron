# Open the product editor from Menu Structure

**Finish line.** Choose **Edit product** from a Structure row or choose its photo to open the existing product editor over the menu. Save the product immediately or close the window and continue at the same row. Keep the menu address, open folders, search and selection. The photo starts at the image control. [Approved design, piece 2(c) and §4](../specs/2026-11-24-menu-editor-redesign-design.md#delivery).

## Reuse the current editor

- Move the open, save and related-form wiring from `screens/products-screen.ts` to `widgets/product-editor-dialog.ts`. Follow `ProductChildCreate` so the editor and child forms still render in the caller's tree. Expose `openExisting(productId, initialField?)`, `openNew(categoryId)`, `close()` and `render(choices)`. The caller supplies API and choices and handles the opened id, close, save and lookup refresh. Products keeps its address, list, existing messages and Add to menus after creation. The editor and child forms keep their draft scopes.
- Integrate in `screens/menus-screen.ts`. Keep the full settings result for VAT and colour. Use loaded products, categories and languages. Read units, courses and modifier lists on request with the existing background client. Close and invalidate the request when changing menu, leaving Structure or disconnecting. Show a failed open above the tree. Show a refused save beside its field or above Save via the editor's existing form message. Report a late refusal with the original target through `#reportRefused` or the shell's late-save reporter if the screen was left. Refresh library products after a successful save, including when a save outlasts its opening. A late result cannot close a later editor or move its focus.
- In `widgets/menu-structure-table.ts`, add row key and optional `image` field to `wt-edit-product`. Structure handles it locally when `dashboard-app.ts` permits Products access. Use `productMedia`'s callback for photo clicks and retain their Products `href` for opening in a new tab. Without Products access the row action follows the existing shell path and the photo keeps its link. Use `#returnFocusTo` and a capturing close listener filtered to the product editor's own modal. Let `focusRowSwatch` find the photo anchor as well as a section button.
- Update `apps/server/src/guide/{en,es}/products.md`, `docs/developers/design-system.md` and `docs/backlog/menu.md`. The new Structure table, draft and included rows belong to 2(d). Price overrides and the other existing Products links stay on their current paths in this piece.
