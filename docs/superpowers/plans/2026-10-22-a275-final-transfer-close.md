# A275 final transfer closure Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Work inline in a registered worktree; do not dispatch agents unless the driver explicitly assigns independent work.

**Goal:** You issue the invoice at the meal, close the entire eligible bill finally as **To be paid by transfer**, and export its invoice for your accountant to reconcile outside Waitron.

**Architecture:** Add a separate whole-bill command around the existing invoice writer and complete settlement contract. One durable closure fact records intent, identity, authority and closure-day attribution in the same transaction; document delivery follows its existing reservation/queue boundary. Extend the existing transfer method's projections, reports and documents without adding a bank-receipt or debt lifecycle.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Hono, Lit, Drizzle over the venue SQLite store, Vitest with real `useVenueDb` and real Chromium.

**Spec:** [Approved minimal design](../specs/2026-10-22-a275-final-transfer-close-design.md). Owner, **2026-10-22 15:55: “A275 looks fine”**. This approves the revised design, plan authoring, fresh-context review and docs-only publication. It does **not** authorise a build or waive RUNNER H2.

## Global Constraints

- Whole bill, positive ordinary uncorrected invoice, no previous bill payments, settlement, void, substitution, unresolved payment/refund, conflicting editor or unsent changes. Full total, zero tip, null cash tendered. Preserve split bills: close each independently.
- **“To be paid by transfer / A pagar por transferencia” is final classification, not bank receipt.** No later receipts, partial receipts, reversal, debt balance, reopening, writeoff, receivables module, bank details or hidden matching. No new tender enum.
- No provider call/row, card attempt/reference, cash effect, drawer job or change. A separately authorised invoice document may print through the existing document boundary; it never opens a drawer.
- Invoice/VAT facts stay on issuance rules. Retain number, dates, issuer, recipient, lines, VAT, fiscal record and outbox; reuse an eligible issued invoice. No rewriting stored records or prior closes.
- Public F1 stays disabled. No F2 splitting/limit workaround, invented recipient or F2-email endpoint. Preserve A231/A741, A555/A948, physical 58/80 mm and QR checks, manual-remedy approval, A444 and applicable B2B enablement dependencies.
- Preserve `settleSale`'s complete-balance contract, fiscal hash inputs, daily-close snapshot/hash shape and all existing behavioral assertions. `packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/write-path.e2e.test.ts` and `packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/inmutabilidad.test.ts` remain **UNEDITED**.
- No production execution, external fiscal/legal verification, provider experiment or physical acceptance is supplied by this plan. Read the TDD skill before any later implementation/test writing; observe meaningful RED, minimal GREEN and the omission controls below. Catch rejected writes outside the whole transaction.
- No compatibility readers, data backfill, historical conversion or edits to shipped migrations. Task 1 proposes one additive schema migration because replay and closure attribution require retained facts. Commit with `git commit -s`; never bypass hooks. Parent controls publication of these docs.

## Review Focus

1. A lost response followed by a frozen day or restart returns the identical stored result, but changing the request under that id conflicts (Tasks 1–2).
2. A second split bill or unserved dish keeps the table occupied even though this bill closed successfully; a release cannot discard another editor's draft (Task 3).
3. An invoice issued on D and closed on D+1 belongs to D+1's transfer line/export while its VAT stays on D; an unrelated node's frozen day cannot block it (Tasks 2 and 5).
4. Reprint, refund and generic “paid” projections can misrepresent transfer as card/bank receipt; all readers and direct refund writes need explicit controls (Task 4).
5. A CSV recipient beginning with a formula, or a later fiscal remedy, must neither execute in a spreadsheet nor erase the original invoice's export row (Task 6).
