## Reader battery

[SumUp status API](https://developer.sumup.com/api/readers/get-status/), read 17 November: “Provides the last known status for a Reader.”; “Battery level percentage”; “Timestamp of the last activity from the device”. Range is 0–100. Owner evidence: 1.67 with activity at 15:22Z persisted while charging; a 17:41Z read returned 100 with activity at 17:36:33Z. Scale is known; freshness is not guaranteed.

4. Proposed watcher policy: omit `batteryPercent` in `packages/payments-sumup/src/sumup-client.ts` unless last_activity age is 0–10 minutes. Missing/invalid/future timestamps suppress it. Reject nonnumeric/nonfinite/out-of-range levels before rounding; preserve zero and 99.6→100. Use Date.now and pin time in tests with vi.setSystemTime; no new clock option. A wrong box clock can suppress or admit an old snapshot; no extra clock mechanism. Connectivity stays independent.
5. No new shared helper, status field or consumer policy. Existing dashboard polling fetches live status; alert cache lasts five minutes (`boot.ts:3734`), so a retained alert can use a snapshot up to fifteen minutes old. These are proposed Waitron limits, not SumUp guarantees. An idle reader may show no battery and raise no battery alert; guide tells staff to check the reader, backlog records unknown idle refresh behavior.
