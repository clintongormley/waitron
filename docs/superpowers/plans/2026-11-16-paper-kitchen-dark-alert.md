# Paper kitchen dark alert — implementation plan

**Status / source:** built in the feature checkout after Claude plan review and watcher clearance; whole-branch review fixes and CI remain. Lane H queue `PAPER-KITCHEN-DARK-ALERT` asks for paper kitchens to avoid a false dark-screen alert while unprinted waiting work still warns. **Finish line / proposed signal:** suppress a station's dark-screen warning only when every otherwise qualifying waiting dish has a completed document linked to its order line and station. For that linked original, require `printJobs.createdAt >= ticketItems.firedAt` and `printedOrResent(printJobs)`. Same-millisecond successful tickets qualify. Paper queued before firing, including old HOLD paper delivered later, does not. One printed dish cannot cover an unprinted neighbour.

Reuse `kitchenPrintJobLines` and `kitchenPrintJobs`: `kitchen-print.ts:768–829,874–926` links route-specific dishes/stations, excluding “rest of order” context; HOLD enqueueing precedes firing (`order-groups.ts:2407–2453`). Reprints create fresh linked jobs; splits/merges carry links. Match the job, line and station without a redundant bill condition. `printedOrResent` follows the original's `resendOf` copies (`print-job-trouble.ts:36–40`), so normal recovery clears this warning too. Its existing behavior counts a completed resend even to another printer; the proposed policy accepts staff's choice of destination. **The watcher must clear that choice explicitly.** Completion is the existing transport result, not proof staff read paper.
