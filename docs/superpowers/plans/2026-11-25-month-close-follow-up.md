# Finish monthly Hours

Managers can settle the selected month's issues, read dates and shifts in their own language, and download the overtime worked under each contract. Before a close, managers get one reminder if issues remain. The next closed month's export carries later changes to earlier totals. The [first plan](2026-11-25-month-close.md) and [clocking design](../specs/2026-10-12-a10-clocking-in-and-out-design.md#decisions-owner-2026-10-12) record the owner's choices. Closing never writes to the clock record. Tips, cadence editing and reopening stay outside this work.
