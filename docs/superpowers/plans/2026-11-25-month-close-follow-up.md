# Finish monthly Hours

Managers can read the selected month's dates and issues. Before closing it they get one reminder if there is work they can act on. A later closed month's export carries corrections to earlier totals. Closing writes nothing to the clock record. Tips, cadence editing and reopening stay outside this work. [First plan](2026-11-25-month-close.md), [clocking decisions](../specs/2026-10-12-a10-clocking-in-and-out-design.md#decisions-owner-2026-10-12).
