**Build.** Reuse `print_jobs` timestamps and request transactions. Driver proposal: `DRAWER_QUEUE_WAIT_MS = 30_000`, all transports. Add `endStaleDrawerJobs(tx: Transaction): Promise<void>` in `runtime.ts`, before claims/reports and inside authorized queue listing; read time after acquiring the transaction. Queued drawers aged ≥30 seconds become `failed`, `print_job.drawer_expired` (“Expired before handoff; will not retry”). Claimed drawers aged ≥`PRINT_JOB_LEASE_MS` (60 seconds) become `failed`, `print_job.drawer_outcome_unknown` (“No result reported; drawer may have opened; will not retry”). Preserve attempts, claim stamps and audit. Missing claim stamps or timestamps further ahead than their bound end conservatively; small backward steps extend waits. Settlement happens on the next request. An agent may send an already-claimed payload later: no physical timing guarantee. A late result from the original claimer may replace only the unknown-outcome state with its actual result; never reclaim or resend it, and reject other agents.

Add optional queue `waitReason: "printer.not_visible"` for queued/retryable failed USB/Bluetooth documents without visibility from any active agent. Reuse in-memory observations and `withinTtl(..., DISCOVERED_TTL_MS)` (15 seconds); paired/scanned alone never count. Restart/no observations says “No print agent currently reports this printer visible. Check the printer connection and print agent.” Derive on reads; preserve `last_error`, showing both reasons. Reuse passive refreshes/styling and EN/ES code wording. Extend `jobReason` separately from `ENDED_BY_SERVER`; new drawer reasons show actual attempts, including zero.

**Limits.** No fiscal/schema/worker/protocol/receipt-system changes or cleanup. Preserve document retries/order/invoice tokens and separate audited drawers; never resend drawers. Traced consumers: runtime/outbox/delete → invoice-print/delivery/status, receipt-print, kitchen-print, print-job-trouble, station-outputs-down, alert-sources → queue API/client/screen. New stored codes are drawer-only. Keep historical specs historical.
