# A803 slice 2: named bills and stable bill numbers — task plan

**Goal.** A bill at a table can carry a short name a waiter types ("Ana · Bill 2 · Clint"), and every
bill keeps the number it was given for as long as it stays with its seated party. A bill that merges
away leaves a gap (Bill 1 and Bill 3), a number is never given twice to the same seated party, a bill
that arrives from elsewhere takes the party's next number, and each new seating starts at Bill 1.
Today the number is worked out from the bill's place in the list on each screen, so it shifts when a
bill goes.

## Decisions

- **D1 Two venue-service tables, not columns.** `bill_identities` (state, mutable: one row per bill
  that has ever been a party bill: its numbering party, its number, its name) and
  `bill_number_allocations` (ledger, append-only: every number ever given, with
  `unique(party_id, number)`). Venue-service owns them, as the master plan says, beside
  `party_allergy` (`packages/venue-service/src/schema/party-allergy.ts`), and the server reaches them
  only through new `VenueServiceContribution` seat functions, as it reaches allergies
  (`VENUE_SERVICE.mergePartyAllergies`, `table-actions.ts:295`). Finding 4 is why not columns.
- **D2 No counter table.** The next number is `max(number) + 1` over the party's rows in
  `bill_number_allocations`; because that table is append-only and unique on `(party_id, number)`,
  the database itself refuses a number given twice. The master plan's "scope counters" add nothing
  this does not already give.
- **D3 The numbering scope is the party that shows the bill**: the root of its family. A bill created
  at a party, or arriving at one, is numbered in that party. When `combineParties` closes `from` into
  `into`, every bill numbered in `from` that is not abandoned **once any merge has run** is
  renumbered in `into` — the open and presented ones that moved and the paid ones that stayed on
  `from` (finding 3) — in `from`'s number order, then bill id. Abandoned bills keep their last row (or
  have none, D4); nothing shows them (`shownBills`, `bill-state.ts:19-21`; `readUnsentDrafts`' filter,
  `order-drafts.ts:1310-1312`), and the list reports their number as null (D7).
- **D4 A bill that merges away as it arrives takes no number at its destination.** Today a join or a
  move with "Merge the bills" (`bills: "merge"`) moves a bill in and then merges it into the
  destination's main bill (`move-bill.ts:303-312`; `table-actions.ts:305-328`), which abandons it
  (`mergeCheckedBills`, `bill-actions.ts:275-278`). `moveBillOnce`'s seated branch numbers the bill
  only when it is not merging; `combineParties` renumbers after the merge, so the abandoned bill is
  skipped. Its old row stays where it was (a counter order merged on arrival has none), so the
  destination's numbers have no gap from it and the source never gives its old number again. This
  keeps `apps/server/src/order-drafts.db.test.ts:3530-3547` (a join with `bills: "merge"`, helper
  `:3418-3428`, that pins Sam's bill as `billNumber: 2`) passing unedited: numbering before the merge
  would make Sam's bill 3.
- **D5 A bill going to the counter keeps its name and loses its number** (row's `party_id` and
  `number` set to null, `bill_identities_scope_ck`). Its allocation row stays, so the party never
  gives that number again. If it later returns to a table it takes that party's next number and its
  name comes back with it ("names follow the bill, numbers belong to the destination").
- **D6 Keys that fail loudly on a rebuild.** `bill_identities.working_order_id` and `.party_id` are
  foreign keys with **no delete rule**, following slice 1's D25: a drizzle rebuild of `working_orders`
  or `parties` runs with foreign keys on, so a cascading child would be emptied silently and a
  no-action child makes the rebuild fail instead (`docs/developers/conventions-data.md:1581-1603`).
  Slice 3 drops `parties.main_bill_id`, which has a key, so a rebuild of `parties` is likely there; it
  will fail on a box holding bill rows rather than lose them, and slice 3's PR already needs a reset.
  Stated at the column. `bill_number_allocations` holds plain ids with no key, because its rows can
  never be deleted (append-only): a key from it would make every party and bill it names
  undeletable for good, whereas a `bill_identities` row is state and a clean-up deletes it first
  (kind K). No product code deletes a party or a bill today
  (`rg -n 'delete\(workingOrders\)|delete\(parties\)' apps packages -g '!*.test.ts'` prints
  nothing); tests do. Stated at the column. `order_portion_events` makes the same choice for its order
  ids (`packages/db/src/schema/order-portions.ts:79-80`), though it keeps a key on `portion_id`
  (`:96-100`), so it is a precedent for the order ids only.
- **D7 Readers refuse to answer for a party bill with no number** (Owner question 5, default).
  `readBillsOfParties` throws a plain `Error` naming the bill when a bill of the family that is not
  abandoned has no `bill_identities` row, or has one whose `number` is null. An abandoned bill is
  exempt and its `billNumber` is reported as null: nothing shows it, a counter order merged on
  arrival has no row (D4), and a merged-away bill's old number could repeat one of the survivor's. So
  `billNumber` is `number | null`, null exactly when the bill is abandoned (stated at the type). Every
  product writer numbers its bills (Tasks 3–5), the venue is reset, and a quiet fallback would bring
  back the wrong or shifting number this slice exists to remove; the throw also turns the existing
  party tests into a check that their paths numbered their bills. Cost: a missed path takes down the
  floor read for the **whole location, on every till** (finding 8), not one table. Pre-live no venue
  holds data and a reset is needed anyway; after go-live the same throw would be an outage, which is
  why Owner question 5 asks, and Task 14 adds a backlog entry to revisit it before the first real
  venue.
- **D8 Server list order unchanged; the till sorts by number.** `readBillsOfParties` keeps ordering by
  opening time, so `apps/server/src/party-table-actions.test.ts:709-713` (which pins
  `[ana.tabId, luis.tabId, second]` after Move guests onto Luis's table, an order number-sorting would
  turn into `[luis.tabId, ana.tabId, second]`) stays unchanged. The till's `shownBills` sorts the
  bills it shows by stored number, then id, and `readUnsentDrafts` lists the floor's unsent orders in
  number order. Where numbers follow opening order — every existing fixture, as Task 8a numbers them —
  both orders agree. Slice 3 may move the sort to the server when it rewrites the result order.
- **D9 The named current kitchen ticket is deferred** (Owner question 1, default). Reason: the master
  plan allows it in slice 2 "only after the A773-2 reservation is released", and it has not been:
  A773-2's PR D, Task 11, rebuilds ticket presentation in exactly `kitchen-print.ts` and
  `kitchen-ticket.ts`, its plan tells itself not to add A803 bill names and leaves named labels to
  A803's tail (finding 6), and no lane has started it. A line added to today's ticket layout now would
  be rewritten by T11 and would put two plans in one function. Staff lose nothing they cannot see:
  the till shows the name on every bill list. Slice 2 leaves `kitchen-print.ts` and
  `kitchen-ticket.ts` untouched and adds a backlog entry that waits for A773-2 PR D.
- **D10 The name follows the party-name rule.** Trimmed, blank means none, at most 40 characters,
  refused `management.request_invalid` naming `field: "name"`: the rename reuses
  `normalisePartyName` and `PARTY_NAME_MAX` unchanged, and the window reuses the approved strings
  `table.name_label`, `table.name_too_long` ("Use at most 40 characters" / "Usa como máximo 40
  caracteres") and `table.name_save`. The CHECK is `length(name) between 1 and 40 and
  name = trim(name)`. (Master plan said 80; Owner question 3.)
- **D11 Renaming is guarded by the revision of the party that shows the bill, not by the bill's
  editor** (Owner question 4, default). Like Name the party, it checks and moves on a party's
  revision (`guardParty`, `parties.ts:414-423`), and does not ask for a take-over when someone else
  has unsent dishes on the bill. A name is not an order, so it cannot collide with an unsent draft.
  The party it guards is the bill's numbering party, `bill_identities.party_id`, **not**
  `working_orders.party_id`: a paid bill left on a party merged into another stays on the closed
  party (finding 3) but shows in the survivor's list, so `guardPathParty`
  (`apps/server/src/bill-actions.ts:124-138`), which guards `working_orders.party_id`, would refuse it
  for ever — `party.out_of_date` (through `refuseMovedParty`) when the till sends the survivor's id,
  `party.not_open` when it sends the closed one. The review's throwaway test (seat two tables, split,
  pay the first bill, Move guests onto the other table) confirmed both refusals; this plan re-read the
  guard (`:131-137`) and did not re-run it. It writes nothing to `working_orders`, so it is allowed on
  an open, presented or paid bill whose numbering party is open; an abandoned bill is `tab.not_open`,
  a bill with no party (a counter order) is `management.request_invalid` naming `billId`.
- **D12 Where the name shows in slice 2:** everywhere the till calls `billName` — the bills list in
  the actions panel, the move/merge/transfer headings and pickers, the Sent panel's bill labels, the
  unpaid-departure window, the pay refusal — as "{party} · Bill {n} · {name}" when named and
  today's "{party} · Bill {n}" otherwise. Not on the floor's unsent marker, on display grounds: the
  marker already puts a person's name right after the bill (`unsentText`,
  `apps/till/src/widgets/table-details-sheet.ts:18-25`: "Bill 2 · Ana has an unsent order: 3
  items", where Ana is the waiter editing), so a bill name there reads "Bill 2 · Clint · Ana has an
  unsent order", two people's names in a row with nothing saying which is the guest. `UnsentDraft`
  keeps its shape and only its number's source changes. Not on paper (D9) or receipts.
- **D13 Personal data.** A bill name is free text that often names a guest, the same kind as a party
  name (A980 design §2.2, row c5). It never goes into an error's params or a log line; the refusal
  names only `field: "name"`. Task 13 adds a pointer for A980's register.
