# Till and handheld redesign · PR 2, Floor bar

**Draft for watcher clearance, 26 November 2026.** The watcher asked for this slice at 20:18 after withdrawing the Tabs and More clearance. On a narrow floor, staff can pick a room, switch Map/List and open one ☰ at the bottom. [Split](2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-pass-1.md), [design §§3, 9.1, 9.4, 9.9](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md).

1. **Move the controls.** Put the footer below the scrolling floor and below notices, within the safe area. The shell measures its own width on either staff form factor and sets `useFloorFooter` on its visible slotted floor below 800 CSS px. That same property suppresses the old floor toolbar controls at every narrow width. At 800 px restore today's wide toolbar, dismiss an open sheet and focus its equivalent control or the floor heading. Keep order, drill and barless screens on their existing paths. [Shell](../../../apps/till/src/widgets/till-shell.ts), [floor](../../../apps/till/src/screens/till-floor-screen.ts).
2. **Keep the floor's choices with the floor.** Move footer markup and styles to `apps/till/src/widgets/floor-bar.ts`. The current floor exposes `footerControls()` and `zonePlanRows()` as rendered fragments and signals an update to its shell with a payload-free event. Its own handlers still select the zone, update the URL and remember the other view. The existing `till-floor-zone-menu` keeps the dialogs and their operations. Its `openPlanAction("spares" | "taken-off" | "reset")` uses the same planned-zone and permission checks as its current buttons. Keep the empty and retry states and put Choose zones for this phone at the foot of the zone sheet. Keep the recovery entry on screens without that sheet. [View memory](../../../apps/till/src/state/floor-view-memory.ts), [zone actions](../../../apps/till/src/widgets/floor-zone-menu.ts), [editor](../../../apps/till/src/widgets/device-zones-dialog.ts).
3. **Use one ☰.** Move the existing menu to the footer on the narrow floor and group current actions as You, Zone, Bills and cash, Your time, and This phone. Put the plan rows in Zone. Keep handheld kitchen and Transfer links in the moved menu, with the existing badge on the closed trigger and its screen-reader status. Retain the till's existing strip links and all action checks, including for the drawer. Use PR 1’s bottom sheet for both lists. Help stays with its existing control. Keep other settings reachable during the transition. [Menu and transfer badge](../../../apps/till/src/widgets/till-shell.ts), [sheet](../../../packages/ui/src/components/wt-row-actions.ts).

Keep the top strip’s Counter, Parked/Waiting and table shortcuts until their replacements land. F 2b adds clock-in time, 3a adds kitchen boards to the zone list, 3b adds existing till counters, 5b adds All zones and urgent jobs, and 5j takes the bar wide. Keep current starts, Back, table return and the rules that protect an order when zone access changes. PR 2 uses the existing APIs and leaves `till-app.ts` out of the patch. [Zone scope](../../../apps/till/src/state/device-zones.ts).
