# A1517-2: allergen exclusions while adding a dish

With “no nuts” selected, you can choose a compatible salad size, agree to remove its preselected walnuts and add the salad without them. Optional incompatible toppings do not hide the dish. This change applies while adding a new dish, including weighed dishes; editing saved or sent dishes keeps its current behaviour.

**Reuse and limits.** `menu-filter.ts` already matches allergen groups/kinds; `api/client.ts:444–450` already removes sold-out extras/labels. Variants and extra products already carry effective inherited declarations (`variant-fallback.ts:109`, `offered-modifiers.ts:154`); null is unknown, not permission to fall back again. Option-list labels carry no allergen data (`modifier-list-types.ts:21`): answers such as doneness or “blue cheese sauce” remain unfiltered. Say this limit plainly in the guide. Adding declarations to option labels would change the catalogue contract and is left out of this item.

**Decisions.**

- Export `allowsAllergenChoice(allergens: ProductAllergens | null, filters: MenuFilters): boolean` from `menu-filter.ts`. During active exclusions, unknown or conflicting declarations fail; reviewed-empty `{}` passes. Reuse cereal/nut-kind matching, including unspecified kinds. Empty exclusion arrays activate nothing; without an active exclusion preserve today’s choices/defaults. Diet filters keep their current scope.
- A product with variants is sold as a variant, so filter its actual effective variants rather than its parent’s declaration. This deliberately changes parent-based visibility. Hide the dish only when no compatible variant exists. Compatibility decides visibility; sold-out availability keeps deciding the grey state. `visibleProducts` returns copies containing only compatible variants. Feed that view to the existing `hasSomethingToSell`/picker paths, using immutable copies and keeping selling ids and canonical menu data intact. Seed the first compatible available variant.
- With an active exclusion, hide a dish when a required extras group cannot meet its original `minPicks` from compatible, available extras: count each item’s `maxQuantity`, with null uncapped, and retain the group’s `maxPicks`. Optional groups may become empty. Required option-list answers remain required and unfiltered. The picker keeps its existing minimum/maximum checks; add no new blocked-group message or replacement workflow.
- Hide incompatible/unknown unselected extras. Before dropping incompatible preselected extras on a new dish, ask inline which named extras will be removed: Remove extras and continue, or Cancel. Confirmation stays disabled until that choice; removal keeps compatible defaults, and staff choose any required replacements themselves. Reuse the current buttons, dialog, draft scope and allergen labels. Add EN/ES wording, without missing-information badges or another modal.
- Pass filters only through new-selection hosts. Keep saved selections, basket editing, inline sent-dish editing and read-only inspection unchanged. Information-window Add resolves its dish from the filtered copies, replacing `#currentInfoOffer`’s unfiltered lookup; if the displayed product disappears, close its information view. Forward filters to both weighing hosts: the counter screen’s payment panel (`till-counter-screen.ts:419`) and the table screen’s menu-browser panel (`till-table-order-screen.ts:2927`). Add no separate handler gates, forced-confirm framework or tests for changing filters behind a modal.
