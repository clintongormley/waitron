# Delete a Bluetooth printer and clear its pairing

Lane H item `BT-PRINTER-DELETE-UNPAIRS` in `~/waitron-campaign-h/queue.md`. The owner asked for one Delete action in the watcher window at about 22:20 on 20 November. The queue also records the pairing refusal seen on the box.

## What staff can do

Choose **Delete** in a Bluetooth printer's row or details and confirm it in the usual dialog. Waitron tries to unpair it through the print agent and removes the printer as it does other receipt and kitchen printers. If the agent is unreachable or refuses, the list still removes the printer and a notice names the box when known and explains how to forget the device in that box's Bluetooth settings. An unanswered command says the printer *may* still be paired. **Pair and add** can bring the same printer back with its saved name and settings. A pairing refused without asking for a PIN explains how to power-cycle the printer, put it in pairing mode and try again.
