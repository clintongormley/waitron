# Delete a Bluetooth printer and clear its pairing

Lane H item `BT-PRINTER-DELETE-UNPAIRS` in `~/waitron-campaign-h/queue.md`. The owner said in the watcher window at about 22:20 on 20 November, “i think we should tie delete and unpair together. in fact, remove unpair. just delete like other printers, which will also unpair”. The queue also records the pairing refusal seen by lane C.

## What staff can do

Choose **Delete** in a Bluetooth printer's row or details and confirm it in the usual dialog. Waitron sends an unpair command to each reachable print agent reporting the printer paired and removes the registration. The proposed order is to queue unpairing and finish Delete immediately, then show the outcome as the boxes answer. The queue asks to unpair and then delete. The watcher must settle whether the screen may remove the row while the commands run. If a box is busy, unreachable or refuses, the dashboard names it where known and explains how to forget the printer in that box's Bluetooth settings. An unanswered command says the printer *may* still be paired. **Pair and add** brings the printer back with its saved name and settings. A pairing refused without a PIN prompt explains how to switch the printer off and on, put it in pairing mode and try again.
