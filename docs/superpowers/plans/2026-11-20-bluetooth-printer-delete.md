Lane H item `BT-PRINTER-DELETE-UNPAIRS` in `~/waitron-campaign-h/queue.md`. The owner said in the watcher window at about 22:20 on 20 November, “i think we should tie delete and unpair together. in fact, remove unpair. just delete like other printers, which will also unpair”. The queue also records the pairing refusal seen by lane C.

## What staff can do

Choose **Delete** and confirm in the usual dialog. Delete removes the printer immediately and sends unpair commands to the boxes reporting it paired. The watcher cleared this order on 20 November at 22:35 in Lane H’s questions.md. The dashboard reports a refusal or missing answer. If a box is busy, unreachable or refuses, the warning names it where known. Switch that box on, then add the printer and delete it again to clear the pairing. An unanswered command says the printer *may* still be paired. Once unpaired, **Pair and add** brings back its saved name and settings. A pairing refused without a PIN prompt tells you to switch the printer off and on, put it in pairing mode and try again.
