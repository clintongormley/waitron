# A1418 PR 1C · Keep waiting for a stalled shutdown upload

[Design §§4, 6](../specs/2026-11-07-a1418-box-stop-intent-design.md) · [Shutdown slices 1A and 1B](2026-11-26-h-a1418-admin-shutdown.md)

Box status pauses checked shutdown when a live upload to a saved S3 destination stops making progress. **Keep waiting** continues the same transfer. **Cancel and resume work** and confirmed **Shut down anyway** stop it first. Scope follows the watcher's 27 November 10:22 planning clearance. Scheduled backups, manual backups, other destinations, completed failures and the spare check keep their current paths.

- **Observe the live request.** Opt in on shutdown `PutObject` and `UploadPart` bodies in `packages/stream/src/s3-file-store.ts` and `s3-client.ts`. A request-scoped sampler in `s3-upload-progress.ts` reads the active socket's byte count and Linux TCP transmit queue. Match both endpoint addresses and ports, including IPv6. Stop listening and sampling on response, close or abort. The spare's paced S3 upload went 15.774 seconds between part replies with bytes still moving. On Linux Node 26, the plain socket timer falsely reported idle while the last 1.6 MiB was still being received. The queue estimate stayed active in that paced control and reported idle in a deliberate hold. Release finished the same PUT with matching bytes. Lane H's `1c-client-progress.md` records the controls, including the unprivileged repeat. The signal estimates bytes acknowledged by the TCP peer. The existing readback verifies the stored archive.
- **Keep the transfer open.** Pass the opt-in through `backup-supervisor.ts`, `backup-sweep.ts` and `s3-backend.ts`. Propose a prompt after 20 seconds without progress. For a qualified live payload, pause its remaining destination budget in `backup-destination-call.ts` and prevent the SDK idle timer from interrupting it. Keep parent cancellation armed. A missing or lost signal gets a bounded failure path. Control requests, readback and rehearsal keep their deadlines. Keep genuine transport error handling. Await upload cleanup and sweep settlement before releasing staging.
- **Require an administrator choice.** In `admin-shutdown.ts`, hold the mark even if the upload and checks finish during the prompt. Keep waiting clears the prompt and starts a fresh observation window on the same attempt. Actual failure shows the existing completed-failure choices. Cancel and confirmed unchecked shutdown abort and await settlement before resuming work or saving the unchecked mark. Reuse the one-incident-per-attempt manager email in `admin-shutdown-duties.ts`. Extend the existing HTTPS `node.manage` PATCH in `box-status.ts` and the dashboard's `servers-api.ts`, `box-status-screen.ts` and `strings.ts`. Update both Box status guides and the A1418 backlog entry.

Start with failing tests. `s3-file-store.upload.test.ts` and `s3-upload-progress.test.ts` cover continued slow progress, one held request resuming, missing observation and abort cleanup. `backup-supervisor.test.ts` covers mixed destinations and readback. `admin-shutdown.test.ts` proves no mark before a choice, Keep waiting without recapture, and settlement before either exit. Extend `box-status.route.test.ts` for HTTPS and permission refusals, `boot.stop-mark.test.ts` for the pause alert, and `box-status-screen.test.ts` for the available choices. Before landing, use the release image to repeat paced and held HTTPS with the proposed timing, including a hold across the old upload deadlines. Require one upload, matching readback and successful rehearsal.

Estimate 250–340 production and 210–300 test lines in one PR. Obtain watcher clearance after the Claude plan review.
