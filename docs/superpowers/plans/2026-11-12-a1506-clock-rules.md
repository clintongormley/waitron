## The owner's rules (2026-11-11 14:34, in the watcher window; the words are in lane B's queue, A1506)

1. **Synchronised with internet time (NTP): sales never stop for the clock**, whatever the tills,
   a jump or the latest invoice's time say. "We trust NTP over everything else. If we're synced
   with NTP and the till has a bad time, then we should fix that on the till, not on our server."
2. **Not synchronised: sales stop and ask for the time to be confirmed only for a reason**: "We
   only stop if we have reason to suspect that the time is wrong and we can't talk to NTP (eg the
   clock changes dramatically or AEAT refuses an invoice because of time)". At a start, the device
   check #2808 built is the reason ("we must check the time manually, which we do with the device
   times").
3. **A time a person confirms from their device is final**, even earlier than the latest invoice:
   "if somebody has confirmed the time on their device, then we trust that time and move forward".
4. **Stop reading AEAT's time**: "i don't think there is an actual API for this. You suggested a 3
   request method previously which was a bad hack. Instead we rely on NTP."

**What a person can do when this is all built.** On a box that keeps internet time, staff are
never stopped by the clock; a till whose own clock is wrong says so on that till. On a box that
cannot reach internet time, payments stop only at a start, after the clock jumps, or after AEAT
refuses a sale as dated in the future, and two devices or one person put it right; a person's
answer is never overruled by the latest invoice's time.

## A1506 PR 1 — a box that cannot reach internet time stops after a jump, and two devices or a person end it

- **The state** (`start-clock-check.ts`): `backToWaiting(cause)` moves any state to waiting,
  "by the internet" included; each caller first reads the shared time-service reading and calls
  it only when that is not synchronised or does not answer. The causes are `start` and
  `future_date_refused` (both on main, `start-clock-check.ts:15`) and the new `clock_jumped`. A look that finds the time service
  synchronised confirms by the internet, as today, so a waiting box whose service comes back
  carries on by itself.
- **The jump** (`clock-watch.ts`): the check at `:361` compares the difference both ways
  (`Math.abs`). On a jump, outside `held`, the watch reads the shared time-service reading (the one
  every reader shares, once a minute); not synchronised, or no answer → `backToWaiting("clock_jumped")`
  and the log line `clock_watch.jumped` with both readings; synchronised → the log line only.
  After A1420 PR 6 the disproof it records today is gone; before it, the disproof stays as well.
- **The notice carries its cause**: `clock.unconfirmed` gains a `cause` param; the till's
  `staffNoticeText` branch (`apps/till/src/i18n/staff-notices.ts:52-56`) picks the words by it,
  and so does the Box status row (dashboard `box-status-rows.ts`); the till guide's
  `{#clock-waiting}` section gains one sentence (EN and ES).
- **Words** (EN and ES, till notice and Box status row): "Payments wait: the server's clock jumped
  and it cannot check the time over the internet. Waiting for devices to agree with its time (1 of
  2 so far)." Spanish: "Los cobros esperan: el reloj del servidor ha saltado y no puede comprobar la
  hora por internet. Esperando a que los dispositivos coincidan con su hora (1 de 2 por ahora)."
- **Tests** (one owner layer each): the state's moves (`start-clock-check.test.ts`); the watch: a
  backward and a forward jump with the reading not synchronised → waiting; the same jumps
  synchronised → no change; a jump during `held` → no change, with the control "same jump outside
  `held` → waiting" (`clock-watch.test.ts`, which injects `wall` and `monotonic`). No boot case:
  boot passes the watch no clocks to fake (`boot.ts:3277`), and 1a's gate tests already own "a
  waiting box refuses". The till's and dashboard's words for each cause in their notice tests.
- **On the test box** (before landing): with the time service synchronised, set the clock 5
  minutes off and let the service step it back; read whether the watch logs `clock_watch.jumped`
  as synchronised. If the flag lags the step, the box waits until the next look (at most 15 s,
  `CLOCK_WATCH_MS`) and then confirms by the internet; say so in the PR.
- **Size:** about 110 production and 190 test lines.

## Questions for the watcher (cases the rules leave open)

- **Q1. Does rule 4 cover the series check's reading of AEAT's web server** (row 15: the same kind
  of request as `authority-time.ts`, at setup, restore and promotion)? If yes, a restore on a box
  that is not synchronised and has no page open waits for the time service; with the setup or
  restore page open, the browser is the second clock, as today. **Recommended: yes**, in A1420
  PR 3, with the "Restore and promotion" sentence of §5 saying "after the time service is
  synchronised or the page's browser agrees within 30 seconds".
- **Q2. `fiscal.clock_drift`** (row 14) reads AEAT's time from its own filing replies and only
  warns administrators; it stops and sets nothing. Delete it under rule 4, or keep it?
  **Recommended: delete**, in A1420 PR 6: while synchronised the owner trusts NTP over it, and when
  not synchronised `clock.sync_off` already tells the administrator.
- **Q3. Rule 3 and AEAT's refusal.** A person confirms the time; later, still not synchronised, AEAT
  refuses a record written after that confirmation as dated in the future. **Recommended: back to
  waiting once** (row 8; A1420 D9's mark means only records written since the confirmation count).
  Rule 2 names AEAT's refusal as a reason; rule 3 answered a time earlier than the latest invoice.
- **Q4. Devices disagreeing with a box that is not synchronised.** Rule 2's "eg" makes the jump and
  AEAT's refusal examples, not a closed list. Two or more devices agreeing with each other that the
  box is over a minute off (today's `clock.disagrees`) could be a third reason. **Recommended: keep
  it an alert, not a stop.** As a stop, only a person or the internet could end it, since devices
  confirm a box only when they agree with it; and the owner's examples are both events, not
  standing disagreements.

## Readings (not questions; the watcher may overrule)

- "Synchronised" is the latest shared time-service reading at the moment a reason happens, not the
  state at the start; a host that does not answer counts as not synchronised, as
  `lookAtClockService` already treats it (`start-clock-check.ts:98-108`).
- A jump while the time service is synchronised is the service's own step and is ignored (whether
  the flag is already set at that step is measured on the test box, PR 1).
- "Never stops a sale while synchronised" means no new stop; a box already waiting confirms at the
  watch's next look, at most 15 s later.
