# Menu editor Preview (piece 1b)

At phone width, **Changes (6)** and **Menu** switch between them. This implements §6 and the Preview parts of §7 of the [approved design](../specs/2026-11-24-menu-editor-redesign-design.md). Keep #4296’s heading actions, the Price overrides clash destination, and existing publish and scheduling requests.
