**Goal:** Replace slice 4's one-step cash correction (#2867, squash `8b6bb438b3`) with a correction
that is opened first, gets its money back in one or more refunds of any payment kind, and files its
corrective invoice once all the money is back; then let a card refund confirmed with nobody signed in
file on the approval given at opening.

**Architecture:** A new correction row (one unfinished per ticket) holds what was approved at
opening. Money goes back through the existing refund code, each refund linked to the correction;
cash keeps #2867's "one pot" spreading through a route of its own. One helper files the corrective:
inside the last cash refund's transaction, or, for a card, after the refund is recorded in its own
transaction. A background sweep raises the hour-old alert and, from PR 2, files the waiting card
cases automatically.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Hono routes in `apps/server`, Drizzle over `node:sqlite`, Vitest with
`useVenueDb` (a real database) and the real Veri\*Factu backend.

**Spec:** the decided note [2026-11-12-a802-correction-primitives.md](2026-11-12-a802-correction-primitives.md)
(§2–§3 and its Status line, which holds the owner's two answers), the approved design
[2026-10-20-a802-partial-paid-invoice-correction-design.md](../specs/2026-10-20-a802-partial-paid-invoice-correction-design.md),

## Words used here

- A **ticket** is a paid **simplified invoice** (type F2), the invoice a guest gets.
- A **corrective invoice** (type **R5**) is a new negative invoice that takes value off a ticket; once
  filed with AEAT (the Spanish tax agency) it cannot be undone. **Filing** it means writing it into
  the record chain with `recordCorrection`.
- The **split** is, for each VAT rate, the price before VAT (**base**) and the VAT.
- A **correction** is the new row this plan adds: what was asked for and approved, and its state.
- A **leg** is one refund (a `tender_refunds` row) linked to a correction. A **cash leg** may be
  several refunds, one per cash payment, made by one request. A **connected card** is a reader
  Waitron talks to; a **hand-keyed card** is a separate terminal staff work by hand, then tick
  "I have returned this money" (`apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts:124–127`).
- A **transaction** is one all-or-nothing database step.
- The **opening approval** is the person whose permission `sale.rectify` (to file corrective
  invoices) allowed the correction to be opened: the signed-in person, or a supervisor by PIN.
- The **background pass** is `recoverTenderRefunds` (`apps/server/src/tender-refund-recovery.ts:24`),
  run about once a minute on the primary box, which asks card companies about refunds still waiting.
- A **drawer job** is the queued command that opens the cash drawer.
- A **replay** is the same request arriving again (a double tap, or a retry after a lost answer): it
  gets the first answer and changes nothing.
- An **alert** is an incident row the dashboard shows under an area, to people holding a permission.
- A **stand-in backend** is a test's copy of the real fiscal backend with one method replaced to
  answer a refusal.
- The **protected fiscal core** is `computeHuella` (the fingerprint each fiscal record carries of
  itself and the one before), the chaining of records, invoice numbering, the never-changed
  `registros_facturacion` table, and the record builders `recordSale`, `recordCorrection` and
  `recordSubstitution` (lane L runbook rule H2). The **golden huella test**
  (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/write-path.e2e.test.ts`) pins known fingerprints.
- **Starting migration**: each migration set has one, rebuilt from the TypeScript schema with
  `pnpm --filter @waitron/db db:generate` (`packages/db/package.json:29`); before go-live a rebuild
  means every venue is reset.

## Review focus

The five conditions most likely to bite a person that no task's happy path exercises, each pinned by
a test in the task named:

1. **A lost answer to a cash leg, retried.** The till resends the same request: it must get the first
   legs back, not pay out again (Task 5, "answers a repeated cash request with its first legs"). Sent
   as a NEW request after the money is all back: refused, the correction is filed (Task 5, "refuses a
   second cash request once the corrective is filed").
2. **A card leg fails after cash has gone back.** €15 cash back, the €10 card refund fails: the
   correction stays open with €15 back; Finish now files −€15.00 (Task 7, "files for the money back
   when a card leg failed").
3. **The ticket can no longer be corrected between opening and the last leg** because another box
   took over (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/correction-admission.ts:53`, `wrong_node`): the cash last
   leg is refused and undone, and Close keeping the refunds ends the correction. A ticket whose
   filing is only stopped (`detenido`, which `packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/drain.ts:1163–1178` sends
   back to waiting) is still correctable and cannot be closed (Task 6, "closes keeping the refunds
   when another box has taken over, and refuses while the ticket's filing is only stopped").
4. **A cash payment sent through Refund a payment with the correction named.** It must be refused,
   or the last cash leg would pay out with no R5 in its transaction (Task 7, "refuses a cash payment
   joining a correction through Refund a payment").
5. **A second till corrects the same ticket, or gives an ordinary refund on it, while a correction is
   unfinished:** refused, naming the open correction so the till can resume it (Task 2, "refuses a
   second correction while one is unfinished"; Task 4, "refuses an ordinary refund on a ticket with an
   unfinished correction").

## 1. What main does today (read at `c76d95f9d2`)

- **#2867's one step.** `acceptInvoiceCorrection` (`apps/server/src/invoice-corrections.ts:97–259`)
  looks the request up for a replay (`:117–141`), checks the reason (`:142–144`), refuses an unpaid
  ticket (`:146–150`), refuses any payment that is not cash (`:151–156`), refuses a pending refund
  (`:157–163`), builds the preview (`:164–167`), refuses when cash left is short (`:168–177`) or the
  split differs from the till's (`:178–185`) or the chain check reports an issue (`:186–187`), files
  the R5 (`:189–210`), then spreads the cash over the cash payments oldest first with one drawer job
  (`:212–257`), one `invoice_corrections` row per cash refund (`:248–255`). Its routes are
  `apps/server/src/invoice-correction-api.ts:51–67` (preview) and `:68–133` (accept), mounted at
  `apps/server/src/till-api.ts:1346`.
- **Its table** (`packages/db/src/schema/invoice-corrections.ts:7–37`) is one row per cash refund,
  append-only (`packages/db/src/classification.ts:103–107`), listed in

  authorizes `sale.refund` through `withTenderRefundAuthorization`

  (`apps/server/src/tender-refund-reservation.ts:84–256`) and completes cash and hand-keyed card
  refunds in that same transaction through `carryNewTenderRefund`
  (`apps/server/src/tender-refund-completion.ts:15–51`), which first refuses while the clock is behind

  resolve and attest routes (`apps/server/src/payments-api.ts:1650–1685`) and the background pass.

- **The session check (protected).** `recordCorrection` authorizes `sale.rectify` against an open
  till session or a management session (`packages/core/src/record-correction.ts:195–205`, input type
  `:83–85`); `authorize` refuses `session.not_open` when the session has ended
  (`packages/identity/src/authorize.ts:49–54`). The background pass holds no session

  (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/record-remedy-corrective.ts:134`, a management session).

  so an open correction holds nothing; "one unfinished correction per ticket" is what stops a second.
- **When a ticket can be corrected.** The regime admits a correction only while the ticket's newest
  filing is waiting, being sent, accepted or accepted with errors
  (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/correction-admission.ts:10–15`); otherwise it answers one of
  `missing`, `wrong_node`, `voided`, `not_simplified`, `filing_refused`, `filing_versions_unusable`

  invoice is refused `replaced` (`correction-history.ts:50–52`). `filing_refused` covers a stopped
  filing (`detenido`), which the drain sends back to waiting (`drain.ts:1163–1178`), and a refused one

- **The forward model.** A bill's last payment issues the invoice in its own transaction
  (`apps/server/src/bill-payments.ts:1320`, `issueWhenFullyPaid` at `:686`); a card that finishes

  throws them together at the end (`tender-refund-recovery.ts:57–104`, the throw at `:101–104`); while

  returns early, counting only (`:31–37`, `refundsPaused` at `apps/server/src/bill-refunds.ts:99–110`).
  The hour-old refund alert runs even when recording keeps failing (`:86–97`; #2899, `0670a983ba`).
- **Alerts deduplicate** by source, device, code and sale while unacknowledged
  (`recordIncidentOnce`, `packages/core/src/incidents.ts:120–151`).

## 3. Decisions this plan makes that the note and the owner's answers do not settle

Each is the default the plan builds; each can be overruled before Task 1.

1. **Order: 1a, 1b, then the fiscal-core change,** for the reasons in §2.
2. **No automatic filing until PR 2.** In 1b a card last leg (connected or hand-keyed) is recorded and
   files nothing, whoever is signed in; the correction stays open with all its money back until
   someone presses Record correction.
3. **Two server actions cover the four ways out.** `/file` files the corrective for the money that
   has gone back: the till labels it **Finish now** when less than the full amount is back and
   **Record correction** when all of it is. `/close` ends a correction: with nothing back it is
   **Discard**; with money back it is **Close keeping the refunds**, allowed only when the filing is
   refused for a reason that cannot clear: `sale.correction_not_admitted` with reason `replaced`,
   `voided`, `wrong_node` or `not_simplified`. Every other refusal (`filing_refused`, which includes a
   stopped filing the drain will send again; `missing`; `filing_versions_unusable`; the clock; the
   chain check) answers `invoice_correction.still_correctable`.
4. **"Money back, corrective waiting" is not a stored state.** It is an open correction whose
   completed legs add up to its amount with none pending; the refusal that keeps it there travels in
   the alert. Stored states: `open`, `filed`, `discarded`, `closed`.
5. **The leg link has no amount column.** A leg is wholly for its correction; a correction leg
   refuses a tip, so the link's amount would always equal the refund's applied amount. It carries the
   till's request id, which the cash leg needs to answer a replay.
6. **Ordinary refunds on a ticket with an unfinished correction are refused**
   (`invoice_correction.already_open`), as the design asks (`…-design.md:245–247`).
7. **Each leg authorizes `sale.refund` as any refund does; the correction actions authorize
   `sale.rectify`.** For the one-tap all-cash case the till sends the same PIN with Open and with the
   cash leg from one confirmation; the PIN is never stored. Today every role holding one holds the
   other (`packages/identity/src/permissions.ts:14–15`; pinned in `permissions.test.ts:83`).
8. **The opening approval is bound to its correction (PR 2).** `recordCorrection` accepts the id of a
   correction row, never a bare person id; it checks the row and re-reads the approver (Task 10).
9. **Alerts go to the tax-filing area, for managers:** a new claim
   `{ prefix: "invoice_correction.", area: "fiscal", permission: "fiscal.view" }` beside core's
   `chain.` and `clock.` (`packages/db/src/alerts.ts:5–11`). The wording sends them to the till's
   Find bill, where Record correction needs `sale.rectify` (supervisors and managers).
10. **Open does not ask the card company whether it takes a partial refund.** A ticket paid by a card
    whose provider refuses partial refunds opens, and its leg is refused `tender.refund_unavailable`
    (`tender-refunds.ts:110–117`); staff then Discard or Finish now.
11. **Every filing re-runs the filing checks** (the regime's admission, the history, the chain check,
    the clock), at whatever moment it happens; the checks at Open are a forecast, not a substitute.
12. **The hour is measured from the correction's earliest completed leg**, checked by the background
    pass, raised once per ticket while unacknowledged (one unfinished correction per ticket makes that
    once per correction).
13. **Cash legs get their own route** (`POST /api/invoice-corrections/:id/cash`): cash is one pot and
    names no payment. Card legs go through Refund a payment with `invoiceCorrectionId`, and a cash
    payment named there is refused (`invoice_correction.cash_route_only`).
14. **Automatic filing retries only what can clear (PR 2).** Each pass, for an open correction whose
    money is all back, the sweep first runs the read-only checks whose refusal is final: the approver
    is still active and holds `sale.rectify`, and the regime's admission does not answer `replaced`,
    `voided`, `wrong_node` or `not_simplified`. If one fails it does not attempt the filing; the alert
    raised the first time stays open. Otherwise it attempts the filing, at most once a pass (about a
    minute): a refusal that can clear (the clock behind or unconfirmed, the chain check, a stopped or
    refused ticket filing) is tried again next pass. Every automatic-filing alert is raised with
    `jobOrigin("payment_check")` and the ticket's sale id, from the till's request and from the pass
    alike, so `recordIncidentOnce` keeps one open alert per ticket.
