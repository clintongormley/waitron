On 24 November the owner chose “3 working days of grace, adjustable.” The watcher’s definition counts weekdays and skips public holidays in the venue calendar. Use the research’s 0–10 day range. Zero closes at the venue’s business-day boundary starting on the first. With three working days and no holiday on 1–3 December, November 2026 closes at the boundary starting 4 December. Show the existing calendar coverage notice when holiday data is unavailable and use the holidays already on file.
