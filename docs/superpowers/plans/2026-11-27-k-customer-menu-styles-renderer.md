# Customer menu styles, first slice

A manager opens **Style** on a regular menu, changes the venue's default colours and fonts, sees the published menu update in the dialog and saves without republishing. **Save style** names the other menus it changes. This is part of [Delivery 4 and §4](../specs/2026-11-24-customer-menu-design.md#4-styles). The watcher confirmed at 20:54 on 27 November that this slice can rely on the design's record of the 24 November conversation.
