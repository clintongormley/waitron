# F3 canje ("can I have a proper invoice?") — design brief & implementation plan

**Fiscal sequence piece 3.** Pieces 1 (sale settlement, #39) and 2 (rectificativas, #46) have landed.
This is the head of the queue. Read-only research; nothing here is built yet.

**One-sentence summary.** A *factura de canje* (AEAT `TipoFactura` **F3**) is a **new registro de alta**
— its own number, its own huella, its own chain position — that issues a **full** invoice (with the
customer's tax details) in **substitution** of one or more previously-issued **simplified** tickets
(`F2`), naming them in a **`FacturasSustituidas`** block, **without annulling them and without being a
rectificativa**. It flows through the same `recordSale → appendToChain → registros_facturacion` path
as an ordinary sale, plus two things the tree does not yet have: a **recipient (`Destinatarios`) block**
in the Veri\*Factu record, and a **generic-layer N:1 link** from the F3 to the tickets it replaces.

## 1. What the AEAT / Veri\*Factu F3 record actually requires

Everything in this section is from the repo's own primary-source findings unless flagged as inference.
Source line references are `docs/compliance/verifactu-findings.md` §10.2 (lines 612–637) and its
provenance row (`:580`): *"F3 canje is not a rectificativa; no annulment; `FacturasSustituidas`;
V2543-06 — AEAT developer FAQ, worked examples for rectifying simplified invoices."*

### 1.1 F3 is a distinct record type, not a rectificativa (CONFIRMED)

> **AEAT, quoted verbatim at `verifactu-findings.md:618-621`:** «No tiene la consideración de
> rectificativa (2º párrafo del artículo 15.6 del […] "ROF" […]), por lo que no estamos ante un caso de
> rectificación (no procede rectificar las facturas simplificadas canjeadas por el mero hecho de
> canjearlas con una factura tipo F3). […] **Siempre debe llevar el destinatario de la misma.**»

Paraphrase: exchanging a simplified ticket for a full invoice is **not** a rectificación — you do **not**
issue an R5 to "correct" the ticket. The correct document is a full invoice of type **F3**, and it
**must always carry the recipient** (destinatario). (Contrast: the R5 rectificativa built in #46
carries *no* recipient and a *negative* total — a completely different operation.)

### 1.2 How AEAT avoids double-declaration (CONFIRMED — this is the crux)

> **AEAT, verbatim at `verifactu-findings.md:627-628`:** «el importe total […] de una factura de canje
> F3 **NO se volverá a tener en cuenta a efectos tributarios** porque ya se declaró a medida que se
> fueron expidiendo las facturas simplificadas a las que canjea»

Paraphrase, precisely: the F3 **is filed** — it is a real registro de alta with its own huella and chain
position, and it **carries the full (positive) `ImporteTotal` and `Desglose`** of the operations. What
prevents double counting is **not** that we suppress or negate anything, but that **`TipoFactura = F3`
plus the `FacturasSustituidas` block tells AEAT this amount was already declared** when the simplified
tickets were issued, so AEAT does **not** re-count it for tax. Two load-bearing consequences:

- **Do NOT annul the substituted simplified tickets** (`verifactu-findings.md:626-628`): they stay
  declared exactly once. There is no anulación record in this flow at all.
- **Do NOT issue a rectificativa** and do **NOT** re-run the sale's economics as a fresh independent
  sale. Our sole correctness duty is: **emit an F3 whose `FacturasSustituidas` correctly names the
  substituted tickets' identities**, so AEAT can make the link. The "not re-counted" property is
  AEAT-side and follows from the tipo; we do not implement it, we *enable* it.

### 1.3 The mandatory fields on the F3 record (CONFIRMED, except the XSD shape — see below)

- **`TipoFactura = "F3"`** — already typed in the library (`packages/verifactu/src/types.ts:2`).
- **`FacturasSustituidas`** — the block naming the substituted invoices' identities
  (`verifactu-findings.md:631`: *"The F3's registro must identify what it replaces, in the
  `FacturasSustituidas` block."*). Each entry is an `IDFacturaAR` triple (IDEmisorFactura +
  NumSerieFactura + FechaExpedicionFactura) — already fully typed, built, serialised and round-tripped
  in the tree (`types.ts:115,230`; `records.ts:109-114`; `xml/serialize.ts:135-142`;
  `registro-row.ts:154,250,353`). **This is the field #46 pre-added the `facturas_sustituidas` column
  for** (`schema/registros.ts:84-85`, *"written only by F3/piece 5 (canje)"*).
- **The recipient (`destinatario`)** — F3 *"siempre debe llevar el destinatario"* (§1.1). **GAP:** the
  Veri\*Factu library models **no recipient block at all** — see §1.5.
- **A positive `ImporteTotal` / `Desglose`** restating the substituted operations (§1.2). Unlike an R5
  (negative), an F3 is positive, so **no `sales_total_ck` change is needed** (`sales.ts:137`: the
  `total >= 0` arm passes for an F3, whose `corrects_sale_id` is NULL).

### 1.4 One F3 may substitute many tickets; payment must not be taken twice (CONFIRMED)

- **N:1** — one F3 can exchange **many** simplified invoices across a period
  (`verifactu-findings.md:629-630`, sourced to *consulta vinculante V2543-06*). This is the single
  biggest data-model difference from rectificativas: `corrects_sale_id` is a **1:1** link; canje needs
  **N:1**.
- **Don't charge twice** (`verifactu-findings.md:632-633`): «deberá tenerse cuidado de no cobrar dos
  veces el importe, se haga con cargo a los tiques o a la factura de canje». The customer already paid
  on the simplified ticket(s); the F3 introduces **no new charge**. This is a payments/settlement
  concern, and the structural guarantee is that the F3 carries **no new tender and no settlement** (see
  §3/§5) — mirroring how the R5 rectificativa is recorded unsettled.

### 1.5 The recipient (`Destinatarios`) gap — CONFIRMED requirement, INFERRED XSD shape

**Confirmed (repo source):** an F3 must carry the destinatario (§1.1). **Confirmed by inspection:** the
library has no way to express it. `RegistroAlta`/`AltaInput` (`types.ts:105-131,218-243`) carry only a
`FacturaSinIdentifDestinatarioArt61d?: SiNo` **flag** (art. 6.1.d, the *absence*-of-recipient marker for
F2) — there is **no `Destinatarios` structure**. The generic boundary already has a `Counterparty`
type (`packages/fiscal/src/backend.ts:36-40`: `{ taxId, legalName, countryCode }`) on
`SaleForFiscalRecord.counterparty`, but it is **always `null`** (`record-sale.ts:331`,
`record-correction.ts:282`) and the backend only reads it to pick `F2` vs `F1`
(`backend.ts:274`) — it **never places the recipient into the record**, because there is no field to
place it in. So today even a non-null counterparty would file an `F1`/`F3` **with the recipient
silently dropped**, which AEAT rejects.

> **INFERENCE, needs the AEAT XSD (or the asesor) before build — CLAUDE.md §1.** The findings confirm
> *that* an F3 requires a destinatario, but give **no XSD shape** for it. The AEAT `RegistroAlta`
> schema's `Destinatarios` element (`IDDestinatario` → `NombreRazon` + (`NIF` | `IDOtro`)) is
> **not** cited anywhere in the repo. **Verify the exact element name, cardinality, the NIF-vs-IDOtro
> choice, and the element's ordinal position within `RegistroAltaType`** against
> `Veri-Factu` `SuministroInformacion.xsd` / the AEAT dev docs before implementing — this is the same
> class of external-source claim CLAUDE.md §1 says needs the source's own words. Getting the position
> wrong fails XSD validation; getting the block absent fails the F3 the same way the missing
> `TipoRectificativa` round-trip would have failed the R5 in #46.

**This gap is not F3-only.** Fixing `Destinatarios` also unblocks future **F1 / B2B** issuance (the
deferred R1 rectificativa needs it too). Worth building as a clean, self-standing library slice.

### 1.6 Series, cross-SIF, and "substituted more than once" — INFERENCES to confirm

- **Own series?** The mandatory-separate-series list (RD 1619/2012 art. 6.1.a, «en todo caso»,
  `verifactu-findings.md:590-599`) names **rectificativas**, not F3 canje. Q5(c) (`:606-610`) records
  that simplified invoices are *absent* from that list, and that AEAT's worked examples use distinct
  series in *practice* (`S-0001` simplified, `F-0001` full) — **practice, not obligation**. So an F3 on
  its own series is **not proven mandatory**; whether Waitron gives it one is an owner decision (§5).
- **Cross-SIF F3** (F3 issued from a different SIF than the substituted tickets) — findings §13
  (`:772-803`) established this for rectificativas *a fortiori* from the identity-linkage argument, and
  the same reasoning applies to F3 (it references tickets only by `FacturasSustituidas` identity, never
  by chain). **Inference**, flagged for the asesor exactly as `record-correction.ts:29-42` already
  flags it.
- **A ticket substituted more than once** would let the same operation appear in two F3s — logically it
  should be substituted at most once. **No cited rule**; an owner decision + a guard (§5).

## 2. The data-model change

Two migrations, one per package (they serialise only against their own journals). **Verified journal
heads (2026-08-02):** `packages/db` at `0013_sale_corrects_link` → **next `0014`**;
`packages/fiscal-verifactu` at `0010_registro_rectificativa` → **next `0011`**. Pre-production, so **no
backfill / BWC** (CLAUDE.md §3) — every new column/table is nullable/empty, exactly as #46 did.

### 2.1 `packages/db` migration `0014` — the generic-layer substitution link + recipient

Canje is **NOT** `corrects_sale_id` reuse. That column is **1:1** and means *"corrects"*; canje is
**N:1** and means *"substitutes"*. Overloading a legally-load-bearing link with a second meaning is the
dishonest option (cf. the `till_id`-as-`server_id` decision in the backlog). Two distinct additions:

**(a) A new `sale_substitutions` join table** (mirrors the composite-tenant-FK shape of `sale_lines` /
`tenders`, `sales.ts:159-173`):

| Column | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid pk | |
| `tenant_id` | uuid not null | |
| `substitution_sale_id` | uuid not null | the F3 sale (the substitute) |
| `substituted_sale_id` | uuid not null | one substituted simplified ticket |

- Composite tenant-consistent FKs **both** `(tenant_id, substitution_sale_id)` and
  `(tenant_id, substituted_sale_id)` → `sales(tenant_id, id)`, `onDelete restrict` (mirroring
  `sale_lines_sale_fk`). Target `sales_tenant_id_key` already exists (`sales.ts:117`).
- One row per (F3, ticket) pair → N rows per F3 = the N:1 fan-out (§1.4).
- **`unique(tenant_id, substituted_sale_id)`** if the owner chooses "a ticket is substituted at most
  once" (§5, decision 4) — this is the DB control for it, translated to `sale.already_substituted`.
- `.enableRLS()` + immutable (append-only), like every sibling child of `sales`.

**(b) Nullable recipient columns on `sales`** — English names, because `destinatario`/`destinatarios`
**are in `SPANISH_WORDS`** (`english-only.ts:148-149`) and `packages/db` is scanned, so the Spanish
token is forbidden here. Mirror the existing `Counterparty` shape (`fiscal/src/backend.ts:36-40`):

- `counterparty_tax_id text` (nullable), `counterparty_legal_name text` (nullable),
  `counterparty_country_code text` (nullable). NULL on ordinary F2 sales; set on an F3 (and, later, F1).
- Immutable table-wide with no extra DDL, exactly as `corrects_sale_id` was (`sales.ts:102-111`).
- **This is an owner design point (§5, decision 5):** store the recipient on the generic `sales` row
  (my recommendation — a full invoice's recipient is a reprint/Z-report fact, and the repo's "no
  cross-boundary join" principle argues for it, `sales.ts:61-64`), **or** keep the recipient only in the
  fiscal registro's `destinatarios` (§2.2) and pass it through `SaleForFiscalRecord.counterparty`
  (which already exists) — leaner but forces a cross-boundary read to reprint. A side `sale_counterparties`
  table is a third option; nullable-on-`sales` matches the `corrects_sale_id` precedent.
- **No `sales_total_ck` change** (§1.3): an F3 is positive with `corrects_sale_id` NULL.

**Downstream consequence to record now (reporting, sub-project 8):** because the F3 carries the full
positive total of already-counted tickets, a naive `sum(sales.total)` Z-report would **double-count**
revenue — the generic-layer analogue of AEAT's "not re-counted". The `sale_substitutions` link is
exactly what lets reporting **exclude** substitution invoices from revenue totals. This is a second,
concrete reason the generic-layer link must exist (not only fiscal identity).

### 2.2 `packages/fiscal-verifactu` migration `0011` — the recipient column on the registro

`packages/fiscal-verifactu` is **exempt** from english-only (`english-only.ts:19`), so the Spanish AEAT
token is correct here.

- **New column** `destinatarios jsonb` on `registros_facturacion`, nullable — beside the four
  rectificativa columns (`schema/registros.ts:89-92`). Justified on **exactly** the jsonb rationale the
  schema already documents (`registros.ts:26-33,77-88`): **not a huella input** (`huella.ts` hashes 8
  named fields; the recipient is not among them — `types.ts:157-167`), only ever re-serialised into XML,
  so jsonb key-reordering is harmless. Stores `RegistroAlta["Destinatarios"]`.
- **`facturas_sustituidas` needs no new column** — #46 already added it (`registros.ts:91`) and it
  **already round-trips** (`registro-row.ts:154,250,353-355`). This is a large saving: piece 3 does
  **not** touch the four rectificativa columns.
- **Recommended defense-in-depth CHECK** (given §5 unrepairability), mirroring
  `registros_tipo_factura_rectificativa_ck` (`registros.ts:191-194`):
  `facturas_sustituidas is null or tipo_factura = 'F3'` — a substitution block may only sit on an F3.
  (Optionally also `destinatarios is null or tipo_factura in ('F1','F3')` once F1 exists.)
- **Immutability & RLS carry automatically** — `registros_facturacion`'s table-wide REVOKE + triggers +
  policy cover any new column, the same receipt `0009`/`0010` relied on (`registros.ts:139-148`).

### 2.3 Round-trip the new `destinatarios` field (the one non-obvious correctness item)

Exactly the gap #46 had to close for the four rectificativa fields (`registro-row.ts`): the drainer
rebuilds a record from its columns (`drain.ts` → `toEnvioRegistro` → `fromRegistroRow` →
`serializeEnvio`), so **`destinatarios` must round-trip or the F3 is filed missing its mandatory
recipient and rejected** — the same failure mode as the R5-missing-`TipoRectificativa` bug #46 fixed.
Three edits in `registro-row.ts`, mirroring the rectificativa fields verbatim
(`registro-row.ts:152-155,248-251,347-358`):

- **`RegistroRow` type**: add `destinatarios: RegistroAlta["Destinatarios"] | null`.
- **`toRegistroRow`** alta branch: `destinatarios: record.Destinatarios ?? null`; anulación branch: `null`.
- **`fromRegistroRow`** alta branch: conditional spread `...(row.destinatarios !== null && {
  Destinatarios: row.destinatarios })`. Safe for verification — not a huella input, so the recomputed
  huella is unchanged (the invariant `records.test.ts` already pins for the rectificativa fields).

### 2.4 The verifactu library — add the `Destinatarios` structure (self-standing slice)

The one place a **new type** is genuinely required (everything F3/`FacturasSustituidas` is already
there). `packages/verifactu` is exempt from english-only. Confirm shapes against the XSD (§1.5) first:

- **Types** (`types.ts`): `Destinatario` (`{ NombreRazon: string } & ({ NIF: string } | { IDOtro: … })`
  — same exclusive-choice idiom as `Encadenamiento`/`DetalleDesglose`, `types.ts:40-42,101-103`), and
  `Destinatarios: { IDDestinatario: Destinatario[] }` on `RegistroAlta` + an input mirror on `AltaInput`.
- **`buildAltaRecord`** (`records.ts:88-118`): conditional-spread `Destinatarios` like
  `FacturasSustituidas` (`records.ts:109-114`).
- **`xml/serialize.ts`**: emit `<sf:Destinatarios>…<sf:IDDestinatario>…` at its correct XSD ordinal
  (verify position — after `NombreRazonEmisor`, per the AEAT schema, **verify**).
- **`xml/parse-request.ts`**: parse it back (the fake-AEAT round-trip suites need it).
- **`validate.ts`**: add a rule that **F1/F3 require `Destinatarios`** and **F2 must not carry it**
  (new `ValidationCode`s, mirroring the 1114/1115 rectificativa rules at `validate.ts:215-231`). This is
  the guard that turns "AEAT rejects an F3 with no recipient" into a local failure.
- **NOT a huella input** — do not touch `huella.ts` (`types.ts:157-167`).

## 3. The writer / entry-point change

Mirror `recordCorrection` (#46) at all three layers. **Generic-layer names must be English**
(`substitution`/`substituted`/`counterparty` all pass the english-only scan and the
`no-regime-vocabulary` FORBIDDEN list = {chain, huella, hash, fingerprint, encadenamiento, registro,
cadena, verifactu, ticketbai, sif, csv, incidencia, aeat}). The Spanish `canje`/`F3`/`sustituida`
vocabulary stays inside the exempt `packages/fiscal-verifactu`. **Method-name choice is an owner
decision (§5, decision 1)** — this brief uses `recordSubstitution` as the placeholder; `recordExchange`
(canje = exchange) is the leading alternative, chosen partly to avoid confusion with the *existing*
`TipoRectificativa = "S"` (rectificativa **por sustitución**), which is a different thing.

### 3.1 `packages/fiscal` — `FiscalBackend.recordSubstitution` (interface)

Regime-neutral, mirroring `recordCorrection` (`fiscal/src/backend.ts:235-239`):

```ts
recordSubstitution(
  tx: Transaction,
  sale: SaleForFiscalRecord,              // the F3's OWN data; counterparty NON-null this time
  substitution: { substitutedSaleIds: SaleId[] },
): Promise<FiscalRecordRef>;
```

- **`SaleForFiscalRecord` is reused unchanged** — its `counterparty` field (unused until now) is finally
  populated. No new interface field.
- Passes the `no-regime-vocabulary` guard (no forbidden token) and needs a `FakeFiscalBackend`
  implementation (`fiscal/src/testing/fake-backend.ts`, mirroring its `recordCorrection`).

### 3.2 `packages/fiscal-verifactu` — `VerifactuBackend.recordSubstitution`

Structurally a hybrid of `recordSale` (assembles an `AltaInput` from the F3's own positive
total/breakdown, `backend.ts:248-305`) and `recordCorrection` (reads each **original** alta by
`saleId`, `backend.ts:442-465`) — but **looped over N tickets** and emitting `FacturasSustituidas`
instead of `FacturasRectificadas`:

1. For **each** `substitutedSaleId`: read its alta registro (`select id_emisor_factura, num_serie_factura,
   fecha_expedicion_factura, tipo_factura … where sale_id = … and tipo_registro = 'alta'`, exactly the
   `OriginalAltaForCorrection` read at `backend.ts:442-447`). Absent → `fiscal.sale_not_recorded`
   (`backend.ts:450`). **Assert `tipo_factura === 'F2'`** (an F3 substitutes *simplified* tickets) →
   else `fiscal.substitution_unsupported` (new, §6), mirroring the `correction_unsupported` assertion
   (`backend.ts:456-465`). Reject an empty `substitutedSaleIds` list.
2. Build the `AltaInput` like `recordSale` (`backend.ts:265-282`), with:
   - `TipoFactura: "F3"`;
   - `Destinatarios` built from `sale.counterparty` (non-null) — the new library field (§2.4);
   - `FacturasSustituidas: substitutedSaleIds.map(…)` → one `IDFacturaAR` per ticket, each
     `FechaExpedicionFactura` reconstructed by the **same algebraic offset-cancellation** the R5 uses
     (`backend.ts:503-506`: `Date.parse(`${stored}T00:00:00Z`) - sale.offsetMinutes*60_000`);
   - **positive** `CuotaTotal`/`ImporteTotal`/`Desglose` from the F3's own figures;
   - **No** `TipoRectificativa`, **no** `FacturasRectificadas`, **no** `ImporteRectificacion`.
3. `appendToChain(tx, tenantId, tillId, { tipo: "alta", saleId: sale.saleId, entorno, input }, sif)` —
   **unchanged** (`backend.ts:520-526`). The F3 is an alta: next `secuencia`, its own huella over the 8
   fields (`TipoFactura = F3` + positive totals flow in), `chain.ts` untouched.
4. Insert the `envios` sidecar `pendiente`; return the `FiscalRecordRef` (`backend.ts:528-537`).

**`entorno` invariant preserved** (CLAUDE.md §5) — `entorno` travels beside `input`, never inside it,
exactly as `recordSale`/`recordCorrection` (`backend.ts:524`). Nothing here changes what is hashed.

**How it guarantees no re-declaration of the underlying sale (§1.2):** the substituted tickets' own alta
registros are **only read**, never re-written and never annulled; the F3 is a **new** alta whose
`FacturasSustituidas` + `TipoFactura=F3` carry the substitution semantics AEAT keys on. The huella/chain
reflect a substitution alta (it carries the `FacturasSustituidas` block), not a fresh unrelated sale.

### 3.3 `packages/core` — `recordSubstitution` (entry point)

New file `packages/core/src/record-substitution.ts`, mirroring `record-correction.ts`:

```ts
interface RecordSubstitutionInput {
  tenantId; tillId;
  seriesId;                          // MUST be the F3's series purpose (§5, decision 3)
  substitutedSaleIds: SaleId[];      // the simplified tickets being exchanged (>= 1)
  counterparty: { taxId; legalName; countryCode };  // REQUIRED — F3 must carry the recipient
  total: string;                     // the F3's own POSITIVE total
  lines: RecordSaleLine[];           // the F3's lines (aggregate of the substituted tickets)
  locale; invoiceLocales;            // supplied or inherited from the first ticket (§5, minor)
  fiscalBackend: string;
  clock: TrustedClock;
}
async function recordSubstitution(tx, backend, input): Promise<{ saleId; fiscal }>;
```

Steps (mirroring `record-correction.ts:88-290`):
1. Look up **each** `substitutedSaleId` (RLS-scoped) → `sale.not_found` if any absent
   (`record-correction.ts:98-105`). Refuse a **voided** ticket → reuse `sale.voided`
   (`record-correction.ts:107-117`). If "at most once" is chosen, refuse an already-substituted ticket
   → `sale.already_substituted` (or rely on the `sale_substitutions` unique + violation translation).
2. Look up the F3 **series** and enforce its purpose → `sale.series_wrong_purpose` /
   `sale.series_wrong_till` (`record-correction.ts:123-151`). **Also extend `record-sale.ts`** so an
   F3/substitution series can't be drawn for an ordinary sale (mirror the reject at
   `record-sale.ts:189-199`) — only if a new purpose value is added (§5, decision 3).
3. `checkIntegrity` → **never block**; aggregate into one `chain.verification_failed` incident and
   proceed (`record-correction.ts:153-172`) — a staff member issuing an invoice a customer is waiting
   for must not be blocked. One clock reading for the whole transaction
   (`record-correction.ts:174-183`).
4. `allocateInvoiceNumber(tx, input.seriesId)` (`record-correction.ts:189`).
5. Insert the F3 **sale** row: **positive** `total`, `correctsSaleId: NULL`, the three
   `counterparty_*` columns, `fiscalState: "recorded"`, `locale`/`invoiceLocales`. **No settlement, no
   tenders** — the money was collected on the F2 (§1.4). Insert `sale_lines`. Insert one
   `sale_substitutions` row per `substitutedSaleId`.
6. `backend.recordSubstitution(tx, saleForFiscalRecord /* counterparty NON-null */,
   { substitutedSaleIds })` (`record-correction.ts:268-285`), `descriptionOfOperation` from the
   location join (`record-correction.ts:248-260`).
7. Return `{ saleId, fiscal }` inside the transaction (the caller commits).

Every touched file keeps its `import "./errors.js"` side-effect so `errors.reachability.test.ts` stays
green.

## 4. TDD implementation plan (ordered, subagent-friendly slices)

Migrations land **first and isolated** so each journal advances once; code slices stack on top.
Failing-test-first throughout; **prove every guard by deletion** (CLAUDE.md §4). Target selection:
anything touching RLS / immutability triggers / the deployment role / chain concurrency → **real
Postgres via Testcontainers** (`TESTCONTAINERS_RYUK_DISABLED=true` locally); pure assembly / round-trip
→ **PGlite**. State the choice per suite.

- **Slice 0 — Library: `Destinatarios` (`packages/verifactu`, code-only, no journal).** *Do first —
  §1.5's XSD verification gates it.* Failing tests: `buildAltaRecord` emits `Destinatarios` (and omits
  it when absent); serialize/parse round-trip; `validate` rejects F1/F3 without it and F2 with it (prove
  each rule by deletion); `records.test.ts`-style huella-invariance test (a record with/without
  `Destinatarios` hashes identically). No other slice depends on the XSD being right, so land this
  cleanly first.
- **Slice 1 — Schema + migrations (the only journal slice).** `packages/db` `0014` (§2.1:
  `sale_substitutions` + `counterparty_*` on `sales`) and `packages/fiscal-verifactu` `0011` (§2.2:
  `destinatarios` + the defense-in-depth CHECK). Real-PG tests: the new join table + columns are

## 5. Open decisions for the human owner

1. **Method / concept naming.** `recordSubstitution` vs `recordExchange` (canje = *exchange*, and it
   dodges confusion with the existing `TipoRectificativa = "S"` *sustitución*) vs `recordFullInvoice`,
   for the core + fiscal-interface method; and the join-table / column names (`sale_substitutions`,
   `substitution_sale_id` / `substituted_sale_id`). Error codes are **never renamed once shipped**
   (CLAUDE.md §3), so this is a before-merge, permanent choice.
2. **Where the recipient lives.** On the generic `sales` row (`counterparty_*`, my recommendation — a
   full invoice's recipient is a reprint/Z-report fact and avoids a cross-boundary read) vs only in the
   fiscal registro's `destinatarios` (leaner) vs a side `sale_counterparties` table.
3. **Does F3 need its own invoice series?** Not proven mandatory (the art. 6.1.a list names only
   rectificativas — §1.6). Reuse the existing `standard` series (simplest, lawful) vs add a new
   `invoice_series.purpose` value (e.g. `'substitution'` — one-line CHECK widen, `series.ts:60`) to
   match AEAT's *practice* of separate full-invoice series. Needs the asesor to confirm what's
   obligatory vs merely conventional.
4. **May a ticket be substituted more than once, and must all tickets be F2?** Recommend: **at most once**
   (a `unique(substituted_sale_id)` guard, else the same operation appears in two F3s) and **F2 only**
   (assert, like `correction_unsupported`). Also: refuse substituting a **voided** ticket (reuse
   `sale.voided`). Confirm.
5. **Settlement / payment coupling.** Recommend the F3 is recorded **unsettled with no new tender** (the
   money was collected on the F2; AEAT: «no cobrar dos veces», §1.4). Confirm this is right vs wanting
   the F3 to reflect the original payment (a Z-report "paid via the original ticket" question — bears on
   sub-project 8).
6. **Total consistency.** Should the F3's `total`/`lines` be **enforced** to equal the sum of the
   substituted tickets, or **trusted** (delta-in, as rectificativas do)? Recommend trust for v1, flag
   for the till UI to compute.
7. **Cross-SIF F3 and the asesor.** The cross-SIF inference (§1.6) and the `Destinatarios` XSD shape
   (§1.5) both want the (currently unengaged) asesor / the AEAT XSD before a real F3 is issued in anger.
