# A1635 · Refund from a paid receipt

Staff can choose Dishes, An amount or The tip, review where the refund goes, get approval and see
what happened. Use [design](../specs/2026-11-26-till-handheld-redesign-design.md) §§7.12–7.13, §13
and the five `bg` HTML frames. The spec introduction records the owner’s approval of the after-payment
write-up on 26 November. Lane I drafted this plan. The watcher clears the build and the owner lands it.

- [ ] **Dishes and destination.** Compose `wt-dialog full-screen`, `wt-full-screen-panel`,
  `wt-top-bar`, `wt-foot-button-bar` and `wt-menu-row` in `give-back-{dialog,lines,styles}.ts`.
  Extend `wt-choice-tile` with an optional description below its label and a trailing limit.
  In `give-back-read.ts` and the till’s `give-back-types.ts`, identify identical eligible portions
  using the filed dish, option and refund facts, including the VAT rate and remaining amount.
  Reuse `portionGroups` and the Pay for items stepper behaviour. Picking two portions sends two
  existing line IDs. Keep measured lines, unavailable lines and dishes with different options,
  prices or refund histories separate. Keep the Why picker, amount editing, draft and Back.
  Show each way’s limit from the refund read and describe the server preview’s destination and
  corrective type. Keep today’s
  print-later wording where no print is planned. The preview contains no confirmed printer.

  pending result. Keep the separate terminal, retry and refund-the-rest paths. Read the filed

  status and name. A failed read leaves the refund visible. Keep the write and filing paths.

  Keep that shortcut and those same destinations. Use today’s receipt if Lane O’s redesign is
  still in flight. Update English and Spanish refund wording and both `table-service.md` guides

approval and pending/completed results. Keep unsaved, retry and accessibility cases. Cover the receipt
