# Products category Delete window implementation plan

Implement PRODUCTS-LIST-4 from [§7.4, Delivery and Spanish](../specs/2026-11-24-products-list-redesign-design.md). Managers see what goes, what stays and where preparation moves before confirming. Reuse the modal, draft protection, Cancel, Delete, previews, checks and requests. The list and editor redesigns are later pieces. Claude reviewed the plan, and the watcher cleared it with five edits on 25 November at 03:54.

- [ ] **Title and choices.** Render in `product-category-delete.ts`, called by `product-browser.ts`. Use “Delete Cheeses?” (list its path only when it adds information), “Delete 3 categories?” over their paths, or “Delete Cheeses, Meats and Burger?” for a mixed selection. Keep numbered paths on both sides of differing destinations, using the nearest surviving parent and its resulting path. Say “Keep its 5 products, with no category” or “Keep its 2 categories and 5 products, in Drinks” (at the top level for categories without a parent). Count categories and products together and omit either if zero, with singular/plural ownership. Say “Delete its 5 products too”. Count the station routing rules removed under each choice, or under the title when no choice is needed. Update EN and ES in `i18n/strings.ts` and retire unused count keys.
- [ ] **Counts and checks.** Sum disjoint outermost summaries. Exclude directly selected categories and active products from additional counts. Keep removes selected categories’ own rules. Delete too removes the whole subtree’s rules. Default to Keep and omit the contents choice when nothing else goes, while still confirming the selected deletion. Preserve the immediate empty, rule-free delete, fresh-count and retry checks, menu and extras warnings and refusals.
- [ ] **Station changes.** Share the grouping and target names in `routing-preview.ts`. Prep stations keeps its current groups, by zone, period and pair. Category Delete groups by from/to pair and counts distinct products (variants by product when the preview identifies them). Use “All 5 will be made in Kitchen instead of Deli prep” only for a common station pair whose product ids exactly match the active products kept from the selection. For another pair, show “3 products will be made in …”; for no station, no preparation or no replacement use “From {from} to {to}”. Give an unqualified station line only when every product in the pair moves in every active zone with null period ids (null zone when the venue has none). Otherwise, including missing routing, say “in some zones or at some times” in both languages. The preview sends no row for a period with no change. Keep the no-change sentence without Details when there are no moves. Use the shared folded Details table and a flexible-height Delete modal. Keep Move unfolded and hide the old Delete preview while refreshing.
- [ ] **Tests and size.** `product-browser.test.ts` covers headings and one mixed title with two categories and a product, overlapping selections, different parents with numbered paths, no rules or contents lost, pair identity, and the “All” rule with both zones moving and one zone moving. Update the copy assertions while preserving the count, retry and refusal checks. Run its a11y and draft suites. `prep-stations-screen.test.ts` keeps the shared grouping cases. Run the file-size guard and lower the cap if required. Estimate 270 production lines added and 150 removed, and 155 test lines added and 45 removed.
- [ ] **Guide and screenshots.** Update `docs/developers/product-categories.md`, the Delete paragraph in `design-system.md`, both `apps/server/src/guide/{en,es}/products.md` guides and `docs/backlog.md`. Capture 1440/1180/390 px × light/dark × EN/ES, showing both choices, expanded Details and a long mixed selection. Send the captures to the watcher for the owner’s decision before landing.
