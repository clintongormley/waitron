**Goal:** Return money from a paid F1/F2 sale safely after its invoice, show actual completed refunds in Transactions and provide a payment acknowledgment, preserving the original invoice.

## Global Constraints

- **Payment only:** a correct charge can have money returned without fiscal effect. No `recordCorrection`, F1 correction eligibility change, invoice rewrite, new fiscal number/record/QR, bill reopening or collection change. A wrong charge's correction remains separate. Refund copy explicitly says the original invoice is unchanged.
- **Durable-attempt rule:** persist before any provider call; one stable key per attempt; interrupted outcomes recover by §6b evidence. Never use the old `refund`/`partialRefund`/`reverseViaStripe` send-and-record path. Do not fix the reconciler in this PR.
- Same `sale.refund` authorization, operator-first/PIN fallback, B14a manual-terminal PIN+confirmation, device tender capability and original-sale zones as pre-invoice refunds. Cash uses CLAUDE.md §5's separate audited drawer rule; printing never opens it.

## Review Focus

- Crash after provider acceptance but before recording must not send a second refund or release reserved money (Task 1).
- Concurrent keys, mixed cards, pre-invoice returns and partial tips cannot over-refund or count a return twice (Tasks 1–2).
- Revoked sessions/profiles/zones and manual PIN failures cannot bypass the pre-invoice authorization rules (Tasks 1, 3).
- Next-day/different-device refunds affect operational cash-up once and leave original VAT/frozen closes unchanged (Task 2).
- Lost UI answers, reopen/reload and late reads retain the attempt identity and never imply a fiscal correction (Tasks 3–4).
