# A1308 dv13: A4 printing runs through `print_jobs` — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended)
> or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`)

**Goal:** Every print goes through `print_jobs`, A4 included (owner, 2026-11-04). An A4 invoice
becomes a job of kind `a4` on its printer, with no payload (the pages are drawn when the agent asks),
and its claim, lease and printer hold live on the job. The delivery record stops being written as a
queue for A4; dv14 then drops its queue columns for every medium. Measurement M6 is this slice's
test.

**Spec:** [README](../specs/2026-11-02-rationalisation/README.md) §5 U4; [tables-devices.md](../specs/2026-11-02-rationalisation/tables-devices.md)
§1.7 ("A4 printing, on this table", the CHECKs), §6 item 7, §8 M6; [tables-ordersales.md](../specs/2026-11-02-rationalisation/tables-ordersales.md)
§1.19. Rules: [slice rules](2026-11-08-a1308-slice-rules.md). Area order:
[dv1](2026-11-08-a1308-dv1-devices-row.md).

| Change | Rule |

dv11's `claim_token_hash` and the existing `claimed_by`, `claimed_at`, `attempts`, `status` serve
`a4` jobs as they serve the others.

**The A4 path on the job** (`tables-devices.md` §1.7, item by item):
- *Queue:* `reserveInvoiceDelivery`'s `a4` branch (`apps/server/src/invoice-delivery.ts:25-31`,
  `:148-165`) inserts a `print_jobs` row (`kind: 'a4'`, the chosen printer, `sale_id`, no payload)
  and the delivery names it through `print_job_id`, as a receipt does. A printer disabled after
  staging still gives a delivery recorded `failed` with `printer_disabled` and no job (`:70-83`);
  that is why the delivery's `printer_id` stays.
- *Claim:* `claimInvoicePagePrint` (`apps/server/src/invoice-page-print.ts:42-110`) keeps its rules
  and reads `print_jobs`: oldest first, only this agent's printers (`printers.agent_id`), one A4 out
  per printer (busy = a job of kind `a4` `printing` on it), a due job on a disabled printer fails
  (job `failed`, no attempts left; delivery `failed`, `printer_disabled`) instead of being handed
  out. It sets the job `printing`, `claimed_by`, `claimed_at`, `claim_token_hash`, `attempts + 1`, and
  the delivery `sending`. It no longer writes the delivery's claim columns or `printer_hold`.
  `print_jobs_pull_idx (printer_id, status)` serves it.
- *Renew:* `renewInvoicePagePrint` (`:116-138`) moves the job's `claimed_at` and writes its
  `printer_hold`; the 60-second lease stays.
- *Report:* `reportInvoicePagePrint` (`:144-168`) checks the claim on the job; the delivery records
  the outcome through `reportInvoiceDelivery` with its rules (history after a newer generation,
  `unknown` kept unless sent); the job ends `done`, or `failed` with no attempts left. `a4` jobs are
  never resent (`canResendPrintJob` takes only `document`, `packages/printing/src/outbox.ts:75-91`).
- *Lapse:* `expireInvoiceDeliveryClaims` ends an `a4` job whose `claimed_at` lapsed: job `failed`, no
  attempts left, `printer_hold` kept; delivery `unknown`, `timeout`, `expired_at`. dv12's invoice mail
  `sweep` (`due` and `run`) reads the job's lapse for `a4` deliveries, so a held A4 whose agent stopped
  renewing still turns uncertain on the server's pass (`alert-sources.invoice-delivery.test.ts:652-694`).
- *Due:* an `a4` job is due when `queued`; it is never retried automatically (§1.7,
  "`next_attempt_at` is not needed here"); dv11's wait rule takes only `document` jobs.
- *The agent's messages* (owner told, 2026-11-04): the `/print-api/agent/page-jobs` routes stay. Today
  `PageJob`'s `id` is the delivery's id and it carries `generation` and `token`
  (`packages/print-agent/src/client.ts:122-137`); after this slice its `id` is the job's id and
  `generation` leaves (`{ id, token, printer }`). The server's `readClaim`
  (`apps/server/src/page-print-api.ts:82-95`) reads a token; its in-memory `claimed` map is keyed by
  job id and token hash. **Server-side, the claim stays an `InvoiceDeliveryClaim`**
  (`{ deliveryId, generation, token, holder }`, `invoice-delivery.ts:168-173`): the routes find the
  delivery through the job's `print_job_id` and build it, so `invoice-page-print.ts`'s functions keep
  their signatures. A test page stays in memory (`deps.testPages`), because it has no sale.
- *Lists:* the Printers screen's queue (`apps/server/src/print-api.ts:1422-1500`) leaves out `a4` jobs
  (`ne(printJobs.kind, "a4")`), as `printJobInTrouble` already leaves out non-documents. The alert
  sources' office-printer reads (`apps/server/src/alert-sources.ts:494-515`) read the hold and the
  printer from the delivery's job.
- `insertPrintJob` (`packages/printing/src/outbox.ts:35-73`) takes an `a4` kind with no payload, on an
  `a4` printer only; a `document` or `drawer` job on an `a4` printer keeps `printer.not_found` (dv3).

## Decisions this plan makes

1. **The delivery's queue columns stay one more slice**, unwritten for A4 (dv14 drops them with the
   readers that remain: email's due time, the status read's times). Splitting keeps each pull request
   under the cap (coordinator's review, 2026-11-08).
2. **Assertions that change** (owner-announced protocol change): the expected page-job wire bodies
   and parsed `PageJob`s in `packages/print-agent/src/client.test.ts`, `agent.test.ts`,
   `apps/server/src/page-print-api.test.ts` and `page-print-agent-e2e.test.ts` (the `id` is the job's;
   no `generation`). `invoice-page-print.test.ts` asserts the server-side claim
   `{ deliveryId, generation, token, holder }` about fifteen times (`:225`, `:406`, `:408`,
   `:510-511`, `:598`, …): those **stay**, since the server-side claim keeps its shape. Every other
   `expect` stays; `alert-sources.invoice-delivery.test.ts:652-694` keeps its assertions, its
   row-reading helper re-pointed to read `printer_hold` from the job.
