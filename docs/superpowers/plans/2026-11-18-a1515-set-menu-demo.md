# Set menus, piece 5: the demo's set menus and its courses — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Madrid demo sells two set menus at lunch, a Menú del día and a Menú especial that
share dishes, and every restaurant dish in it has a course, with Drinks, Bread and Coffee courses
added, so a person trying the demo can order set menus at a table end to end.

**Architecture:** Data first, in the Spain pack (`packages/country-es/src/demo/`): courses on
categories and products, the added courses, and the two set menus. The demo seed
(`apps/server/scripts/demo-seed/`) creates the added courses and the Fire now rows, gives each
product its course, and builds the set menus through piece 1's operations. The lunch staff menu is
kept as the ordinary menu that holds them, because a period cannot sell a set menu directly (piece
1, Task 2). The Madrid baseline test (`seed.madrid-baseline.test.ts`), which compares the seeded
demo against a frozen copy row by row, is told about every change through its one existing
whole-database declaration, `madridWithSharedOffers`, which this piece extends. A first, separate
task checks the spec's claim that a Fire now course raises no reminders.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 7, drizzle, the demo seed scripts in `apps/server`, Vitest 4.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-set-menus-design.md](../specs/2026-11-17-set-menus-design.md),
sections 6 "Courses for drinks, bread and coffee" and 7 "Demo and guides", and piece 5 of "The
pieces, for planning". Line numbers below are from `54dd6458f8`.

**Order:** last of the five pieces. It uses piece 1's set-menu writes (`createMenu` with
`kind: "set_menu"`, `addSetMenuDishes`, `saveSetMenuLines`, `saveSetMenuVariantChoices`,
`updateMenuItem` for a surcharge, the `{ kind: "set_menu", menuId }` member, `readSetMenu`),
piece 1's `listZoneOffers(...).setMenus`, piece 2's `setMenuOfferOf` and piece 3's `fitSetMenus`.
Task 1 depends on nothing and may land on its own, earlier. Tasks 2a–6 land after pieces 1–4, so
the demo never offers a set menu the till cannot sell, pay or print.

**Review:** read once against the spec by a fresh-context reviewer on 18 November 2026, which ran
`madridWithSharedOffers` on hand-built tables and otherwise read and grepped. Its findings are
applied: one baseline declaration instead of a second helper that could never run after the first;
starter courses found by any of their names; the holder's rename moved to the seeding task; Task 1's
server cases no longer claim a deletion check they cannot fail; Tasks 2 and 5 split; Tapas, the
house bread's real category, the beer, the cola and the coffee variant named exactly.

## Decisions this plan makes (the builder's, for the owner)

Marked **my call** where they first appear in a task.

1. **The set menus sit in the lunch staff menu, renamed "Set menus" («Menús»).** That ordinary
   menu stays the Lunch period's staff menu (13:00–16:00, `seed-floor.ts:192-196,214`). Its Starters
   and Mains sections go; its Drinks folder and its lunch price for the Negroni (9.00) stay; the two
   set menus are added as folders. Placing them in Casa Delgado instead would sell them at dinner
   too, since Dinner sells Casa Delgado (`seed-floor.ts:197-201`).
2. **The five lunch dishes stay products, but no longer on an à la carte menu.** These are salad,
   gazpacho, lentils, roast chicken and battered hake. They are offered only inside the set menus
   (owner, 17 November: set-menu plates are separate entries from their à la carte twins). Their
   product prices stay as they are and show nowhere at the till. Roast chicken keeps its paid Sides
   extras and Sirloin its Doneness question. So the demo shows the owner's original point: a dish
   in a set menu asks its own questions and takes its own extras.
3. **The two set menus,** both at the reduced (10%) class. Products are named by their image:
   - **Menú del día, €14.00.** Lines: 1 × Starters, 1 × Mains, 1 × Desserts or Coffee, 1 × Bread,
     1 × Drinks: the owner's "primero, segundo, postre o café, pan y bebida". Dishes:
     `ensalada-mixta`, `gazpacho`, `lentejas`; `pollo-asado`, `merluza-rebozada`, and `solomillo`
     at +€5.00; `flan`, `arroz-con-leche`; `cafe-solo` with its "Double coffee" variant left out;
     `pan-de-la-casa`; `vino-tinto`, `agua-mineral`, `cana-cerveza`, `refresco-cola`.
   - **Menú especial, €20.00.** The same lines, except that the second is 1 × Mains or Starters
     (the owner's one-way swap). Dishes: every del día dish, `solomillo` included and both coffee
     variants, plus `bacalao`, `merluza`, `crema-catalana` and `tarta-de-santiago`.
   - **The worked example.** Four set menus with four starters, two sirloins, one cod and one
     battered hake fit as 3 del día + 1 especial = €72.00. That is the spec's worked example
     (section 3), and Task 5a tests it. The reviewer checked every split of the four between the
     two types.
4. **Courses.** Each restaurant dish gets the course of its kind:
   - Tapas and Sharing plates → Sharing plates;
   - the lunch starters → Starters;
   - Mains → Mains;
   - Desserts → Desserts;
   - cocktails, wine and beer, and soft drinks → Drinks;
   - coffee → Coffee;
   - the house bread (`pan-de-la-casa`, in Tapas) → Bread.

   The regional dishes get the course of their kind too, chosen by the implementer and listed in
   the commit message. The deli's charcuterie, cheeses and conserves get none: they are sold at the
   counter, where courses do nothing.
5. **The added courses are the demo's, not Spain's.** The demo seed creates Coffee, Drinks and
   Bread after Spain's four starter courses, in that order. They are named in the same language as
   those four, which is the venue's setup language, not the seed's (`provisioning.ts:29,70`). A new
   Spanish venue still starts with the four (`packages/country-es/src/spain.ts:332-337`), and the
   guide tells it how to add the others (spec section 6: "a venue adds"). Drinks and Bread are Fire
   now in both departments. Coffee is held, as Desserts are (spec section 6).
6. **No seeded past sale includes a set menu.** The demo's back-dated sales are fiscal records
   written straight through `seedSales`. Faking set-menu bills there would bypass pieces 2 and 4.
7. **The demo now holds mains and desserts at every table, dinner included.** This follows from
   giving dishes courses, which the spec asks for (section 7): Spain's Mains and Desserts are "held
   until fired". So a demo table's mains wait for **Fire**, where today every dish is sent at once.
   The guide's demo paragraph says so (Task 6).

## Review Focus

1. **The setup language differs from the seed language.** A Spanish venue's starter courses are
   named in Spanish even when the demo is seeded in English; the baseline expects Spanish names in
   both of its runs (`seed.madrid-baseline.test.ts:1912-1918`). A lookup by the seed's language
   would throw in every English seed. Task 2b tests both pairings.
2. **The regional demos.** Catalonia, Valencia and Galicia spread Madrid's menus and add a "House
   specialities" section (`casa-delgado-ca.ts:130-155`). A regional dish without a course, or a
   set-menu dish image a regional data set lacks, must fail a data test, not the seed at runtime.
   Tasks 2a and 4 run their data tests over every Spanish data set.
3. **A set menu has no published version of its own.** The seed publishes every id in `menuIds`
   (`seed.ts:102-105`); publishing a set menu is refused `set_menu.wrong_place` (piece 1, decision
   3). Task 5a keeps the set menus' ids apart from `menuIds` and tests that both are served through
   the holder's published version.
4. **The seated bar tables.** The downstairs bar takes counter orders but also has tables B1–B3
   (`packages/country-es/src/demo/floor.ts:156-158`). A set menu belongs on a seated table's bill
   there, and never on a counter order (spec section 5, owned by piece 2). Task 6's look checks
   both.
5. **The deli department's courses.** `seedFloor` copies the restaurant department's course rows
   into the deli (`seed-floor.ts:76-87`). Fire now rows written after that copy would be missing
   from the deli, and the spec says every department. Task 2b's test reads both departments.
