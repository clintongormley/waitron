## Rulings made by the planner (for the plan reviewer)

- **R1 — Keep the box in the page and hide it; do not reuse the handheld's step loop.** The design
  says the counter "measures its till strip, as the handheld's does". The handheld finds room by
  trying: it steps back, re-creates the brand mark, and measures (`:674-691`). On a counter that
  fails twice: a re-created box is 0 px wide until its image's `load` event (`wt-venue-logo.ts`,
  `ratio` 0 until `#loaded`), so the try measures an empty box, shows it, and then takes it out
  again once the image loads — the strip jumping 108 px and back; and the counter's counts change
  all through service, which is exactly why the handheld owes no step-back on `transferCount`
  (`:49-50`). Keeping one loaded box and computing room arithmetically avoids both. This is the
  plan's one departure from the design's wording, recorded in the design by Task 6.
- **R2 — The room rule.** `room = logoWidth > 0 && content + logoWidth + gap <= stripWidthWithoutLogo`,
  where `logoWidth` is the box's `getBoundingClientRect().width` (the same in and out of the row,
  since its width comes from its own height and the image's shape); `gap` is `.head`'s computed
  `column-gap`; `content` is the span of the strip's children (`max(right) − min(left)`, which a
  sideways scroll does not change); and `stripWidthWithoutLogo` is the strip's
  `getBoundingClientRect().width`, plus `logoWidth + gap` while the box is in the row. A box still
  loading, or failed, is 0 px wide and so has no room: it never pushes the strip by the gap.
- **R3 — What is observed.** One `ResizeObserver` (`#counterObserver`) on the host (window and
  container width), the logo box (its image loads, fails, or swaps light/dark), `.session` (the name
  and the Help switch render after the shell), and each child of `.till-strip` (labels, counts,
  locale, fonts, text size). None of these changes size when the logo goes in or out, so the
  observer cannot loop; the strip itself is **not** observed, because its width is what the logo
  changes. Re-synced after every counter render (disconnect, observe the current set). Also measured
  after every counter render once `#settled()` resolves.
- **R4 — What the place shows by venue-logo state.** `none`: nothing (as today). `waiting` (no stored
  copy, no answer yet): nothing — a counter with no logo draws nothing, so there is no Waitron mark to
  swap away, which is what section 12's empty box exists to avoid on the other surfaces. `logo`
  (loaded or failed): the box, with **no** fallback child, `alt` = `legalName`, `light`/`dark` as the
  handheld's (`:699-707`) and the same `reportVenueLogoFailure` / `reportVenueLogoLoaded` handlers. A
  failed box is 0 px wide, so it is out of the row by R2 and retries itself every 60 s; no shell timer.
- **R5 — Held while the zone menu is open.** While `wt-row-actions[data-counter-zones]`'s popup is
  open, a measurement keeps the current `counterLogoRoom`; the popup's `toggle` to closed measures
  again. Mirrors "Opening More holds its contents steady until it closes" (`design-system.md:5489-5490`).
  The ☰ and the name's menu sit in `.session`, which never moves (R8), so they need no hold.
- **R6 — Where the line goes in the counter's ☰.** Last, after Log out and a rule, as on the
  handheld. When the Testing group shows (Demo or Preparation installs only, `:1008-1015`), the line
  still comes last, after Testing: the backlog entry's title says "at the foot", the handheld's line
  is the menu's last child (`till-shell.powered-by.test.ts`, "comes last, after Log out and a rule"),
  and on every live till Testing is absent, so the line is directly under Log out as the mock-ups
  draw it.
- **R7 — Fixed outcomes only where CI's fonts cannot flip them.** The design's eight bars are all
  tested against the rule (R2) and the no-move checks (R8). A fixed outcome is asserted only where
  the margin is wide or one-sided: the four quiet bars show the logo (343-658 px free against 108);
  the two busy 1024 bars leave it out (54 and −11 px free; a larger font only lowers that). The two
  busy 1280 bars assert the rule and log the outcome (macOS: shown). Deterministic both-sides
  controls come from widths computed at run time (Task 1's boundary cases).
- **R8 — "No work control ever moves or shrinks for it", as tested.** While the logo shows: the strip
  does not scroll (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth`); every strip child is as wide as on the same bar with
  no logo (±0.5 px); the `.session` group's box is where it is with no logo (±0.5 px); nothing moves
  into a menu. The strip's controls do shift right by the logo's width and the gap while it shows —
  the design's pictures (`21-logo-till-*`, `t2-till-menu-*`) draw exactly that, and its trade-off
  ("the logo can come and go as the counts appear", line 1485) accepts it. The plan reviewer should
  confirm this reading.
- **R9 — Name for a screen reader.** `alt` = the shell's `legalName`, which the app fills from
  `issuer.venueName` (`till-app.ts:19501`) for every form factor — the same value the handheld's bar
  and the sign-in use (design line 1464).
