**Words used in this plan.**
- A **notice** is one staff notice as slice 4's reader returns it (`OwnedStaffNotice`,
  `apps/server/src/staff-notices.ts:16-18`): `owner`, `key`, `code`, `severity`, `params`, no words.
- The **strip** is the one line under the top bar of every signed-in till and handheld that shows
  the most serious notice (design §4.2, picture 2).
- The **equipment read** is `GET /api/device/equipment` (`apps/server/src/device-api.ts:382-389`),
  which a signed-in till or handheld sends at sign-in and then every 15 s
  (`apps/till/src/state/equipment-poll.ts:4`; started at `apps/till/src/till-app.ts:5005`, first
  read at `:5006`).
- The **stop** is slice 3's full-disk stop, `DiskFullState` (`apps/server/src/disk-full-state.ts:6-22`):
  "noted" means `since()` is not null.
- The **warning** is slice 5's low-space state (`FreeSpaceState.current().lowSince`,
  `apps/server/src/free-space-watch.ts:13-23`): on below 2 GiB, off at 2.5 GiB.
- A **sighting** is the once-a-minute `devices.last_seen_at` write a device-authenticated request
  makes (`readDeviceProof`, `apps/server/src/device-session.ts:242-258`).
- The **unsent count** is the Veri\*Factu module's blue notice, "12 sales not yet sent to the tax
  agency · Retried automatically" (design §4.2).

**Goal:** every signed-in till and handheld shows, under its top bar, the most serious thing wrong
with the box that staff should know — the red "Nothing can be saved", the red "Sales can't be
recorded", the amber "The box is nearly out of space", the blue unsent count — with "+N" when more
are waiting and the list on a tap; the box works these out on the equipment read it already
answers, and the read keeps answering on a full disk.

**Architecture:** five pieces.
1. `@waitron/module`'s `StaffNoticeReadContext` gains an optional `filing` fact, and the Veri\*Factu
   module's one staff-notice source answers the unsent count beside its clock notice.
2. `apps/server` gains the box's own source (`storage.disk_full` / `storage.space_low`), a quieter
   log for the reader, and puts `notices` and `noticesUnavailable` on the equipment read's answer.
   Boot hands the route the box's source first and the enabled modules' after it, the till's
   clock, and the filing facts.
3. A device's proof no longer fails a request when its sighting is refused for a full disk, so the
   equipment read answers on a full disk (M6).
4. The till keeps the notices of each equipment read in the order the reads were sent, and draws
   them in a new `till-notice-strip`, slotted into `till-shell` between the header and the body.
5. The station screen says why a bump was refused on a full disk; a root guard holds every
   notice code to till words; a guide page, the developer docs and the backlog follow.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Hono, `node:sqlite` behind drizzle, Lit, Vitest 4.1.11 (Node for
`apps/server`, `packages/fiscal-verifactu` and the root guards; real headless Chromium for
`apps/till`).

## Departures from the design

1. **The unsent count leaves out records AEAT refused and records set aside.** Design §4.2: "It
   counts every record not yet accepted". This slice counts the records the drain will send again
   by itself — `pendiente`, `enviando`, and `detenido`, which a held chain's hourly probe sends
   (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/drain.ts:994-1031`) **(read)** — and not `rechazado` (AEAT has
   answered it) or `retenido` (set aside for a person, never sent, `drain.ts:860-870`) **(read)**.
   Why: the notice says "Retried automatically", which is false for those two; each already has a
   manager alert and a filing case (`submission-alerts.ts:48-69`, `filing-cases.ts`). Recorded in
   the backlog by Task 12.
2. **The strip draws no tinted background.** Picture 2 tints each strip red, amber or blue. No
   token holds those tints and `color-mix()` is refused (M8), so the strip takes wt-banner's shape
   (`packages/ui/src/components/wt-banner.ts:16-21`): the raised surface, an inline-start edge and
   the icon in the tone's colour (decision 14). Adding tint tokens is design-system work with its
   own contrast checks; Task 12 records it.
3. **A device's proof changes (auth).** Not in the design: M6 shows the equipment read fails on a
   full disk, which the design assumes it does not. Task 3 changes only what happens when the
   sighting write is refused for a full disk. This adds the **auth** risk trigger to the slice.

## Decisions this plan makes that the spec does not

Each is a DEFAULT to build; the owner or the plan review may overrule any.

1. **The notices ride on the equipment read only, as two new keys.** `GET /api/device/equipment`
   answers `{ roles, notices, noticesUnavailable }`: `notices` is the reader's list, in its order,
   each with `owner`, `key`, `code`, `severity`, `params`; `noticesUnavailable` is the reader's
   `unavailable` (owners whose source failed). `PUT /api/device/equipment` (`device-api.ts:392-408`)
   and `readDeviceEquipment` (`device-equipment.ts:206`) are unchanged: the PUT is a person's
   choice, not the 15-second read, and leaving it alone keeps its answer `DeviceEquipment`
   (`:62`). With no notice dependencies mounted (every existing test mount), the GET answers
   `notices: []`, `noticesUnavailable: []`. No new route, so no profile-action or zone map row
   (`till-api.profile-zones.test.ts:229` already lists this route as naming no zone).
2. **Worked out inside the read's own transaction, from one clock reading.** The GET runs
   `readDeviceEquipment` and then `readStaffNotices` in the one `withTransaction` it already opens
   (`device-api.ts:385-387`); M2 shows such a read answers on a full disk. The reading is one
   `clock.now()` of the clock boot gives the till routes (`tillClock = systemClock()`,
   `apps/server/src/boot.ts:1655`), so the notice judges the clock exactly as slice 3's refusal does
   (`issueMoment`, `apps/server/src/issue-moment.ts:21-23`). M3 puts the unsent count at about 2 ms
   for 18 000 waiting records, under the write lock, per device per 15 s.
3. **The box's own source goes first, under the owner `box`.** Boot passes
   `[{ owner: "box", source: boxStaffNoticeSource(…) }, ...enabledStaffNoticeSources(setsToMigrate)]`.
   The reader orders by severity and then by source order (`staff-notices.ts:66`; slice 4 decision
   6), so "two reds at once: the disk's shows first" (design §4.2) holds. `box` is no module's name
   (M8).
4. **The box's source: `storage.disk_full` while the stop is noted, otherwise `storage.space_low`
   while the warning is on.** Both carry `params: {}` — the strip's words name no figure. It reads
   `disk.since()` and `space.current()`, never the disk, so a read costs nothing and a stop is shown
   from the moment it is noted. The amber is left out while the red shows, as the bell does
   (`storageAlertSource`, `apps/server/src/alert-sources.ts:310-342`; slice 5 decision 3): "nearly
   out of space" is false while nothing can be saved.
5. **The unsent count joins the Veri\*Factu module's existing source.** `FISCAL_STAFF_NOTICES` stays
   one source (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/staff-notices.ts:18-20`), which now answers the clock
   notice and then the unsent count. One source keeps slice 4's pins unedited
   (`staff-notices.test.ts`, "the seat's one source…", `toHaveLength(1)`;
   `packages/composition/src/composition.test.ts:326-333`), and slice 8 adds its clock-behind
   notice to the same source, where design §4.2's "the clock's own notice rather than the
   behind-the-last-sale one" lives. Cost: if the count's query fails, the clock notice of that read
   goes with it (decision 8 keeps the last one shown). Code `fiscal.records_unsent`, severity
   `notice`, `params: { count }`, key `fiscal.records_unsent`.
6. **When the count shows.** Only when the context's `filing.enabled` is true, at least one record
   waits (`envios.estado` in `pendiente`, `enviando`, `detenido`; Departure 1), and either one of
   those waiting records has `incidencia` set or `filing.awaitingCredential` is true. `incidencia`
   is set on a waiting record by a failed send's back-off (`backoffBatch`, `drain.ts:1093-1110`; and
   `:1560-1563`), by putting a stalled claim back (`:491-515`), and on every held record (`:838`),
   and is never cleared (`setEstado` ORs it in, `:1604`) **(read)**. A record waiting out AEAT's own
   wait between sends (`drain.ts:182-183`, `:214-218`) has none, so the count does not show then,
   as design §4.2 asks. A restart that puts a stalled claim back raises `incidencia` too, so the
   count can show after a restart cut a send short; the design's "from the first failed send
   attempt" is read to include an interrupted one. It counts every waiting record, those queued
   after the failure too, "until no record waits".
7. **The filing facts come from the host, as an optional context field.** `StaffNoticeReadContext`
   gains `readonly filing?: { readonly enabled: boolean; readonly awaitingCredential: boolean }`
   (slice 4 decision 3 left exactly this for slice 6). Absent, the count answers nothing — so every
   existing call that passes `{ tx, reading }` is unchanged. Boot fills it on each read:
   `enabled = fiscalDrainEnabled(config) && holders.singletonRole.current === "primary"`, the
   expression the certificate route already uses (`boot.ts:1946`), so a demo or prepare box shows
   no count (`onboarding-policy.ts:12-16`); `awaitingCredential = awaitingFiscalCert.current`
   (`boot.ts:1473`; written by `runPass`, `apps/server/src/pass.ts:51-58`, `:78-87`). The names are
   generic on purpose: the module contract names no regime.
8. **A failed check: the device keeps what that owner last said, and shows nothing new.** When
   `noticesUnavailable` names an owner, the till keeps that owner's notices from its previous read
   and drops every other owner's old ones; on the first read after sign-in there is nothing to keep.
   Why: the unsent count is a legal duty while records wait (design §6 default 8), and a failing
   query is no reason to stop showing it; staff can do nothing about the failure itself, and design
   §4.2 gives it no words, so no line says "a check failed". The manager learns of it from the log
   (decision 9), which the Diagnóstico page tails (design §2.8).
9. **The reader's log lines are limited at the route, and `readStaffNotices` is not edited.** The
   route hands the reader a logger from `createStaffNoticeLog(log)` (new, in
   `apps/server/src/staff-notices.ts`), built once per `mountDeviceApi`. It passes every event
   through except: `staff_notice.source_unavailable`, written only when its `owner` was not already
   failing (one line per failing spell, whatever the devices and reads); `staff_notice.duplicate_key`,
   written once per `owner` and `key` for the life of the process. After each read the route calls
   `settle(unavailable)`, which writes `staff_notice.source_recovered` (info, `{ owner }`) for each
   owner that was failing and no longer is. Not logged again: a change of `errorCode` inside a
   failing spell. Today's reader logs on every read (`staff-notices.ts:56`, `:62`); slice 4's tests
   of that (`staff-notices.test.ts` cases 2, 4, 6) stay as they are, since the reader still does.
10. **A root guard holds every notice code to till words: `scripts/staff-notice-codes.test.ts`.**
    Modelled on `scripts/ongoing-alert-codes.test.ts:1-42`: it reads `code: "…"` literals in a listed
    set of files (`apps/server/src/box-staff-notices.ts`, `packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/staff-notices.ts`)
    and requires `staff_notice.<code>` in the till's `en` and `es` (`apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`).
    Weaker than its name: it reads text, only those files, and only that literal shape.
11. **`clock.unusable`'s notice has its own words, not the refusal's.** The design table gives it
    "Sales can't be recorded: the box's clock is wrong · Tell a manager"; the refusal sentence
    (`apps/till/src/i18n/codes.ts:762-765`) tells staff what to do about a sale already refused and
    a card already charged, which is wrong on a strip seen by everyone. The notice's params
    (`clockAt`, `offsetMinutes`) are carried but not shown (concise product text, design §6 default 5).
12. **The words.** In `apps/till/src/i18n/strings.ts`, English then Spanish, from design §4.2's table:
