**Words used in this plan.**
- A **notice** is one staff notice as slice 4's reader returns it (`OwnedStaffNotice`,
  `apps/server/src/staff-notices.ts:16-18`): `owner`, `key`, `code`, `severity`, `params`, no words.
- The **strip** is the one line under the top bar of every signed-in till and handheld that shows
  the most serious notice (design §4.2, picture 2).
- The **equipment read** is `GET /api/device/equipment` (`apps/server/src/device-api.ts:382-389`),
  which a signed-in till or handheld sends at sign-in and then every 15 s
  (`apps/till/src/state/equipment-poll.ts:4`; started at `apps/till/src/till-app.ts:5005`, first
  read at `:5006`).
- The **stop** is slice 3's full-disk stop, `DiskFullState` (`apps/server/src/disk-full-state.ts:6-22`):
  "noted" means `since()` is not null.
- The **warning** is slice 5's low-space state (`FreeSpaceState.current().lowSince`,
  `apps/server/src/free-space-watch.ts:13-23`): on below 2 GiB, off at 2.5 GiB.
- A **stamp** is the once-a-minute `devices.last_seen_at` save a device-authenticated or
  session-checked request makes (`readDeviceProof`, `apps/server/src/device-session.ts:242-258`;
  `requireSession`, `apps/server/src/till-session.ts:116-118`).
- A **turn** is one `db.withWriteLock(…)` body: the store's write lock, `begin immediate` to
  `commit`, with no change-log drain (`withTransaction` is a turn plus the drain,
  `packages/db/src/tenancy.ts:33-43`).
- A **waiting change** is a `change_log` row no transaction has drained yet.
- The **unsent count** is the Veri\*Factu module's blue notice, "12 sales not yet sent to the tax
  agency · Retried automatically" (design §4.2).

**Goal:** every signed-in till and handheld shows, under its top bar, the most serious thing wrong
with the box that staff should know — the red "Nothing can be saved", the red "Sales can't be
recorded", the amber "The box is nearly out of space", the blue unsent count — with "+N" when more
are waiting and the list on a tap; the box works these out on the equipment read it already
answers, and that read, and the till's session checks, keep answering on a full disk.

**Architecture:** five pieces.
1. `@waitron/module`'s `StaffNoticeReadContext` gains an optional `filing` fact, and the Veri\*Factu
   module's one staff-notice source answers the unsent count beside its clock notice, from the
   marks the drain itself leaves on a failed pass.
2. `apps/server` gains the box's own source (`storage.disk_full` / `storage.space_low`), a quieter
   log for the reader, and a second reader entry point that asks each source in a turn of its own,
   refusing a source that changes a row. The equipment read answers `notices` and
   `noticesUnavailable`, reading the equipment in one turn and the notices in theirs, with no drain.
   Boot hands the route the box's source first and the enabled modules' after it, the till's
   clock, and whether this box files.
3. A refused stamp no longer fails a request, in `readDeviceProof` and in `requireSession`, so the
   equipment read and every session-checked read answer on a full disk (M6, M10).
4. The till keeps the notices of each equipment read in the order the reads were sent, and draws
   them in a new `till-notice-strip`, slotted into `till-shell` between the header and the body.
5. The station screen says why a bump was refused on a full disk; a root guard holds every notice
   code to till words; two guide pages, the developer docs and the backlog follow.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Hono, `node:sqlite` behind drizzle, Lit, Vitest 4.1.11 (Node for
`apps/server`, `packages/fiscal-verifactu` and the root guards; real headless Chromium for
`apps/till`).

## Departures from the design

1. **The unsent count leaves out records AEAT refused.** Design §4.2: "It counts every record not
   yet accepted, voids and corrections too". The house reading of art. 16.4 is a count "mientras
   quede alguno de estos por remitir" (`docs/compliance/verifactu-findings.md:368`). A `rechazado`
   record has been sent and answered, and stays `rechazado` (`drain.ts:1448-1453`) **(read)**, so it
   is not "por remitir", the drain never sends it again, and "Retried automatically" would be false
   for it; it already has a filing case (`drain.ts:878`, `:1466`, `:1785`) and the manager's
   `fiscal.filing_cases_open` alert (`packages/fiscal-verifactu/src/submission-alerts.ts:64-73`)
   **(read)**. Held (`retenido`) records ARE counted, as the design says (the coordinator's ruling):
   they were never sent (`drain.ts:860-870`). The hint "Retried automatically" is not true of them
   — a person resolves their filing case — which Task 14 puts to the owner as a wording question.
2. **The strip draws no tinted background, and its words are not coloured.** Picture 2 tints each
   strip red, amber or blue and draws the red notice's words in red. No token is named for those
   tints and `color-mix()` is refused (M8). The strip takes wt-banner's surface, bottom border and
   inline-start edge (`packages/ui/src/components/wt-banner.ts:16-21`) and adds what wt-banner does
   not have: tones, and an icon, with the edge and the icon in the tone's colour (decision 14). The
   words stay `--wt-color-text`. Task 14 records the tint tokens as backlog.
3. **The device proof and the session check change (auth).** Not in the design: M6 and M10 show the
   equipment read and every session-checked read fail on a full disk once a stamp is due. Tasks 3
   and 4 change only what happens when the stamp is refused for a full disk. This adds the **auth**
   risk trigger.
4. **"Any refused save shows 'Not saved…'" (design §4.6 item 2) is checked on three surfaces only.**
   The draft save and the unpaid departure already show it (M4), the station screen gains it (Task
   10). The other till write surfaces were not audited; on a till or a handheld the red strip shows
   above whatever line they draw while the stop holds. Task 14 keeps an open entry for the audit.
5. **No strip on the clock-in and attendance pages.** `till-app` draws those pages instead of the
   shell (`apps/till/src/till-app.ts:18340-18394`) **(read)**, and the strip lives in the shell
   (decision 15). A signed-in person sees them briefly; Task 14 records it.

## Decisions this plan makes that the spec does not

Each is a DEFAULT to build; the owner or the plan review may overrule any.

1. **The notices ride on the equipment read only, as two new keys.** `GET /api/device/equipment`
   answers `{ roles, notices, noticesUnavailable }`: `notices` is the reader's list, in its order,
   each with `owner`, `key`, `code`, `severity`, `params`; `noticesUnavailable` is the reader's
   `unavailable` (owners whose source failed). `PUT /api/device/equipment` (`device-api.ts:392-408`)
   and `readDeviceEquipment` (`apps/server/src/device-equipment.ts:206`) are unchanged. With no
