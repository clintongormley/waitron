## Result, reuse and boundaries

Four HTTP paths gain regression checks: membership read, adjustment approver, refund override and manual-refund confirmer. Unknown, suspended and wrong-secret identities must receive the same complete refusal after exactly one scrypt derivation. A higher failure count on another address cannot displace a live password counter's owner.
Reuse `createPasswordThrottle`, its bounded hot map and fixed bucket arrays, fingerprint ownership, existing PIN delay/idle helpers, real venue fixtures and `testing/watched-scrypt.ts`. Keep the existing normalization, three free failures, doubling waits capped at 60 seconds, 15-minute idle expiry, in-flight exclusion and backward-clock policy.
No new map, capacity refusal, persisted state, protocol, migration, UI or support framework. Other backlog issues stay open: malformed-input log reasons, Connect cursor behavior, other override routes, PIN saturation and password-reset timing.
