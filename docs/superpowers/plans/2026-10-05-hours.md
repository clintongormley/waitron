## Owner choices required before the build

These are recommendations, not answers attributed to the owner. Approve or amend them before implementing dependent tests.

1. **Unset is different from Closed.** Recommend an unconfigured week read as seven `not_set` cells: “No hours set” for a department, “No hours restriction” for a nondefault station. A department then has no public opening claim; a station remains open. A configured week is a complete seven-day replacement with explicit Closed, Open all day or periods. Offer Configure hours and Clear schedule as whole-week actions. Configure opens a seven-day draft initialized Closed, states that unsupplied days will be Closed, and saves only after confirmation; Clear schedule restores all seven `not_set` cells. Do not offer `not_set` as a per-cell choice in a configured week. Reject mixed `not_set`/configured days, so an omitted day cannot accidentally open a configured station. Do not let an empty periods array mean either Closed or inheritance. This makes the current station `no_hours` behavior visible (`packages/venue-service/src/routing.ts:108`, inspected, not run). The extra unset presentation needs approval because §7 illustrates only periods and Closed.
2. **Calendar and overnight ownership.** Recommend real `YYYY-MM-DD` venue-local civil dates, not UTC dates or cutover-shifted business days. At Tuesday 00:30, evaluate Monday's overnight tail and Tuesday's own schedule. Tuesday Closed does not truncate Monday's tail; Monday's special override replaces Monday's full schedule, including its tail. Use half-open timed periods, start included/end excluded, duration below 24 hours; equal endpoints are refused. **Additional recommendation for approval:** an explicit `all_day` cell represents [00:00, next midnight), without equal-endpoint sentinel values; it publishes a positive 24-hour opening claim. It participates in adjacent-date overlap validation, so it cannot overlap the prior date's tail. Reject overlap across adjacent opening dates, including Sunday/Monday, rather than choosing a winning row. **Forward-validation recommendation:** validate current/future special dates and any prior tail entering today, leaving past rows out of future weekly-edit conflicts; the contract section defines this scope.
3. **Time zone and clock changes (DST).** Recommend recurring wall-clock membership: both occurrences of a repeated local minute have the same open/closed answer; skipped minutes cannot be evaluated, and an interval spanning the jump remains open at the first real local minute inside it. For date-specific periods, reject an endpoint that does not occur; where an endpoint repeats, explain before saving that both occurrences follow these hours. Store no UTC offset in a recurring cell. This is a proposed Hours/menu-period rule, distinct from choosing an occurrence for a one-off menu publication. W98's write-boundary clock-change validation must adopt the approved rule; if the owner chooses per-occurrence periods, revise the shared period type and tests before either build. Use runtime clock fixtures, not assertions about external holiday or time-zone policy.
4. **Business-day cutover.** Recommend keeping the existing manual open/closed override keyed to `station_day_states.business_day` and expiring at the cutover, while weekly/special schedules use civil opening dates. Precedence: inactive, active default, unreadable clock's existing permissive behavior, current business-day manual override, resolved hours. Calendar never changes report/close dates. At cutover a manual closure expires even if the prior civil date's overnight period remains open. Label that expiry on Prep stations; do not rename its storage key to calendar date.
5. **Changes and retention.** Recommend schedule edits affect subsequent route decisions immediately, never move already sent work. Keep schedules on rename/deactivation/reactivation. New department/station has `not_set` standard hours and inherits special-date cells; a venue-wide closure flag also closes a subsequently created department and nondefault station. When making a station default, retain its schedules and day overrides but ignore them; warn that demoting it resumes its saved schedule/current unexpired override. Show its disabled column and forbid writes server-side even from a stale editor. This recommendation deliberately preserves rows; approval must cover the possible closure on demotion.

   2026-10-06: the change is now refused when the kept hours would clash or use a time the clock skips (`assertDemotedStationHours`); the refusal names the special date to move or delete.
6. **Close whole venue and colour.** Recommend a date-level `closeWholeVenue` flag, rather than copying Closed to today's columns alone. It resolves every active department/nondefault station Closed, including subjects added later; default stays open. Show cells locked by this flag, retaining their saved overrides for reopening. Closed's fixed colour applies only if every active department is explicitly/resolved Closed; `not_set` is not Closed and partial closure is not Closed. A special date otherwise uses its chosen colour; an ordinary partially closed date stays standard. **Palette recommendation for approval:** use the mockup's six selectable palette keys (`red`, `amber`, `grey`, `blue`, `green`, `purple`), illustrated in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-venue-operations-mockups/hours-v3.html:53`. Reserve distinct standard/closed tokens outside that set. The wire/schema enumeration below is conditional on approval of this palette. This formalizes §7's inferred colour rule and closure behavior.
7. **Old schedules and reset.** Recommend replacing the two interval-only weekly stores with explicit cells/periods and retiring their tables after the UI switch. No converter or compatibility reader. A pre-live venue must be reset/reconfigured before using the changed schema; old schedules are not copied, and old configuration bundles are refused by the existing module-version check. This is a destructive schedule change needing explicit approval, not a measured necessity. Fresh/reset venues start unrestricted stations and unpublished department hours. State the reset consequence in the build PR's first line; this documentation change needs no reset.
8. **Single department.** Recommend showing its actual name on Hours, consistent with the named columns in §7. This page-specific choice needs approval; it does not settle A254's wider hiding/screen survey. Task 5's sole-department assertion is conditional on that approval.
9. **Permissions.** Recommend `venue.view` for Hours reads/navigation (including a supervisor's read-only calendar), `venue_service.manage` for all schedule/date writes. Read permission grants no edit, duplicate, delete, whole-venue closure or manual station override. Retain the existing manual-override write permission.
