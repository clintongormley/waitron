[Device home page design, piece 1](../specs/2026-11-24-device-home-page-design.md#delivery)

This covers shortcuts, the full menu, opened sections and search results on the till and in the dashboard Home page preview. Set-menu folders, the guest preview, search behaviour and editor settings are outside this piece.
