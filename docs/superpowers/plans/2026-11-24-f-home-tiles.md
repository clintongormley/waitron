# Till and dashboard tile plan

[Device home page design, piece 1](../specs/2026-11-24-device-home-page-design.md#delivery)

Staff can read the price of an ordinary product or its variant range on the till and in the dashboard Home page preview. They can recognise a section by its folder, including on a photo. This applies to shortcuts, the full menu, opened sections and search results. Set-menu folders, the guest preview, search behaviour, editor settings and published documents are outside this piece.

- Write and run the failing cases first. In `packages/shared/src/menu-document/device-home.test.ts`, pin a range from the variants on the menu, a single price when those considered are equal, the full range when all are unavailable, and the measured-unit suffix. In `packages/menu/src/menu-publication.test.ts`, prove the existing preview read carries a sold-out variant's ID while the document and its hash stay the same. In the till's `menu-browser.test.ts` and dashboard's `device-home-preview.test.ts`, pin the displayed price on the product tile and the real button's accessible name on a section with a photo. Update the existing `/ea` and visible Section checks with equally strict assertions of the new appearance. Keep the measured-unit and diet-filter notice checks. Record the changed assertions in the PR.
- Put one formatter in `packages/shared/src/menu-document/device-home.ts` and export it through `packages/shared/src/index.ts`. Accept a base price, variants with price and availability, the already-localised unit suffix or an empty suffix for Each, and the locale. Reuse `menuPriceRange` in `customer-menu-presentation.ts` by narrowing its parameter type to the price fields it already reads, and use `formatMoney`. When a priced variant is available, take the available subset. When none is, use all variants. Variants inherit their parent's pricing unit. In `apps/till/src/widgets/menu-browser.ts` use `productUnit` and `unitName` with the real Each ID. The preview's `apps/dashboard/src/widgets/device-home-preview.ts` already uses `unitPresentation`, so apply the same ID check there.
- The dashboard currently draws from a frozen offer. Collect unavailable variant IDs from the ordinary offers already read in `packages/menu/src/menu-document.ts` and send them in `previewMenu` in `packages/menu/src/menu-publication.ts`, as optional `unavailableVariantIds` on `MenuPreview` in `packages/shared/src/menu-document/menu-document-types.ts`. Omit the field when empty. Keep availability outside the published document and pass the field through `apps/dashboard/src/screens/menus-screen.ts` to the preview. Both surfaces consider all variant prices if the parent itself is unavailable. Reuse the current preview read and its invalidation.
- In the two tile renderers, keep the existing folder icon and put it on a token-styled badge on photo tiles. Keep the translated Section text visually hidden in the button's accessible name. The till's diet-filter notice stays visible. Rewrite the tile paragraph in `docs/developers/design-system.md`, update the English and Spanish Products and Table service guides, and record piece 1's completion in `docs/backlog/menu.md` alongside the remaining editor work.
- Run the four changed test files and both widgets' accessibility suites. Run the guide heading and link checks. Capture the dashboard Home page tab at 1440 px and the real till and handheld views at 1280 and 390 px. Cover English and Spanish, light and dark, Colours and Thumbnails, with and without shortcuts. Give the watcher the captures for the owner before landing. Keep captures outside git.

Estimate about 100 production lines, 100 test lines and 20 guide and contract lines. The local Lane D delete-menus branch also edits the dashboard preview. The queue names Lane C's tag and preview work and Lane D's later variant picker.
