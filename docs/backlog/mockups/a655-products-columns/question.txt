## Question 2 of 2 — the Spanish words for "Standalone"

Picture: 06-spanish-light.png

Today the column says «Pedido por separado» and the editor field «Pedir por separado». English
will be: column "Standalone", editor field and filter "Standalone ordering by", values Anyone /
Staff only / Nobody (Spanish: Cualquiera / Solo el personal / Nadie).

- **A (recommended) — column «Por separado», editor field and filter «Quién lo pide por
  separado».** Short heading; the field reads as a question the values answer ("Who orders it on
  its own? Anyone").
- **B — column «Suelto», editor field and filter «Quién lo pide suelto».** Shortest; more
  colloquial ("loose", as in "sold loose").
- **C — keep today's column «Pedido por separado»; editor field and filter «Pedido por separado
  por».** No new word to learn, but the heading stays long and the field reads awkwardly.
