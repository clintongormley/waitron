# A799: issued full invoice required; capture facts proposed

The owner requires an issued full customer invoice, F1, to preserve its original
customer name and address through upgrade before A799 closes. Harbour's Mara Soler,
18 Market Street example is invented. If the qualifying older product cannot issue
F1 normally, keep A799 open and identify the prerequisite item from evidence.

**Question E:** keep the IDs, invoice numbers and timestamps the frozen product
assigns, freezing them once with independent expected facts (recommended), or require
a fresh capture from scratch to produce identical initial values too?

Both choices reuse the same frozen bundle for every later upgrade comparison. Only
the creation of another historical bundle differs. Exact comparisons stay exact;
a new bundle gets separately reviewed expectations and never replaces an original.
The alternative needs an old-image-compatible mechanism whose feasibility is unverified.
