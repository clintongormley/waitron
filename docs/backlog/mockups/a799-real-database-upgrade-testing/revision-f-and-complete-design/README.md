## Design conditions carried into planning review

A760 execution and destructive reset keep separate authority. Qualifying capture and
candidate work stay within the same resulting post-reset migration history. A distinct
later candidate must actually apply pending migrations through its normal packaged
entry and boot. A same-image run is restart-only, and a later candidate without pending
migrations does not fill that required upgrade case.

Keep whole stopped-state capture, pinned product-path transcripts, independent
expectations frozen before candidate access, isolated disposable copies, exact typed
facts, relationships, schema/protections and raw fiscal bytes. Preserve the captured
node/SIF installation/series identity, next number and chain. Keep optional-module-off
cases: retained rows after a supported disable, or a separate never-enabled fixture
with retained-data coverage explicitly missing. The candidate never supplies expected
facts. Fiscal writers, golden huella and `inmutabilidad` remain unchanged.

Harbour's issued F1 to **Mara Soler, 18 Market Street** is invented. That same issued
invoice must keep its original recipient details even if the live customer changes.
If the qualifying old product cannot issue F1 normally, identify the prerequisite
from evidence and leave A799 open. An unpaid order, F2 receipt, restart, new sale,
fabricated F1, workaround or new writer cannot replace it. No F1 enablement is authorised.

Report preservation before a separate new sale on another preserved copy with the
same identity and chain. Yesterday's illustrative 0.350 kg cheese line at €22.00/kg
stays €7.70; a new line at €24.00/kg is €8.40. These are example calculations, not
observed outputs. Neither result compensates for the other or for missing issued F1.

The future plan must assess actual historical/candidate interfaces, safe identity
seams, qualifying image/platform availability, module paths, clocks, containment,
archive and resource/time budgets, and private CI downloads through existing access.
The proposed trusted fetch stage checks committed hashes and removes credentials
before candidate execution; this is not verified access or isolation. Missing,
corrupt, inaccessible or unsafe inputs and missing prerequisites block runtime work.
They do not permit seeds, skips, new permissions or weaker requirements. A genuine
additional product decision must return to the owner explicitly.
