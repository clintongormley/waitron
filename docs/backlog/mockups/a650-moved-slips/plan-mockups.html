A650 · 5. The slips after the owner's rulings MOCKUP

What the kitchen printer prints under the build plan. Every case in English at 80 mm (42 columns) and Spanish at 58 mm (30 columns). The order line carries the party's own name when it has one (ruling 3). A slip prints when only the order number changed (ruling 4). Held dishes already on a HOLD ticket are listed under HOLD on the same slip (ruling 5).

A650 · 6. "(was 28)" on the till MOCKUP

After Merge bills at Mesa 91, order 28's dishes are on order 27, but the kitchen's paper says 28. The Pass screen, the kitchen screens and the counter's Waiting and Held lists show the numbers that order's dishes were sent under as well, muted, after its own. It stays as long as the order is on that screen. Drawn in the till's light theme; the real screens use the same tokens in the dark theme.
