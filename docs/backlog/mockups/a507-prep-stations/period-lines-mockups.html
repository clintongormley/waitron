What to look at
  1. CAFÉS sends everything to Bar, except at Breakfast, when it goes to Coffee. Café con leche inherits both, so they show in grey.
  2. PIZZAS goes to the Pizza oven at Lunch and Dinner, and to Kitchen the rest of the day. (Three or more periods in a row are written first–last, such as “Breakfast–Dinner”.)
  3. POSTRES' Terrace line names Lunch, but no Lunch menu sells a dessert, so the line can never apply — the amber warning says so (that warning exists today).
  4. Rows with a line grow to two or three lines tall; rows without stay one line.

What to look at
  1. Every row stays one line tall, so more of the menu fits on screen.
  2. The marker is blue when the lines are set on this row, grey when inherited, and amber when one can never apply (POSTRES, shown open).
  3. You cannot see which station a period goes to without pointing at it — a scan for “what goes to the Pizza oven” needs hovering.
