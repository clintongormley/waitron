2 · Where the fold button sits (question 1)MOCKUP

(a) Recommended: the ☰ button at the top left of the banner, which phones already use to open the menu, is shown at every width. On a wide screen it folds and unfolds the sidebar; on a phone it opens the drawer as today. (b) A “Fold” button at the foot of the sidebar, which becomes a ⇥ icon at the foot of the strip.

3 · Clicking a section's icon in the strip (question 2)MOCKUP

You are on Products and click the Team icon. (a) Recommended: a small panel opens beside the icon listing Team's pages; picking one opens it and the panel closes; the strip stays folded. Pointing at an icon without clicking shows the section's name. (b) The whole sidebar unfolds with Team open, and stays unfolded until you fold it again (as today, opening Team closes Products and menus).

4 · Remembering the choice, and tablets (question 3)MOCKUP

(a) Recommended: the choice is remembered in this browser — fold it once and the dashboard opens folded next time, on this computer only. Until someone chooses, a window narrower than a laptop (a tablet, 1024 px) opens folded and a wider one opens unfolded. Phones (768 px or less) keep today's drawer. (b) Not remembered: every visit opens unfolded.

5 · An icon for each section (question 4)MOCKUP

Recommended icon for each section in large, with an alternative beside it. Every icon comes from Google's Material Symbols, the set some of the dashboard's existing icons are adapted from. Settings has a solid gear today; the recommendation swaps it for the outlined one so the strip matches, in the unfolded sidebar too.
