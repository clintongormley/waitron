# Sales

## Find and reprint a drawer close {#drawer-close-history}

On the dashboard, open **Sales** and choose the dates in **From** and **To**. Under **Drawer close
history**, choose a drawer and open a row to see who counted the cash, any difference, what went to
the bank and what stayed in the drawer. A pending count appears on the day it was started. A
finished close appears on the day it was finished.

To print another copy, choose an active receipt printer and select **Reprint close** on the
finished row. Use a different receipt printer if the drawer's own printer is unavailable. The card
figure shows payments taken before refunds.
