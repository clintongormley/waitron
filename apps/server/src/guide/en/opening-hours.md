# Opening hours

## Check a day's hours {#day-hours}

Open a date to see when each department and zone is open. The Month and the day pop-up combine periods that touch: **09:00–00:00** means continuous opening. Grey italic times follow the department's hours.

For the period detail, choose **Open this week** in the pop-up. In Week, click a day's heading or any of its bars to open that day's hours. A gap in a zone's bars means it is closed then.

To change the usual timetable, follow the season's **standard hours** link at the top of the pop-up. For an exception, choose **Make a special day** at the bottom.

## Resolve a clash with the business-day start {#business-day-start}

Use the department, day and times in the clash details to find the hours. If you are in the start preview, choose **Review opening hours**. Open the named season or special day and check any zone or period listed. Adjust the hours or choose a different start time.

If the business-day clock changes while you are editing the start time, close and reopen the editor. Check the latest start and preview before you save.

## Close a department for a special day {#department-closed}

In the special-day editor, choose the department and tick **Closed** under the date. This closes that department all day and leaves the others on their own hours. A full-day Closed stroke also checks this tick. Untick it before painting new hours.

To close every department, use the separate **Close the whole venue** tick under that date. Untick it to change an individual department’s closure.

If an all-day closure blocks a change to the venue’s business-day start, untick the listed closures and save. Change the start time, then paint the closures again.

## Paint a zone closure {#zone-closures}

Paint the zone’s whole **Closed** stretch, even past its department’s current closing time.

## Compare prep-station hours {#station-hours}

In Month or Week, choose **All prep stations** in **Show** to compare stations by sending department, or choose one station to see which departments send it orders. Choose **Show all** to see departments followed by stations. The station icon distinguishes them from zones with similar names. Click anywhere on a station’s row, including its closed stretches, to open the day box beside that point.

Station hours follow your painted periods, their menus, routing and the closures of sending zones. To change when a station gets orders, edit its periods and menus in **Periods**, its routes in **Prep stations**, or the closing times of the zones that send to it. The default station shows the periods that send it orders too. Open a date to read the departments and their zones, followed by the selected stations, or all stations when you have no station filter.

## Select days together {#select-days}

In Year or Month, select a run with Shift-click and add separate dates with Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows). On a phone, press and hold a date, then drag to pick a run. While the selection bar shows, tap dates to add or remove them, then choose **Make a special day**.

## Holiday names {#holiday-names}

When you plan around a public holiday, its name follows your dashboard language. Spain's supplied holidays have English display names in English and their official published names in Spanish. For example, 25 December appears as **Christmas Day** or **Natividad del Señor**. Changing the screen language keeps the holiday on the same date.

Your own special-day names stay as you entered them. If you create a new special day on a holiday, its suggested name uses your current screen language. You can edit that name before saving.

## Choose a holiday area {#holiday-area}

Some holidays depend on the island or part of a province where your venue stands. If the Year view asks for a holiday area, choose the one that applies to your address. The choices follow your dashboard language too; **Lleida, outside Aran** appears as **Lleida, fuera del territorio de Arán** in Spanish.

Changing the screen language keeps your chosen area. To change which holidays apply, change the area itself.
Review public holidays after changing the venue’s city. Choosing another holiday area replaces your previous choice.

## Edit seasons {#seasons}

Open **Seasons** under **Venue operations**. Choose a row to change its name, colour or start date, or **Add season** to create one. Each season runs until the next starts. Use the row’s **⋯** menu to edit or delete a season; check which hours will be lost before confirming a deletion.

In **Calendar** → **Staffing**, select a date to see how many people each area needs and the hours for each role. If you can manage the rota, the day box shows whether that week’s rota is **Draft** or **Published**. Select **Rota for this week** to open it.

To plan staffing for a season, open the season from the month you want to plan. Check any dated change to the business-day start above the grid. In a Staffing painter, select **Opening hours** to see the timetable for the same season or special day.

On a special day, a date that follows its season shows the season’s staffing in faint blocks. Drawing a block starts an override with those blocks already in place. Use the date’s **⋯** menu to **Follow the season** again or **Clear** staffing for that date.

Open the **Rota** tab to see the week of the last date you selected in **Staffing**. Until you select a date, the tab shows the venue’s current week.

## Edit periods and check their hours {#periods}

Open **Periods** under **Venue operations**, beside **Calendar**. Choose a row to edit it, or **Add period** to create one. Use the row’s **⋯** menu to edit or delete it.

In **Painted in**, choose a season or special day to check its timetable.
