# Opening hours

## Check a day's hours {#day-hours}

Open a date to see when each department and zone is open. The Month and the day pop-up combine periods that touch: **09:00–00:00** means continuous opening. Grey italic times follow the department's hours.

For the period detail, choose **Open this week** in the pop-up. In Week, click a day's heading or any of its bars to open that day's hours. A gap in a zone's bars means it is closed then.

To change the usual timetable, choose **Edit <season>'s standard week** beside the season at the top of the pop-up. For an exception, choose **Make a special day** at the bottom.

## Paint a zone closure {#zone-closures}

Paint the zone’s whole **Closed** stretch, even past its department’s current closing time.

## Select days together {#select-days}

In Year or Month, select a run with Shift-click and add separate dates with Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows). On a phone, press and hold a date, then drag to pick a run. While the selection bar shows, tap dates to add or remove them, then choose **Make a special day**.

## Holiday names {#holiday-names}

When you plan around a public holiday, its name follows your dashboard language. Spain's supplied holidays have English display names in English and their official published names in Spanish. For example, 25 December appears as **Christmas Day** or **Natividad del Señor**. Changing the screen language keeps the holiday on the same date.

Your own special-day names stay as you entered them. If you create a new special day on a holiday, its suggested name uses your current screen language. You can edit that name before saving.

## Choose a holiday area {#holiday-area}

Some holidays depend on the island or part of a province where your venue stands. If the Year view asks for a holiday area, choose the one that applies to your address. The choices follow your dashboard language too; **Lleida, outside Aran** appears as **Lleida, fuera del territorio de Arán** in Spanish.

Changing the screen language keeps your chosen area. To change which holidays apply, change the area itself.
Review public holidays after changing the venue’s city. Choosing another holiday area replaces your previous choice.

## Edit seasons {#seasons}

Open **Seasons** under **Venue operations** to change their names, colours and start dates. Each season runs until the next starts. **Save** keeps you on the page; **Cancel** discards your unsaved changes.

## Check where a period is used {#periods}

Open **Periods** under **Venue operations**, beside **Calendar**. In **Painted in**, choose a season or special day to check its timetable.
