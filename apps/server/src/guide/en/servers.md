# Servers

## When a box has handed over {#handed-over}

A venue runs on one box at a time. When you move the venue from one box to another, the box it
leaves steps down: it restarts, and from then on it takes no sales and sends nothing to the tax
agency. That way the venue's sales and invoice records never end up split between two boxes.

If you open the old box's address in a browser, it shows a page titled **This box has handed
over**. The page names the box the venue now runs on, and lists each step of the handover that the
old box has recorded, with the date and time it recorded it, in the venue's time zone.

The page has no buttons, because nothing on it needs you: the old box is meant to be in this state.
It sends its invoice records to the new box by itself, then becomes the spare.

The page is in Spanish when your browser prefers Spanish, and in English otherwise.
