# About Waitron

## Show the «declaración responsable» {#declaration}

The «declaración responsable» is Waitron's signed statement that it meets Spain's rules for
invoicing software. A tax inspector or your gestor may ask to see it.

To open it:

- On the dashboard, choose **About Waitron** from the account menu, or the line at the foot of the
  sign-in page.
- On a till, choose **About Waitron** from your name's menu, or from **More** (☰) on a handheld.
- On a kiosk, a kitchen screen, the PIN screen, the pairing screen and the setup wizard, choose
  **ⓘ**.
- Before the box is set up, or while it is stopped, open `/legal/declaration` on the box's
  address.

When an inspector or your gestor asks for a copy, choose **Descargar PDF** under the declaration
and send them the file.
