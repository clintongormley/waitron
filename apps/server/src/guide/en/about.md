# About Waitron

## Show the «declaración responsable» {#declaration}

The «declaración responsable» is Waitron's statement that it meets Spain's rules for
invoicing software. A tax inspector or your tax adviser may ask to see it.

To open it:

- On the dashboard, choose **About Waitron** from the account menu, or the line at the foot of the
  sign-in page.
- On a till or a handheld, choose **About Waitron** from **More** (☰).
- On a kitchen screen, on a till's or handheld's sign-in screen, on the **Set up this device** and
  **Waiting for approval** screens, and in the setup wizard, choose **ⓘ**.
- Before the box is set up, or while it is stopped, open `/legal/declaration` on the box's
  address.

When an inspector or your tax adviser asks for a copy, choose **Descargar PDF** under the declaration
and send them the file.

If they ask about invoices from an earlier version of Waitron, open that version under
**Versiones anteriores**.
