# Purchases

Enter a supplier's invoice in **Purchases** so its VAT breakdown is available for reporting.

## Enter an invoice {#enter-invoice}

Choose **Add invoice** and copy the supplier's tax ID, name, invoice number and issue date from
their invoice. Enter the date you received it, the total and each line of its VAT breakdown.
Choose **Save** to record it. To correct an existing entry, open its edit action.

## Invoice numbers {#invoice-numbers}

A supplier may restart their numbers each January. You can record invoice 001 issued in 2026 and
invoice 001 issued by the same supplier in 2027. When you enter the same supplier and number with
a different issue year, the form shows the other recorded year before you save. Check the issue
date against the supplier's invoice; the warning lets you continue.

You cannot record the same supplier's number twice within one issue year, even if you received
the invoices in different years. Changing an existing invoice's issue date, supplier or number
must also leave that combination unique within its issue year.

## Deduction period {#deduction-period}

The **Received on** date determines which reporting period includes the invoice's input VAT.
The **Issued on** year controls the duplicate-number check. For example, an invoice issued on
31 December 2026 and received on 5 January 2027 belongs to the January receipt period and keeps
2026 as its issue year.
